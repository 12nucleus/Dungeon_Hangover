# DUNGEON HANGOVER: 50 FLOORS OF REGRET
## Complete Game Design Bible

---

# PART I: THE 50-FLOOR STORYLINE

## THE PREMISE

You are **Greg**. Last night you drank an irresponsible quantity of ale at **The Murky Flagon**, insulted a goblin, arm-wrestled a horse, and attempted to propose to a chandelier. Now you are at the bottom of a 50-floor dungeon called **The Spire of Regret**, in your underwear, with a headache that could fell an ogre.

The Spire is a living dungeon — it grows around regret. Every floor crystallizes a bad decision, a broken promise, or a thing left undone. The deeper you were, the worse the mistake. You are at Floor 50 — the foundation — which means your worst regret built this place.

The only way out is **up**.

---

## THE NARRATOR

A disembodied voice, bone-dry and deeply unimpressed, comments on your every action. The narrator is not the dungeon. The narrator is the part of your brain that knows you screwed up. It existed before the Spire. It will exist after. It is the voice that said "this is fine" while the room was on fire.

**Sample narrator lines:**
- *"You picked up the rusty dagger. It breaks if you look at it too hard. Much like your dignity."*
- *"The rat stares at you. You stare at the rat. This is the most meaningful relationship you've had in weeks."*
- *"You died. Place a finger on the screen to continue. Figuratively. You have no fingers to spare."*

---

## THE ARCHAIC (THE DUNGEON'S HISTORY)

The Spire was not built. It **grew**.

Long ago, the world above was ruled by the **Paragon**, a being of pure order who made one mistake: it created a soul and told it to "be better." The soul tried. Failed. Tried harder. Failed harder. That soul became the first regret, and regret became a seed, and the seed became a root system that sank into the earth and twisted into stone.

The Spire is the physical manifestation of every Paragon's failure. It hungers for new regrets because that is what it is made of. Every person who enters adds their own floor. Greg's addition is Floor 50 — not because his regrets are the worst, but because they are the *most recent*, and the Spire always builds from the bottom up.

At the top of the Spire — Floor 1 — is the Paragon's Throne, where the Paragon sits, still trying to be better, still failing, holding the dungeon together with sheer stubborn will.

---

## THE FIVE ACTS

### ACT I: THE TANGLED ROOTS (Floors 50–41)
**Theme:** Sewers, mold, vermin, forgotten cellars. You are literally at the bottom.

**Narrative function:** Introduction. Survival. Meeting the Hermit. Learning the basics. The world's rules. The tone is slapstick — you are fighting rats and mold with a rusty dagger. But underneath, you start finding notes, bones, and echoes of people who came before.

**Escalation:** By Floor 41, the vermin are organized. There is a hierarchy. Something is organizing them.

---

### ACT II: THE HOLLOW MARKETS (Floors 40–31)
**Theme:** Goblin bazaars, fungal gardens, underground rivers, the first signs of society.

**Narrative function:** Civilization. The goblins have built a parody of the surface world — shops, a legal system, a feudal lord. They are absurd but functional. You can trade. You can negotiate. You can be betrayed. The tone shifts from slapstick to darkly comic social satire.

**Escalation:** The goblin king, **Gribnab the Soapy**, rules the upper goblin floors from his bath throne. He is petty, vain, and terrifying. The goblins fear him. You need to get past him.

---

### ACT III: THE SCORCHED HALLS (Floors 30–21)
**Theme:** Ancient dwarven ruins. Lava tubes. The bones of a dead civilization.

**Narrative function:** History. The dwarves built downward to mine something. They found it. It destroyed them. Their ghosts remain, still mining, still arguing about guild law. The tone becomes melancholy. The comedy is darker — gallows humor from people who have been dead for centuries and still won't shut up about regulations.

**Escalation:** You find the dwarven records. They found what the Spire was built around — the **Lodestone of Regret**, a crystallized first failure at the dungeon's core. If you destroy it, the Spire collapses. But you realize: the Paragon is guarding it. And the Paragon is not evil — it is *trying*.

---

### ACT IV: THE ASCENT (Floors 20–11)
**Theme:** Verticality becomes real. You see sky through cracks. The air changes. Forests of stone. Bridges over nothing.

**Narrative function:** Confrontation with self. The Spire recognizes you. It shows you yourself — your failures, your choices. Mirror enemies (copies of your build). Rooms that recreate the tavern where it all started. The narrator gets quieter because *you* are becoming the voice.

**Escalation:** Floor 13 is the **Floor of Faces** — every NPC you've met or failed to save stands in a long hallway and watches you pass. They do not speak. They just watch.

---

### ACT V: THE PARAGON'S CROWN (Floors 10–1)
**Theme:** Marble. Gold. Light. The dungeon becomes beautiful. It is the most dangerous part.

**Narrative function:** Truth. The Paragon is not a boss. It is a child who has been trying to be good for ten thousand years and failing every single day, and the dungeon is the weight of that failure compressed into stone. It does not want to fight you. It wants to *ask you a question*.

**The Final Floor (Floor 1):** The Throne Room. The Paragon is small. It is crying. It asks: *"Can you be better?"*

---

## THE ENDINGS

### Ending A: The Ascension (Default)
You tell the Paragon: "I don't know. But I can try."
The Paragon smiles. It shatters. The Spire shatters. You wake up in The Murky Flagon, hungover, alive, and the chandelier is still there. You buy it a drink. Credits roll.

### Ending B: The Tyrant (Secret)
If you killed every companion and NPC, you sit on the Throne. You become the new Paragon. The dungeon rebuilds around you. The narrator becomes *you*. You hear a new drunk stumble into Floor 50. You sigh. *"Oh, for f—"*

### Ending C: The Hermit (Secret)
If you completed every companion arc, every side quest, and returned every stolen item to its owner, the Hermit meets you at Floor 1 before the Paragon. He says: *"You did it. Every one. I didn't think a drunk could have this much heart."* He reveals he is the **Spire's First Regret** — the Paragon's first failure, given form. He has been waiting for someone to care enough to fix what was broken. He dissolves into light. The Paragon, freed from its first failure, lets go. You wake up. The Hermit's ring is in your pocket. It is warm.

### Ending D: The Goblin (Hidden)
If you befriend the Goblin King, he invites you to stay. You abdicate. The goblins win. You live in a bathtub forever. It's actually pretty comfortable.

# PART II: THE 15 CLASSES (SKILL POOLS)

There are no classes at character creation. At each level-up, you choose a skill from any of the 15 pools. Your "class" is what players call your build after the fact.

| # | Class Name | Identity | Core Mechanic | Tone |
|---|-----------|----------|---------------|------|
| 1 | **Bar Bouncer** | Big, mean, door policy | Grappling, shove, single-target lockdown | "You're not on the list." |
| 2 | **Gutter Rogue** | Dirty fighter, opportunist | Backstab, bleed, steals, trap use | "Nothing personal. Actually, it is." |
| 3 | **Karaoke Bard** | Buff/debuff through song | Ally buff, enemy debuff, charm via performance | "It's not about the voice, it's about the FEELING." |
| 4 | **Sommelier** | Potion explainer, know-it-all | Identify buffs, potion crafting, "pairing" debuffs | "Notes of oak and poor life choices." |
| 5 | **Barista** | Speed-obsessed, jittery | Extra actions, critical hits, speed buffs/no caffeine crash | "I haven't slept in six floors." |
| 6 | **Accountant** | Status effect auditor | Debuff stacking, "audit" damage based on enemy level | "You've been... fiddling the books." |

# PART III: ABILITY/SPELL TREES

Each class has 5 skills. Unlock order is fixed per class, but you can pick from any tree at any level-up (level requirements are *class-level*, meaning the number of skills you've taken from that specific tree).

# PART IV: SIDE QUESTS BY FLOOR

Each floor has one main quest (progression) and 1–3 side quests (optional, reward loot/experience/lore).

## ACT I: THE TANGLED ROOTS (50–41)

**Floor 50 — Sewer Cellar**
- **Main:** Retrieve the Hermit's finger from the Boss Rat. Get starting equipment.
- **Side 1 — "The Other Hermit":** A second Hermit in a hidden room claims the first is a liar. You must choose who to believe. (Reward: the *truthful* Hermit gives a unique ring.)
- **Side 2 — "Cursed Gold":** A pile of gold in a collapsed treasury. Taking it inflicts **Cursed** (–20% loot quality) until you donate it to a ghost in Floor 47.
- **Side 3 — "The Soap Conundrum":** Find the Goblin King's soap to pass the locked door. (Required for progression if you knock. Alternative: break the door down with 2+ Strength or a Plumber skill.)

**Floor 49 — Fungal Grotto**
- **Main:** Navigate the mushroom field. Reach the staircase.
- **Side 1 — "Mycologist's Request":** Collect 5 glowing mushrooms for a talking mushroom. Reward: the mushroom joins you as a companion (heals 2 HP/turn, dies if you use fire).
- **Side 2 — "Spore Madness":** A room full of hallucinogenic spores. You must navigate it while **Hallucinating** (randomized movement). Reward: permanent +5% resistance to status effects.
- **Side 3 — "The Mushroom's Warning":** A dying mushroom tells you about Floor 47's ghost. (Unlocks context for Floor 47's side quest.)

**Floor 48 — Collapsed Library**
- **Main:** Find the exit through the rubble.
- **Side 1 — "The Paragon's First Record":** Find a specific book. It tells you the Paragon was a child. (Lore. Unlocks Ending C clue.)
- **Side 2 — "Bookworm":** A giant bookworm blocks a treasure room. You can fight it or feed it 3 books. Reward: a skill tome (random skill from any tree).
- **Side 3 — "Overdue Fines":** A ghost librarian demands you return 3 scattered books. Reward: **Library Card** (passive: +10% XP gain).

**Floor 47 — Bone Yard**
- **Main:** Cross the bone field without waking the Bone Rat King.
- **Side 1 — "The Ghost's Donation":** If you took the Cursed Gold on Floor 50, a ghost here asks for it. Donating removes Curse and rewards **Blessed Penny** (+5% gold find).
- **Side 2 — "Bone Rat King":** Optional mini-boss. Defeat him for **Bone Crown** (+10% damage to beasts).
- **Side 3 — "The Failed Adventurer":** A ghost adventurer tells you about Floor 40's goblin market. (Lore.)

**Floor 46 — Drowned Chapel**
- **Main:** The chapel is flooding. Escape before you drown.
- **Side 1 — "The Drowning Goblin":** A goblin is drowning in the chapel. Save him: he becomes a recurring NPC who gives you discounts at the goblin market. Loot his body: you get his gold but the goblins on Floor 40 are hostile.
- **Side 2 — "The Sunken Altar":** Dive underwater to find a hidden altar. Reward: **Holy Water** (consumable, removes all debuffs).
- **Side 3 — "The Hymn":** Sing the hymn inscribed on the wall (Karaoke Bard skill or find the sheet music). Reward: **Blessed** (+10% all stats) for 3 fights.

**Floor 45 — The Rat King's Court**
- **Main:** Navigate rat politics. The Rat King wants tribute.
- **Side 1 — "Ratatouille":** Bring the Rat King a specific cheese from Floor 44. Reward: safe passage + **Rat Whisker** (passive: +5% evasion).
- **Side 2 — "The Usurper":** A rat noble wants to overthrow the King. Help him: new Rat King, different rewards. Refuse: the King rewards you.
- **Side 3 — "Court Jester":** A rat jester tells jokes. If you laugh (choose the right dialogue), he gives you a **Jester's Bell** (consumable, confuses all enemies for 1 turn).

**Floor 44 — Wax Museum**
- **Main:** The wax figures move when you're not looking. Reach the exit.
- **Side 1 — "Your Statue":** Find the wax statue of Greg. It has your exact gear. Destroy it: +10% XP. Leave it: it appears in Floor 13.
- **Side 2 — "The Sculptor":** A ghost sculptor wants you to pose. Stand still for 3 turns while enemies attack. Reward: **Wax Figurine** (consumable, creates a decoy).
- **Side 3 — "Melting Point":** Use fire to melt a blocked door. Behind it: a treasure room. (Requires fire source.)

**Floor 43 — The Tunnel Mouth**
- **Main:** The floor is a living throat. You must be swallowed and fight your way out.
- **Side 1 — "Indigestion":** Drop a toxic item in the stomach acid. The tunnel spits you out early, skipping the boss. Reward: **Stomach Acid Vial** (consumable, corrosive damage).
- **Side 2 — "The Tonsil":** A hidden room in the "tonsil." Reward: **Tonsil Stone** (passive: +5% poison resistance).
- **Side 3 — "Swallow Your Pride":** Let the tunnel digest you partially. You take damage but gain **Digestive Fortitude** (+10% poison resistance permanent).

**Floor 42 — The Weeping Hall**
- **Main:** The walls cry. The floor is slippery. Navigate to the exit.
- **Side 1 — "Dry The Walls":** Find the source of the tears (a ghost child). Comfort her: she stops crying and rewards **Child's Tear** (consumable, full heal).
- **Side 2 — "Slip and Slide":** Navigate a room entirely made of tears without falling. Reward: **Tear Salve** (passive: +10% ice resistance).
- **Side 3 — "The Narrator Cries Too":** The narrator admits it's sad. If you acknowledge it (dialogue choice), the narrator is slightly nicer for the rest of the game. (No mechanical reward. But it's something.)

**Floor 41 — Gates of the Hollow Market**
- **Main:** Enter the goblin market. First shop available.
- **Side 1 — "The Toll":** A goblin demands payment. Pay: safe passage. Fight: you can but the shop prices double.
- **Side 2 — "The Mapmaker":** A goblin sells a map of Floor 40's secret rooms. Buy it: reveals hidden rooms. Steal it: goblins are hostile.
- **Side 3 — "First Impression":** A goblin child asks if you're a hero. Say yes: you get a flower. Say no: you get a rock. Both are useless but the narrator comments.

## ACT II: THE HOLLOW MARKETS (40–31)

**Floor 40 — Goblin Bazaar**
- **Main:** Navigate the market. Find the exit.
- **Side 1 — "The Drowning Goblin's Debt":** If you saved the goblin on Floor 46, he gives you a discount. If you looted him, you're banned from shops.
- **Side 2 — "Counterfeit Goods":** A goblin sells "legendary" items that are actually junk. Buy one: it's useless. Expose him: he flees and leaves real loot.
- **Side 3 — "The Auction":** A rare item is being auctioned. You can bid (gold), steal (Gutter Rogue skill), or fight. Reward: **Auctioneer's Gavel** (passive: +5% gold from sales).

**Floor 39 — Soap Trader's Row**
- **Main:** The goblin economy runs on soap. Find a way to trade.
- **Side 1 — "The Soapmaker's Secret":** Learn the recipe for goblin soap. You can craft it from fat + ash. Used as currency.
- **Side 2 — "Soap Heist":** Steal a bar of premium soap from a guarded shop. Reward: **Premium Soap** (trade for best items).
- **Side 3 — "The Smuggler":** A goblin smuggler will trade soap for information about the surface. (Lore.)

**Floor 38 — The Dice Parlour**
- **Main:** Goblins challenge you to dice. You can play or fight.
- **Side 1 — "All In":** Bet your levels. Win: +2 levels. Lose: –1 level. (You can only lose 1 level per run.)
- **Side 2 — "Loaded Dice":** Find the goblin's trick dice. Use them: always win. Expose them: fight.
- **Side 3 — "The Cheater's Hand":** A goblin accuses you of cheating. Prove your innocence (skill check) or fight.

**Floor 37 — Goblin Court**
- **Main:** The goblin nobles mock you. Survive the social encounter.
- **Side 1 — "Flatter the Baron":** Compliment the goblin baron. He gives you a title and a useless medal.
- **Side 2 — "Insult the Baron":** Insult him. Fight his guards. Reward: **Baron's Signet Ring** (+10% gold find).
- **Side 3 — "The Secret Passage":** Find a hidden tunnel behind the throne. Leads to a treasure room.

**Floor 36 — The Bathhouse Antechamber**
- **Main:** Gribnab's guards demand soap. Get through.
- **Side 1 — "The Soap You Found":** If you have soap from Floor 50 or 39, you can pass. If not, you must find another way.
- **Side 2 — "The Guard's Secret":** One guard is sympathetic. Bribe him with food. He tells you about the King's weakness.
- **Side 3 — "The Other Entrance":** Find a sewer grate that leads to the bathhouse. (Plumber skill or wrench required.)

**Floor 35 — Fungal Deep Gardens**
- **Main:** The mushroom companion evolves or dies here.
- **Side 1 — "Evolution":** If you have the mushroom companion, it evolves into a combat form (attacks for you) or a healing form (heals more). You choose.
- **Side 2 — "The Mushroom King":** A giant mushroom boss. Defeat it: **Mushroom Crown** (+10% nature damage).
- **Side 3 — "Spore Sacrifice":** Let the mushroom companion die to save yourself from a spore cloud. It dies. You live. The narrator is quiet for a moment.

**Floor 34 — The Toll Bridge**
- **Main:** A troll blocks the bridge. He doesn't want gold.
- **Side 1 — "The Compliment":** Compliment the troll. He's never been complimented. He cries and lets you pass. Reward: **Tear-Stained Pebble** (passive: +5% ice damage).
- **Side 2 — "The Insult":** Insult the troll. Fight him. Reward: **Troll's Tooth** (passive: +5% physical damage).
- **Side 3 — "The Riddle":** Answer the troll's riddle. Correct: he lets you give a compliment AND pass. Wrong: fight.

**Floor 33 — The Forgotten Kitchen**
- **Main:** A ghost chef runs a kitchen. He feeds you.
- **Side 1 — "The Soup":** Eat the soup. Full heal + **Well Fed** (+5% all stats for 3 fights).
- **Side 2 — "The Recipe":** Learn the recipe. You can now craft the soup yourself (Haunted Chef skill required).
- **Side 3 — "The Chef's Regret":** The chef tells you his story. He died because he wouldn't leave his kitchen during a fire. (Lore. Unlocks Ending C clue.)

**Floor 32 — The Whispering Gallery**
- **Main:** Echoes of your choices play back. Twisted.
- **Side 1 — "Listen to the Echoes":** Hear what you said to NPCs. They're distorted. You can apologize (dialogue). No mechanical reward but it matters for Ending C.
- **Side 2 — "Scream Back":** Scream at the echoes. They scream back. You take damage but gain **Deafened** (+10% resistance to charm/fear).
- **Side 3 — "The Whispering Voice":** A specific whisper tells you about Floor 27's Lodestone shard. (Lore.)

**Floor 31 — The Goblin King's Outer Sanctum**
- **Main:** First real goblin army encounter. Fight through.
- **Side 1 — "The Deserter":** A goblin deserter wants to join you. He knows the King's patrol patterns. Reward: **Goblin Map** (reveals enemy positions on Floor 30).
- **Side 2 — "The War Drum":** Destroy the goblin war drum. Goblins lose their buff. Easier fight.
- **Side 3 — "The Banner":** Steal the goblin banner. Goblins are **Demoralized** (–15% damage).

## ACT III: THE SCORCHED HALLS (30–21)

**Floor 30 — Dwarven Gate**
- **Main:** A ghost doorkeeper demands ID. You don't have any.
- **Side 1 — "Forge an ID":** Use a Haunted Chef or Plumber skill to create a fake ID. It works. Barely.
- **Side 2 — "The Registry":** Find your name in the dwarven registry. It's there. You were here before. (Lore. Major reveal.)
- **Side 3 — "The Doorkeeper's Fee":** Pay the doorkeeper in ale. He lets you pass and gives you **Dwarven Ale** (consumable, +20% damage for 1 turn, –20% accuracy).

**Floor 29 — The Molten Tunnels**
- **Main:** Lava. Fire. Heat. Navigate without burning.
- **Side 1 — "The Coolant":** Find a way to cool the lava. (Water skill or item.) Creates a path.
- **Side 2 — "The Fire Elemental":** A fire elemental blocks the way. Fight or negotiate (offer it something flammable).
- **Side 3 — "The Forge":** A dwarven forge still works. You can upgrade one weapon here. (+10% damage, permanent for the run.)

**Floor 28 — The Mining Office**
- **Main:** Ghost dwarves argue about guild law. You must resolve it.
- **Side 1 — "The Union Dispute":** Side with the miners: they give you a pickaxe. Side with the foreman: you get gold.
- **Side 2 — "The Foremen's Ledger":** Find the ledger. It shows what they were mining: the Lodestone shard. (Lore.)
- **Side 3 — "The Bureaucrat":** A ghost bureaucrat demands you fill out forms. Do it: you get a **Permit** (passive: +5% shop discounts). Refuse: fight.

**Floor 27 — The Grand Excavation**
- **Main:** You find the Lodestone shard. It's guarded.
- **Side 1 — "The Lodestone":** Take the shard. It pulses. You hear the Paragon's voice for the first time. (Lore. Critical.)
- **Side 2 — "The Miner's Ghost":** A miner warns you not to take it. If you don't, he gives you **Miner's Helmet** (+10% fire resistance).
- **Side 3 — "The Shard's Memory":** Touch the shard without taking it. You see the Paragon's first failure. (Lore.)

**Floor 26 — The Broken Lift**
- **Main:** A vertical puzzle. Climbing inside climbing.
- **Side 1 — "The Mechanic":** Fix the lift. It takes time but skips the puzzle.
- **Side 2 — "The Climb":** Climb manually. Harder but you find a hidden room with loot.
- **Side 3 — "The Drop":** Fall down a shaft. You take damage but land in a secret area.

**Floor 25 — The First Echo**
- **Main:** Fight a mirror version of yourself.
- **Side 1 — "Surrender":** Let the mirror win. It merges with you. You gain +10% all stats but lose 10% max HP.
- **Side 2 — "Destroy It":** Kill the mirror. Normal rewards.
- **Side 3 — "Talk to It":** The mirror speaks. It knows things. (Lore. It tells you about Floor 13.)

**Floor 24 — The Bathhouse Catacombs**
- **Main:** Gribnab's bath extends into death. Undead in bathrobes.
- **Side 1 — "The Bathhouse Ghost":** A ghost misses his bath. Find his soap. He rewards you.
- **Side 2 — "The Hot Spring":** A natural hot spring. Rest: full heal. But you're **Relaxed** (–10% damage for 2 fights).
- **Side 3 — "The Drain":** Find the drain. It leads to a secret room. (Plumber skill.)

**Floor 23 — The Dwarven Bar**
- **Main:** Ghosts still serving ghost ale. Best lore floor.
- **Side 1 — "The Ghost Ale":** Drink it. You see the dwarves' last day. (Lore.)
- **Side 2 — "The Bartender":** The bartender tells you about the Paragon. He knew it. (Lore.)
- **Side 3 — "The Tab":** The dwarves have an open tab. Pay it: they give you **Dwarven Stein** (passive: +5% damage when below 50% HP).

**Floor 22 — The Collapsing Gallery**
- **Main:** The Spire is starting to notice you. The floor collapses as you move.
- **Side 1 — "The Sprint":** Run. Don't stop. Reach the exit.
- **Side 2 — "The Careful Path":** Move slowly. Find hidden rooms.
- **Side 3 — "The Fall":** Fall through the floor. You land on Floor 21 early. (Skip Floor 22's boss.)

**Floor 21 — The Architect's Tomb**
- **Main:** The dwarf who designed the deepest floors. His journals.
- **Side 1 — "The Journals":** Read them. You learn the Spire was designed to contain the Paragon. (Lore. Critical.)
- **Side 2 — "The Architect's Ghost":** He's still here. He wants to know if his design worked. Tell him yes: he gives you **Architect's Compass** (passive: reveals hidden doors). Tell him no: he attacks.
- **Side 3 — "The Blueprint":** Find the blueprint. It shows a secret path from Floor 20 to Floor 15. (Unlocks shortcut.)

## ACT IV: THE ASCENT (20–11)

**Floor 20 — The Wind Halls**
- **Main:** You hear the surface. First Act IV floor.
- **Side 1 — "The Wind's Message":** The wind carries voices. You hear someone calling your name. (Lore.)

- **Side 3 — "The Echo of the Tavern":** You hear The Murky Flagon. The narrator is quiet.

**Floor 19 — The Glass Bridge**
- **Main:** A bridge over nothing. It shows your reflection.
- **Side 1 — "The Reflection":** Your reflection moves independently. It knows your build. (Lore.)
- **Side 2 — "Break the Glass":** Shatter the bridge. You fall but land on a hidden floor. (Secret area.)
- **Side 3 — "Walk Carefully":** Cross without breaking anything. Reward: **Glass Shard** (passive: +5% critical hit damage).

**Floor 18 — The Court of Whispers**
- **Main:** Fae-like beings who trade in secrets, not gold.
- **Side 1 — "Trade a Secret":** Give them a secret (your real name, a regret). They give you a boon.
- **Side 2 — "Refuse":** They're offended. Fight.
- **Side 3 — "The Whispering Deal":** They offer to tell you the Paragon's name. Price: a memory. (Lore.)

**Floor 17 — The Garden of Stone**
- **Main:** Petrified forest. Beautiful. Dead.
- **Side 1 — "The Petrified Tree":** A tree that was once alive. Touch it: you see its last moment. (Lore.)
- **Side 2 — "The Stone Fruit":** Eat a stone fruit. It's... stone. You take damage but gain **Petrified Stomach** (+10% poison resistance).
- **Side 3 — "The Gardener":** A ghost gardener tends the stone plants. He's been doing it for centuries. (Lore.)

**Floor 16 — The War Camp**
- **Main:** An army that died waiting for a war that never came.
- **Side 1 — "The General":** The general wants to know if the war happened. Tell him yes: he's at peace. Tell him no: he attacks.
- **Side 2 — "The Soldier's Letter":** Find a letter from a soldier's family. Deliver it to Floor 11's ghost. (Cross-floor quest.)
- **Side 3 — "The War Drum":** Beat the drum. The army marches. They clear your path but you lose **War Drum** (consumable, buffs all allies).

**Floor 15 — The Mirror Floor**
- **Main:** Every room is your reflection. Your build. Your items.
- **Side 1 — "The Perfect Mirror":** A room where your reflection is better than you. Fight it.
- **Side 2 — "The Broken Mirror":** A room where your reflection is worse. It begs for help. (Dialogue choice.)
- **Side 3 — "The Mirror's Gift":** A mirror shows you a skill you don't have. You can learn it. (One free skill from any tree.)

**Floor 14 — The Floor of Doors**
- **Main:** 100 doors. One leads on. 99 lead to Floor 50.
- **Side 1 — "The Correct Door":** Find clues to identify the right door. (Logic puzzle.)
- **Side 2 — "The Wrong Door":** Go through a wrong door on purpose. You're back at Floor 50. (Reset. But you keep your levels.)
- **Side 3 — "The Door Maker":** A ghost made the doors. He tells you which is correct. For a price.

**Floor 13 — The Floor of Faces**
- **Main:** Every NPC you've met or failed to save stands in a long hallway. They watch.
- **Side 1 — "The Faces Speak":** If you saved them, they nod. If you didn't, they look away.
- **Side 2 — "The New Face":** A face you don't recognize. It's you. From a previous run. (Meta.)
- **Side 3 — "The Empty Space":** A space where a face should be. Someone you didn't meet. (Lore.)

**Floor 12 — The Chasm**
- **Main:** A floor that is just falling. Forever. You have to climb sideways.
- **Side 1 — "The Climb":** Climb the walls. Hard. But you find a ledge with loot.
- **Side 2 — "The Fall":** Let yourself fall. You land on Floor 11. (Skip.)
- **Side 3 — "The Wind":** Use the wind to glide. (Requires specific item or skill.)

**Floor 11 — The Gate of Questions**
- **Main:** A real sphinx. Riddles with actual answers you must deduce.
- **Side 1 — "The Riddle":** Answer correctly: pass. Answer incorrectly: fight.
- **Side 2 — "The Sphinx's Bargain":** The sphinx will let you pass if you give up a skill. (Permanent loss.)
- **Side 3 — "The Soldier's Letter":** If you found the letter on Floor 16, deliver it here. The sphinx is the soldier's mother. (Emotional. Lore.)

- **Side 3 — "The Narrator's Warning":** The narrator says: "This is the last time I can help you."

**Floor 9 — The Hall of Lessons**
- **Main:** You learn the Paragon's history. It was a child.
- **Side 1 — "The Lesson":** A ghost teacher tells you about the Paragon. (Lore.)
- **Side 2 — "The Child's Drawing":** A drawing of the Paragon as a child. It's... sad.
- **Side 3 — "The Teacher's Regret":** The teacher failed the Paragon. (Lore.)

**Floor 8 — The Throne Anteroom**
- **Main:** The Paragon's voice for the first time. It sounds scared.
- **Side 1 — "The Voice":** The Paragon asks: "Are you here to hurt me?" (Dialogue choice.)
- **Side 2 — "The Guard":** A spectral guard blocks the way. He's protecting the Paragon.
- **Side 3 — "The Door":** The door to Floor 7 is locked. The key is your choices.

**Floor 7 — The Floor of Regrets**
- **Main:** Your specific regrets, manifested.
- **Side 1 — "The Tavern":** Recreate the night you got drunk. You can choose differently. (Doesn't change anything. But it's something.)
- **Side 2 — "The Chandelier":** The chandelier you proposed to. It's here. It says yes. (Absurd.)
- **Side 3 — "The Horse":** The horse you arm-wrestled. It's here. It wants a rematch.

**Floor 6 — The Garden of Second Chances**
- **Main:** Everything is fixable here. You don't need to fix it.
- **Side 1 — "The Fix":** Fix something. It stays fixed. (No reward. Just... fixed.)
- **Side 2 — "The Letting Go":** Leave something broken. It's okay.
- **Side 3 — "The Garden's Secret":** The garden is the Paragon's first happy memory. (Lore.)

**Floor 5 — The Armory of Hope**
- **Main:** The best loot in the game. Earned, not given.
- **Side 1 — "The Weapon":** Choose a weapon. It's perfect for your build.
- **Side 2 — "The Armor":** Choose armor. It's perfect for your build.
- **Side 3 — "The Shield":** A shield that blocks one fatal hit per fight.

**Floor 4 — The Bridge Between**
- **Main:** A long bridge. No enemies. Music. You walk.
- **Side 1 — "The Walk":** Just walk. The narrator is quiet. The music plays.
- **Side 2 — "The Memory":** You remember why you're here.
- **Side 3 — "The Choice":** You can turn back. You don't.

**Floor 3 — The Penultimate Room**

## THE DROWNED MAJESTY (Gribnab's Club)

**Type:** Blunt Weapon (Two-Handed)
**Damage:** 8
**Requirement:** Level 3
**Tier:** 3 (Boss Drop)

**Effects:**
- **Sovereign Sudds:** 15% chance on hit to inflict **Slippery** on target (50% fall chance when moving, 2 turns)
- **Soap Crust:** The club is crusted with ancient soap. It never gets dirty. You do.
- **Slippery Grip:** 5% chance on attack to drop the weapon. It slides out of your hands. The narrator comments every time.
- **Bath Water Residue:** The club is always slightly wet. +5% damage to fire-type enemies (water beats fire). –5% damage to ice-type enemies (water freezes).

**Flavor Text:**
*"This club was the symbol of Gribnab's reign. It is made from the wood of a bathhouse that burned down three hundred years ago. It is crusted with the soap of a thousand baths. It smells like strawberries and tyranny. It is the most powerful weapon you have ever held. It is also very slippery."*

**Narrator on equip:**
*"You picked up the Goblin King's club. It's wet. It's soapy. It's yours now. You are a naked man with a soapy club. This is your kingdom."*

**Narrator on dropping it:**
*"You dropped the club. Again. This is the third time. The club is judging you. The club has seen better wielders. The club is not impressed."*
