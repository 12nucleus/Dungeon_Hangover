# PART A: ALL 50 BOSS DIALOGUES

Each boss has: **Intro** (when you enter), **Mid-Fight** (at 50% HP), **Death** (when killed), and **Special** (unique trigger).

---

## FLOOR 50 — BOSS RAT (The Finger Gnawer)
**Type:** Giant Rat. Not sentient. But the narrator translates.

**Intro:**
> *"The rat sits on its bone pedestal like a king on a throne. It is the size of a dog. Its eyes are black and wet. It is chewing on something. It is chewing on a finger. It stops chewing and looks at you. It does not look away."*

**Mid-Fight:**
> *"The rat is bleeding. It is angry. It is a rat. It was always angry. But now it is angry AND bleeding, which is a combination that really gets a rat going."*

**Death:**
> *"The rat falls. The finger rolls across the floor. You pick it up. It still has the ring on it. The ring is warm. You do not know why the ring is warm. You do not ask."*

**Special (if you offer it food instead of fighting):**
> *"You hold out a piece of cheese. The rat sniffs. The rat takes the cheese. The rat leaves the finger. The rat leaves the room. The rat does not look back. You have made a friend. You have made a rat friend. This is the least weird thing that has happened today."*

## FLOOR 49 — THE SPORE MOTHER
**Type:** Giant fungal hive-mind. Speaks in a chorus of dead voices.

**Intro:**
> **Spore Mother:** "We are the garden. We are the rot. We are what grows when nothing else will. You are meat. You will be soil."
> **Greg:** "Can I just pass through?"
> **Spore Mother:** "You can be compost through."

**Mid-Fight:**
> **Spore Mother:** "You burn us. We grow back. You cut us. We spread. You are fighting the inevitable. We are the inevitable. We are the thing that happens when you leave food in a drawer for six months."

**Death:**
> **Spore Mother:** "We... we were so close to being a forest. We had plans. We had... spores..." *(dissolves into glowing dust)*

**Special (if you have the mushroom companion):**
> **Spore Mother:** "You carry one of ours. The small one. The one that followed you. It chose poorly. But it chose. That is more than most fungi ever do." *(The mushroom companion absorbs the Spore Mother and evolves.)*

**Type:** Spectral librarian. Enormous. Made of floating books.

**Death:**
