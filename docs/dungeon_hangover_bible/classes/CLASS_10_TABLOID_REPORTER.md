# TABLOID REPORTER — "EXCLUSIVE: LOCAL IDIOT DOOMED."

## CLASS LORE

Once wrote a newspaper article. Not for a REAL newspaper — wrote for "The Dirty Mug Gazette," a newsletter that Nobody read. The article was titled "LOCAL MAN PROPOSES TO CHANDELIER — CHANDELIER SAYS YES." Most-read issue in Gazette history. Not because it was GOOD. Because it was TRUE. And because there were PICTURES.

Became a tabloid reporter. Not a GOOD tabloid reporter — the kind who shows up at a crime scene with a CAMERA and a COMPLETE DISREGARD for personal safety. Has reported on MANY things. Most of them tried to KILL. But the PHOTOS were worth it.

The editor of the Gazette said "This is LIBEUL." Said "It's TRUE." The editor said "That's WORSE." Said "But it SOLD PAPERS." The editor said "We don't SELL papers. We GIVE them away." Said "Then it's NOT libel." The editor said "That's NOT how LAW works." Said "It's how MY law works." The editor fired. Started an OWN paper. It lasted ONE issue. That issue had SEVEN lawsuits.

Philosophy: If it's not PHOTOGRAPHED, it didn't HAPPEN. And if it IS photographed, it's NEWS. And I am the NEWS.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Reveal, debuff, spread panic.
**Core Mechanic:** "EXCLUSIVE: LOCAL IDIOT DOOMED." Debuff via headlines, reveal enemy stats, spread panic.
**Tone:** Sensational, invasive, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** REVEALS. EXPOSES. Writes HEADLINES that become REALITY. The reason newspapers EXIST — so HEADLINES can be written.

## PROS

- **Best reveal in the game.** Can see EVERYTHING — HP, stats, next actions, hidden abilities. Revealed many things. With cameras.
- **AoE panic is BROKEN.** Headline + The Breaking News = ALL enemies are Panicked. Panicked many things. With headlines.
- **The Interview is FREE.** Know enemy's next 3 actions for 0 AP. Known many things. For free.

## CONS

- **Low direct damage.** Deals almost NO damage directly. Needs allies to FINISH the job. Needed many allies. With headlines.
- **Reliant on debuffs.** No debuffs = weak Scoop. No enemies = no Headline. Had NO enemies in many fights. It was BORING.
- **Invasive.** Exposes EVERYTHING. Including SECRETS. Exposed many secrets. The narrator is UNCOMFORTABLE.
