# ACCOUNTANT — "You've Been... Fiddling The Books."

## CLASS LORE

Did taxes once. Not for fun — found a shoebox full of receipts behind The Dirty Mug and thought "MONEY could be made from this." Couldn't. Made NEGATIVE money. But learned something: EVERYONE is fiddling the books. EVERYONE is cooking something. And it can be SMELLED.

Became an accountant. Not a GOOD accountant — the kind who shows up at audits with a briefcase full of VENDETTA. Once audited a goblin-owned business and found SEVEN sets of books. The goblin cried. Didn't care. Numbers don't LIE. People lie. Goblins lie. But numbers? Numbers are HONEST.

Once looked at a tavern owner's books and said "These don't balance." The owner said "They're FINE." They were NOT fine. The owner was laundering money through a cheese wheel distribution company. The accountant reported it. The owner got arrested. The accountant got FIRED. Worth it.

Philosophy: Numbers don't lie. People lie. I am the numbers. And I am DISAPPOINTED.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Debuff stacking, conditional damage, gold interaction.
**Core Mechanic:** "You've been... fiddling the books." Debuff stacking, conditional damage, gold interaction.
**Tone:** Bitter, pedantic, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** AUDITS. Finds DISCREPANCIES. Stacks DEBUFFS and watches enemies SLOWLY DIE from AUDIT-RELATED STRESS. The reason numbers were INVENTED.
