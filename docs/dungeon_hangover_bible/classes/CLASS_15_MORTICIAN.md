# MORTICIAN — "You're Not Dead Enough. Let Me Help."

## CLASS LORE

Once worked at a funeral home. Not because of being MORBID — worked at a funeral home because the funeral home had a CAFETERIA and thought the cafeteria was OPEN TO THE PUBLIC. It wasn't. The cafeteria was for THE DEAD. Ate lunch with THE DEAD for THREE WEEKS before anyone noticed.

Became a mortician. Not a GOOD mortician — the kind who shows up at a FUNERAL with a MAKEUP KIT and a COMPLETE DISREGARD for the concept of "dignity in death." Has prepared MANY bodies. Most of them looked BETTER DEAD. But learned things. Death is not the END — death is just a CAREER CHANGE. The dead are willING WORKERS. They just need the RIGHT MOTIVATION. And if death can't be beaten, it should be HIRED.

The funeral home director said "Don't TALK to the bodies." Said "They're DEAD." Said "They can TALK back." The director said "That's INSANE." Said "That's EFFICIENCY." The director said "That's a FIRING OFFENSE." Said "I'm RESIGNING." The director said "You're HIRED." Said "I thought I just RESIGNED." The director said "We DON'T have a RESIGNATION FORM." Said "I'll MAKE one." The director said "That's the SPIRIT."

Philosophy: Death is not the end. Death is a LAYOFF. And I am the RECRUITER.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Death exploiter, undead control, post-mortem.
**Core Mechanic:** "You're not dead enough. Let me help." Heal off kills, reanimate dead as temporary allies, embalming.
**Tone:** Macabre, professional, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** EXPLOITS death. REANIMATES. Makes the DEAD work. The reason funeral homes have EMBALMING FLUID — so it can be WEAPONIZED.

## COMBO DETAILS

**[COMBO: Grave Markers + Reanimation]:** Kill one enemy (gain +5% dmg), then reanimate them as a zombie. STRONGER and they're YOURS. Killed many things. Then reanimated them.

**[COMBO: Embalming Fluid + Last Rites]:** Prevent healing, then execute at 15% HP. Can't HEAL and they're DYING. Embalmed many things. Then last-rited them.

**[COMBO: The Morgue + The Resurrection]:** Store a body, then resurrect ALL dead at once. Stored many things. Then resurrected them.

**[COMBO: The Coffin Nail + The Burial]:** Nail them in place, then bury them. TRAPPED and BURIED. Nailed many things. Then buried them.

**[COMBO: The Death Walks + The Final Curtain]:** Mark all enemies for death, then curtain them. MARKED and CURTAINED. Walked many things. Then curtained them.

## PROS

- **Best summon variety in the game.** Can summon 10+ different undead types. Summoned many dead things. They're ALL different.
- **The Last Rites is BROKEN.** Execute at 15% HP for 1 AP. Executed many things. At 15% HP.
- **Healing off every kill.** The Death Certificate + Grave Markers = heals AND gets stronger. Healed many times. From kills.

## CONS

- **Useless if nothing dies.** No dead enemies = no Reanimation. No kills = no Grave Markers. Had NO dead in many fights. It was PEACEFUL. And USELESS.
- **Fragile summons.** Zombies have 50% stats. Skeletons have 5 HP. Lost many summons. To a STRONG WIND.
- **Macabre.** Requires talking to CORPSES. Talked to many corpses. The narrator is UNCOMFORTABLE.
