# DENTIST — "This Will Hurt Me More Than You. No, Actually, Just You."

## CLASS LORE

Went to the dentist once. The dentist said "You have SEVENTEEN cavities." Said "That's IMPOSSIBLE." The dentist said "You drink ENERGY DRINKS and eat CANDY for BREAKFAST." Said "It's called BREAKFAST OF CHAMPIONS." The dentist said "It's called BREAKFAST OF HOLES."

Became a dentist. Not a GOOD dentist — the kind who shows up at your house at 3 AM with a DRILL and a DREAM. Once pulled a tooth from a GOBLIN. The goblin didn't have DENTAL INSURANCE. Didn't care. Pulled MANY teeth. From MANY things. Has a COLLECTION.

The boarding house had a dentist. Dr. Payne. Told everyone "Flossing is OPTIONAL." Believed this for twenty years. Then Dr. Payne said "It's NOT optional, you IDIOT." Asked "But you SAID—" Dr. Payne said "I was being SARCASTIC." Said "Oh." That conversation changed everything.

Philosophy: Teeth are just BONES that SHOW. And I am the BONE SHOW. Open wide. This is going to HURT.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Precision damage, status application, bind.
**Core Mechanic:** "This will hurt me more than you. No, actually, just you." Accurate strikes, bleeding, binds.
**Tone:** Clinical, sadistic, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** PRECISE. Targets WEAK POINTS. Drills. Fills. EXTRACTS. The reason teeth were INVENTED — so they could be PULLED.

## PROS

- **Best precision damage in the game.** Ignores armor, targets weaknesses, and stacks Cavities. Precisioned many things. With drills.
- **Cavities is a UNIQUE status.** Stacks to 5 and ticks forever. Stacked many cavities. In many things.
- **Extraction is the best execute.** Non-boss below 30% HP: instant kill. Executed many things. With extraction.

## CONS

- **Low AoE.** Built for SINGLE TARGET. Against swarms, OVERWHELMED. Been overwhelmed by many swarms. With drills.
- **Reliant on debuffs.** No debuffs = weak Drill. Had NO debuffs in many fights. It was BAD.
- **Dental puns.** Tired of dental puns. Been tired of MANY dental puns. They're EXHAUSTING.
