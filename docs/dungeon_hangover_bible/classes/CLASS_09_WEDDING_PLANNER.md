# WEDDING PLANNER — "EVERYONE Has A Processional Order."

## CLASS LORE

Once planned a wedding. Not because of being qualified — the original planner had a "nervous breakdown" (Greg's words) and Greg was the only one in the room who owned a clipboard. The wedding was a DISASTER. The cake collapsed. The groom cried. The bride's aunt started a FIGHT. But learned something: chaos is just PLANNING that hasn't been SCHEDULED yet.

Became a wedding planner. Not a good one — the kind who shows up with a WHISTLE and a SPREADSHEET and a DETERMINATION to make this wedding HAPPEN even if it KILLS everyone involved. Has planned MANY weddings. Most of them ended in FIRE. But they were ORGANIZED fires.

The Dirty Mug hosted a wedding once. The groom: goblin. The bride: TROLL. The wedding planner (not Greg, the OTHER planner) quit halfway through. Stepped in. Said "EVERYONE has a PROCESSIONAL ORDER." The goblin said "What's a processional?" Said "It's the ORDER you WALK IN." Said "I don't WALK in order." Said "You do NOW." The wedding happened. Nobody died. Never hired again.

Philosophy: There's a processional order. There's a seating chart. And if you don't FOLLOW it, I will MAKE you follow it.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Ally buffer, chaos coordinator, positioning.
**Core Mechanic:** "EVERYONE has a processional order." Multi-target buffs, positioning, ally coordination.
**Tone:** Manic, organized, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** Coordinates. BUFFS. Makes sure everyone is in the RIGHT PLACE at the RIGHT TIME. The reason weddings have TIMELINES — so they can be ENFORCED.
