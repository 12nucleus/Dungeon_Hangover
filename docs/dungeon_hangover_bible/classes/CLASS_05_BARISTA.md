# BARISTA — "I Haven't Slept In Six Floors."

## CLASS LORE

Worked at a coffee shop. Not a GOOD coffee shop. The kind of coffee shop that makes someone question every life choice that led to that moment. "Bean There Done That" was the name. Hated it. Hated the customers. Hated the espresso machine. Hated the BEANS.

But learned things. Coffee is not a BEVERAGE — coffee is a WEAPON. The right amount of caffeine at the right moment can make a man feel INVINCIBLE. The wrong amount can make a man see SOUNDS. Espresso is just coffee that's been through TRAUMA.

Drank a LOT of coffee. So much that the blood type is now ROBUSTA. So much that time can be seen THROUGH. So much that a horse was arm-wrestled AND WON (the horse was also on coffee). A CAFFEINE DEMON.

Philosophy: Sleep is for the weak. Coffee is for the STRONG. And is the STRONGEST. Also, haven't slept in six floors. Can see COLORS that don't EXIST.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Speed, extra actions, critical hits.
**Core Mechanic:** "I haven't slept in six floors." Speed is life. Speed is DEATH. Speed is caffeine.
**Tone:** Jittery, manic, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** FAST. Attacks FIRST. Attacks AGAIN. Attacks so fast that enemies can't KEEP UP. Runs on PURE CAFFEINE and RAGE. Will crash EVENTUALLY. But not TODAY.

## COMBO DETAILS

**[COMBO: Double Shot + The Triple Shot]:** Two attacks + three attacks = FIVE attacks in one turn. Attacked FIVE times in many coffee shops.

**[COMBO: Speed Brew + The Overdose]:** +4 AP + +4 AP = +8 AP per turn. EIGHT actions. Had EIGHT actions in many coffee shops.

**[COMBO: Grind + The Crema]:** +10% crit + heal on crit = heals EVERY crit. Healed many times. In coffee shops.

**[COMBO: Caffeine Rush + The Crash]:** +1 AP then crash, then +3 AP and +20% dmg = a ROCKET. Been a rocket in many coffee shops.

**[COMBO: Steam Wand + The Frappuccino]:** –10% accuracy + freeze = can't hit AND can't move. Froze many things. In coffee shops.

## PROS

- **Highest action economy in the game.** Can attack 5+ times per turn. Attacked many times. In many coffee shops.
- **Incredible crit scaling.** Grind + The Crema = crits heal. Crit many things. In coffee shops.
- **Permanent power growth.** The Barista's Promise grants +1 AP per kill. Permanent AP. Been permanent in many coffee shops.

## CONS

- **The crash is REAL.** Speed Brew causes Exhaustion. The Overdose causes Crashed. Crashed in many coffee shops. It's UGLY.
- **Low base damage.** Hits FAST but SOFT. Without crits, a TICKLE machine. Tickled many things. In coffee shops.
- **Caffeine dependency.** NEEDS AP to function. No AP = no skills. Had no AP in many coffee shops. It was BAD.
