# HAUNTED CHEF — "It's Not Poisoned. It's Just... Assertive."

## CLASS LORE

Can't cook. Has NEVER been able to cook. Once burned WATER. Once set fire to a SANDWICH. Once made a dish so bad that the DISH complained. But keeps trying. Keeps cooking. Because of believing that if enough SALT and enough HOPE are added, anything becomes FOOD.

Worked at a restaurant once. "Le Château de La Rue" — two Michelin stars. Was hired as a DISHWASHER. Promoted to LINE COOK after the previous line cook had a "disagreement" with a soufflé. Was FIRED after having a disagreement with a BUNCH of soufflés. But learned things. Food is CHEMISTRY. Chemistry is MAGIC. Magic is just CHEMISTRY that hasn't been HEALTH-INSPECTED yet.

The Dirty Mug's kitchen was a DISASTER. The health inspector came. Said "This kitchen is a CRIME SCENE." The chef said "It's RUSTIC." The inspector said "Rustic doesn't have MOLD." The chef said "That's ARTISANAL mold." The inspector said "That's a HEALTH CODE VIOLATION." The chef said "It's a FLAVOR VIOLATION." The inspector closed the place. The chef got hired at the Mug. Same problems. Different kitchen.

Philosophy: Every dish tells a story. Every story has a RECIPE. And I am the RECIPE. Also, try the soup. It's FINE. (It's not fine.)

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Consumable damage, cooking, food effects.
**Core Mechanic:** "It's not poisoned. It's just... assertive." Consumable damage, cooking during combat, food effects.
**Tone:** Unhinged, culinary, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** COOKS. Throws FOOD. Makes DISHES that Haunt You. The reason kitchens have FIRE EXTINGUISHERS — so they can be IGNORED.

## COMBO DETAILS

**[COMBO: Hot Oil + The Flambé]:** Oil makes them slippery. Flambé sets them on FIRE. SLICK and BURNING. Oiled many things. Then flambéed them.

**[COMBO: The Salt + The Sous Vide]:** Salt prevents healing. Sous Vide deals dmg over time. They're DYING and CAN'T HEAL. Salted many things. Then sous-vided them.

**[COMBO: The Deglaze + The Gastrique]:** Deglaze buffs allies. Gastrique confuses enemies. Allies are STRONG, enemies are CONFUSED. Deglazed many things. Then gastriqued them.

**[COMBO: The Mise en Place + The Julienne]:** Mise en Place reduces skill costs. Julienne bleeds target. Organized many things. Then julienned them.

**[COMBO: The Honey + The Confit]:** Honey makes them sticky and attractive. Confit prevents death. They're SLOW, ATTRACTED, and CAN'T DIE. Honeyed many things. Then confited them.

## PROS

- **Best consumable generation in the game.** Creates FOOD, HEALING, and BUFFS. Generated many consumables. In kitchens.
- **The Salt is BROKEN.** Prevents healing for 3 turns. Salted many things. They couldn't heal.
- **Incredible debuff variety.** Bleeding, Burning, Confused, Slow, Blind — has debuffs for EVERYTHING. Debuffed many things. With food.

## CONS

- **Unpredictable.** Soufflé collapses. The Flambé attracts enemies. The Honey attracts enemies. Attracted many enemies. With honey.
- **Reliant on ingredients.** No dead enemies = No Stock. No potions = no healing. Had NO ingredients in many fights. It was BAD.
- **The food is QUESTIONABLE.** Cooking is... assertive. Allies sometimes refuse to eat. Cooked many meals. Most of them were RETURNED.
