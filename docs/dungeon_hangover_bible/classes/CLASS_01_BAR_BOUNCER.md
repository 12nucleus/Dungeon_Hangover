# BAR BOUNCER — "You're Not On The List"

## CLASS LORE

Greg was never a bouncer. A PATRON. The kind bouncers remember — the loud kind, the stupid kind, the kind that gets LOUDER. Bounced from The Dirty Mug fourteen times. A perfect record. O'Toole's. The Salty Dog. That place with the fish tank that someone may have fallen into during a bet. A bouncing legend.

But here's the thing about being bounced fifty times: you learn things. You learn how a bouncer THINKS. How they MOVE. Where they LOOK and where they DON'T. Every bouncer has a tell — a shift of weight, a glance, a moment of HESITATION. And if you're fast enough, small enough, and STUPID enough, you can get past ANYONE.

Greg is fast enough. Small enough. Stupid enough.

Doesn't bounce people because of being big. Bounces people because of being bounced MORE TIMES THAN ANYONE and LEARNED. The GRIP. The SHOVE. The LOOK. Every bouncer trick in the book? Already been on the receiving end. Remembered.

Three times in one night at The Dirty Mug. That record stands. Greg kept coming back. Always comes back. The bouncers called it "The Greg Cycle" — gets in, gets loud, gets bounced, comes back louder, gets bounced HARDER, comes back SOUNDER. A cycle. A beautiful cycle. The cycle that built an unbREAKABLE spirit.

Greg's philosophy: I've been thrown out of better places than this. You're not on the list. I'M not on the list. NOBODY'S on the list. The list is BULLSHIT.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Melee lockdown. Single-target control. Battlefield denial.
**Core Mechanic:** Grapple, shove, and physically prevent enemies from acting or moving.
**Tone:** "You're not on the list." Stubborn, belligerent, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** Locks down one enemy at a time. Grapples. Shoves. Ejects. The reason the rat can't reach the wizard. The reason the goblin can't run away. The reason YOU are not getting past, and FIRM about it. Bounced from every tavern in the city. Knows EVERY trick. DONE being bounced.

**[COMBO: Stool Smash + Last Call]:** If Stool Smash kills, Last Call's execute threshold is checked again, potentially chaining to adjacent enemies. Smashed many stools. In many taverns.

**[COMBO: Bouncer's Grip + Dentist's Floss]:** Grapple + Bind = 2-turn lockdown at 2 dmg/turn. They're not moving. Not acting. Just SUFFERING. Been in many headlocks. Knows the feel.

**[COMBO: Bouncer's Crash-Out + Mortician's Grave Markers]:** +10% per kill (Bouncer) + 5% per kill (Mortician) = +15% per kill. The body count is a BUFF. A long body count. In taverns.

## PROS

- **Best single-target control in the game.** Can lock down ANY enemy, including bosses. Grapple, banish, stun, eject — the enemy is NOT fighting back.
- **Incredibly tanky.** High physical resistance, damage reflection, and low-HP buffs make this the hardest class to kill. Survived fourteen tavern ejections. Can survive ANYTHING.
- **Beginner-friendly.** Tier 1 skills are straightforward and effective. Doesn't need to overthink. Just needs to SHOVE.

## CONS

- **Struggles against swarms.** Built for single-target lockdown. Against 5+ enemies, needs Crowd Control and The Gate or gets overwhelmed.
- **Low ranged options.** MELEE ONLY. If the enemy flies or runs away, needs to CHASE. Chased many people. In taverns. EXHAUSTING.
- **Predictable.** Does ONE thing: lock down and punch. If the enemy is immune to stun/grapple, has a BAD time.
