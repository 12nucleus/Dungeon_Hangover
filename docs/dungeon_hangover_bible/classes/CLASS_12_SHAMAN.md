# SHAMAN — "The Spirits Say You Smell."

## CLASS LORE

Once attended a spiritual retreat. Not because of being SPIRITUAL — attended because the retreat was next to a BREWERY and thought it was a BEER FESTIVAL. It wasn't. It was a spiritual retreat. Was the only one there who wasn't "open to the experience." Was open to BEER.

But something happened. Drank the ceremonial tea. The tea was... AWFUL. But SAW things. Saw SPIRITS. Saw the SPIRIT of The Dirty Mug's bouncer (still angry). Saw the SPIRIT of the chandelier that was proposed to (still saying yes). Saw MANY spirits. And the spirits saw BACK.

Became a shaman. Not a GOOD shaman — the kind who shows up with a RATTLE and a DREAM and a COMPLETE DISREGARD for spiritual PROTOCOL. Has summoned MANY spirits. Most of them are ANGRY. But they're USEFUL.

The spiritual elder said "The spirits are ANGRY." Said "The spirits are ALWAYS angry." The elder said "No, these are ANGRIER." Asked "Can we USE that?" The elder said "That's not how SPIRITUALITY works." Said "But can we?" The elder said "YES but you SHOULDN'T." Said "So that's a YES." Expelled from the retreat. Started an OWN retreat. It lasted ONE day. That day had SEVEN exorcisms.

Philosophy: The spirits are ALWAYS talking. The spirits are ALWAYS judging. And the spirits say you SMELL. But they also say you're DOOMED, so LISTEN.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Spirit summoning, status infliction, cursing.
**Core Mechanic:** "The spirits say you smell." Persistent AoE, spirit companion, cursing.
**Tone:** Mystical, unsettling, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** SUMMONS. CURSES. Makes the spirits FIGHT. The reason shamans use DRUMS — so they can be POUNDED.

## PROS

- **Best spirit summoning in the game.** Can have MULTIPLE spirits active. Summoned many spirits. Most of them are ANGRY.
- **The Curse is BROKEN.** Deals dmg per debuff. With 5 debuffs, that's 20 dmg/turn. Cursed many things. They're DYING.
- **The Spirit Walk is FREE invulnerability.** Become ethereal for 1 AP. Walked many things. Safely.

## CONS

- **Spirits are UNRELIABLE.** Spirits attack RANDOMLY. Sometimes they hit FRIENDS. Hit many friends. With spirits.
- **The Vision Quest is USELESS in combat.** Can't attack while visioning. Visioned many things. While getting HIT.
- **Spirit dependency.** No spirits = no damage. Had NO spirits in many fights. It was LONELY.
