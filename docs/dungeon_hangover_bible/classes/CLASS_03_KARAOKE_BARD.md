# KARAOKE BARD — "It's Not About The Voice, It's About The FEELING."

## CLASS LORE

Has never been able to sing. Sang at a cousin's wedding once — THREE people cried. Not from joy. From PAIN. Sang at The Dirty Mug's weekly karaoke night and the BARTENDER asked to stop. Bartenders NEVER ask. That's how bad it is. A health hazard.

But here's the thing: it WORKS. Not because it's good. Because it's REAL. Sings with the PAIN of a man who proposed to a chandelier. Sings with the HOPE of a man who woke up in his underwear at the bottom of a dungeon and DECIDED TO CLIMB. Sings with the CONVICTION of someone who doesn't know they're bad. And that CONVICTION is CONTAGIOUS.

The karaoke nickname is "The Greg Echo." Not because of echoing. Because the voice ECHOES in the minds of everyone who hears it. Can't FORGET it. Tried. Failed. The voice is HAUNTING.

Was performing at The Dirty Mug the night the chandelier proposal happened. SAW the chandelier say yes. It wasn't the candlelight flickering. An actual YES. Crystal resonance. Musical affirmation. Knew then: either the WORST person ever met or the BEST. Still climbing to find out which.

Philosophy: They can boo. They can throw bottles. They can leave. But they'll REMEMBER. They'll remember FOREVER. And that's SHOWMANSHIP.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Ally buff, enemy debuff, charm.
**Core Mechanic:** "It's not about the voice, it's about the FEELING." Songs buff allies, debuff enemies, and charm the unwilling.
**Tone:** Dramatic, musical, surprisingly powerful.
**Playstyle:** Sings. Songs feel the battlefield. Allies hit harder. Allies defend better. Enemies miss. Enemies fear. Enemies DANCE (against their will). Doesn't fight DIRECTLY. Makes EVERYONE ELSE fight better (or worse).

## COMBO DETAILS

**[COMBO: Power Ballad + Anthem]:** +15% dmg (ballad) + +15% dmg and +10% speed (anthem) = +30% dmg and +10% speed. Buffed many times. With songs.

**[COMBO: Crowd Work + Whisper Song]:** Charm one enemy (Crowd Work), then whisper-song another. TWO charmed enemies. Charmed many people. With songs.

**[COMBO: Mic Drop + Musical Number]:** Stun all (Mic Drop), then force all to DANCE (Musical Number). STUNNED and DANCING. Stunned many things. Then made them dance.

**[COMBO: Sheet Music (Steal Skill) + Any Skill]:** Copy ANY skill from any tree, then use it. Copied many skills. From many trees.

**[COMBO: The Lullaby + Scream Singing]:** Make them Drowsy (Lullaby), then deafen them (Scream Singing). SLEEPY and DEAF. Lullabied many things. Then scream-sang them.

## PROS

- **Best buff/debuff class in the game.** Can buff allies, debuff enemies, AND charm. Buffed many people. At taverns.
- **Charm is broken.** Crowd Work and The Final Song turn enemies into allies temporarily. Turned many enemies into allies. At taverns.
- **Great evasion.** Headliner gives up to +40% evasion. Hard to hit. Been hard to hit in many taverns.

## CONS

- **Terrible direct damage.** Deals almost NO damage directly. Needs allies (groupies, companions, charmed enemies) to deal damage. Needed many allies. At taverns.
- **Singing has a cost.** Mic Drop makes you Hoarse. Power Ballad makes you Distracted. Every buff has a DRAWBACK. Had many drawbacks. At taverns.
- **Voice-dependent.** If Silenced, loses MOST abilities. Been silenced in many taverns. It was the QUIETEST ever.
