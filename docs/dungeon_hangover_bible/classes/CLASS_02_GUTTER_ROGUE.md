# GUTTER ROGUE — "Nothing Personal. Actually, It Is."

## CLASS LORE

Grew up in the alleys behind The Dirty Mug. Not IN The Dirty Mug — couldn't afford it. Lived in the alley, eating what the tavern threw away. Learned to steal before learning to walk. Learned to lie before learning to talk. Learned to FIGHT before learning to think. And learned to fight DIRTY before learning to fight at all.

Once stole a wallet, a watch, and a SANDWICH from a city guard. The guard didn't notice. The guard was DRUNK. Was ELEVEN. A prodigy.

Always been a little BITTER. A little PETTY. The kind of person who, when knocked down, doesn't get back up — rolls into the shadows and WAITS. Been waiting whole life. Waiting for the perfect moment. The perfect angle. For you to turn around so EVERYTHING can be taken.

Has a particular set of skills. Can look at an enemy and know EXACTLY how much HP they have, how much gold they're carrying, and what their TAX BRACKET would be if they weren't TRYING TO KILL. Can audit ANYTHING. Has audited MANY things. Found DISCREPANCIES.

Philosophy: If you're not cheating, you're not trying. If you're not stealing, you're not living. If you're not stabbing them in the back, you're doing it WRONG.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Bleed, steal, trap, stealth.
**Core Mechanic:** "Nothing personal. Actually, it is." Exploit weaknesses, fight dirty, profit.
**Tone:** Petty, acquisitive, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** A coward who WINS. Doesn't fight fair. Fights FIRST, from behind, with a poisoned knife, after stealing your wallet. The reason locks exist. The reason locks FAIL.

**[COMBO: Backstab + Shadow Step + Ambush]:** Teleport behind, attack from behind while hidden = 3x dmg + 40% Backstab = 4.2x dmg multiplier. One-shotted many people. In alleys.

**[COMBO: Smoke Bomb + Pocket Sand + The Blackout]:** –40% accuracy from smoke, –20% from sand, + Confused from Blackout. Can't hit SQUAT. Blinded many people. In alleys.

**[COMBO: Gutter Stank + Poison Blade + Nauseated]:** Miss = Nauseated (–1 accuracy). Hit = Poisoned (1/turn). A bioweapon. Been a bioweapon in every alley.

## PROS

- **Highest single-target burst in the game.** Backstab + Ambush + Shadow Step = 4.2x dmg multiplier. Can one-shot ANYTHING. One-shotted many things. In alleys.
- **Best loot generation.** Pickpocket, The Heist, and The Perfect Crime mean MORE loot than anyone. Stole many things. In alleys.
- **Unmatched stealth.** Vanish, Shadow Step, and Smoke Bomb make nearly impossible to hit. Been unseen in every alley.

## CONS

- **Fragile.** Lowest HP and defense of any class. If caught, DIES. Been caught in many alleys. It HURT.
- **Reliant on positioning.** Backstab requires being BEHIND. Ambush requires being HIDDEN. If can't get behind or hide, loses HALF the damage.
- **Ethically questionable.** Steals. Cheats. Fights dirty. Been ethically questionable in every alley. The narrator JUDGES.
