# INSURANCE ADJUSTER — "That's Not Covered Under Your Plan."

## CLASS LORE

Once filed an insurance claim. Not because of HAVING insurance — filed a claim because an insurance FORM was found and thought "MONEY could be made from this." Couldn't. Was DENIED. The claim was for "emotional damages sustained during chandelier proposal." The insurance company said "chandeliers are NOT covered." Said "THIS chandelier said YES." The insurance company said "STILL not covered."

Became an insurance adjuster. Not a GOOD insurance adjuster — the kind who shows up at a CLAIM with a CAMERA and a COMPLETE DISREGARD for the concept of "customer satisfaction." Has adjusted MANY claims. Most of them were DENIED. But learned things. RISK is just PROFIT that hasn't been CALCULATED yet. Every enemy has a WEAKNESS and every weakness has a DEDUCTIBLE. And if enough claims are denied, the claims start to deny THEMSELVES.

The insurance company said "You're denying TOO MANY claims." Said "I'm denying the RIGHT amount." The company said "You denied a claim for a HOUSE FIRE." Said "The house was ALREADY on fire when they bought the policy." The company said "That's NOT how INSURANCE works." Said "It's how MY insurance works." Was fired. Started an OWN company. It lasted ONE week. That week had ZERO approved claims.

Philosophy: Every risk has a PRICE. Every price has a DEDUCTIBLE. And your deductible is YOUR PROBLEM.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Risk assessor, fine print trap, calculated counters.
**Core Mechanic:** "That's not covered under your plan." Calculated counters, conditional buffs, denial of enemy attacks.
**Tone:** Bureaucratic, punishing, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** ASSESSES. CALCULATES. DENIES. The reason insurance has FINE PRINT — so it can be READ.

## COMBO DETAILS

**[COMBO: Risk Assessment + Claim Denied]:** Learn their pattern, then deny their strongest attack. LEARNED many patterns. Then denied them.

**[COMBO: Deductible + The Liability]:** Reduce their dmg AND make them take reflect dmg. WEAK and HURTING. Deducted many things. Then liability'd them.

**[COMBO: The Out-of-Network + The Exclusion]:** Make them take more dmg AND prevent healing. FRAGILE and DYING. Out-of-networked many things. Then excluded them.

**[COMBO: The Settlement + The Appeal]:** Offer them a settlement (surrender or dmg), then appeal if they refuse. Settled many things. Then appealed them.

**[COMBO: The Fine Print + The Pre-Existing Condition]:** Cancel their actions AND deal more dmg to debuffed enemies. CANCELLED and BURNING. Fine-printed many things. Then pre-existinged them.

## PROS

- **Best damage mitigation in the game.** Can deny attacks, reduce dmg, and cap hits. Mitigated many things. With policies.
- **The Claim Denied is BROKEN.** Negate ANY enemy action for 1 AP. Denied many things. Completely.
- **Incredible utility.** Assess, deny, heal, buff, steal gold — has a POLICY for everything. Policed many things. With insurance.

## CONS

- **Boring.** LITERALLY insurance. The narrator is ALREADY bored. Been bored by many insurance things.
- **Paperwork.** Every skill requires FINE PRINT. The fine print is LONG. Read many fine prints. They're EXHAUSTING.
- **Reliant on enemies.** No enemies = nothing to deny. No attacks = no deductible. Had NO enemies in many fights. It was PEACEFUL. And BORING.
