# PLUMBER — "Where's Your Shut-Off Valve?"

## CLASS LORE

Fixed a toilet once. Thought "MONEY could be made from this." Couldn't. Made NEGATIVE money. The toilet EXPLODED. Learned something: water is POWER. Water is PRESSURE. Water is DESTRUCTION.

Became a plumber. Not a GOOD plumber — the kind who shows up at your house at 2 AM with a WRENCH and a DREAM. Once fixed a pipe in The Dirty Mug's basement. The pipe was RUPTURED. The pipe was ANGRY. Fixed it. The pipe STILL leaks. Doesn't care. Fixed MANY pipes. Most of them STILL leak.

The Dirty Mug's pipes were a DISASTER. Lead pipes. Clay pipes. One pipe made of CHEESE (don't ask). The health inspector came. Closed the place for a WEEK. The plumber was called. Fixed everything in a DAY. The inspector came back. Said "This is WORSE now." The plumber said "But it's FIXED." The inspector said "That's not what FIXED means." The plumber said "It is to ME."

Philosophy: Water finds a way. I am the way. And the way is LEAKING.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Environment, AoE, pressure.
**Core Mechanic:** "Where's your shut-off valve?" Unclog, burst pipes, pressure, wrench smashes.
**Tone:** Wet, destructive, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** Manipulates the ENVIRONMENT. Bursts pipes. Creates FLOODS. The reason plumbing was INVENTED — so it could be BROKEN.

## COMBO DETAILS

**[COMBO: Burst Pipe + Main Drain]:** Burst Pipe wets everyone. Main Drain deals 8 dmg to wet targets (double = 16 dmg). Burst many pipes. Then main-drained them.

**[COMBO: The Plunger + The Drain Snake]:** Pull them toward you, then bind them with a snake. CLOSE and TRAPPED. Plunged many things. Then snaked them.

**[COMBO: The Hot Water + The Cold Water]:** Scald them, then freeze them. Thermal SHOCK. BURNING and FROZEN. Hot-watered many things. Then cold-watered them.

**[COMBO: The Sump Pump + Burst Pipe]:** Soak them, then burst a pipe. Wet targets take DOUBLE. Soaked many things. Then burst them.

**[COMBO: The Water Bill + The Sewer]:** Bankrupt them, then sewer them. POOR and FILTHY. Billed many things. Then sewered them.

## PROS

- **Best AoE in the game.** Can hit ALL enemies in huge radiuses. Hit many things. With pipes.
- **Environmental destruction.** Uses the ROOM. Pipes, water, toilets. Destroyed many rooms. With plumbing.
- **Wet status is BROKEN.** Wet targets take double from Burst Pipe and Main Drain. Wet many things. Then burst them.

## CONS

- **Reliant on water.** No pipes = no Burst Pipe. No water = no Main Drain. Had no water in many fights. It was BAD.
- **Friendly fire.** AoE hits EVERYONE. Companions get WET too. Wet many companions. They're STILL mad.
- **Leaking.** Side effect: the room floods. Flooded many rooms. The flood is PERMANENT.
