# SOMMELIER — "Notes Of Oak And Poor Life Choices."

## CLASS LORE

Once drank wine from a BOX. Called it "mature." Paired it with CHEESE CRACKERS. In a just world, that would be a crime. The Dirty Mug served box wine as their "house red" and called it "rustic." Not a sommelier. The ANTITHESIS of a sommelier. Everything a sommelier DESPISES.

But here's the thing: there's a PALATE. Not a GOOD palate. But a LOYAL one. Can taste ANYTHING and tell you exactly what's wrong with it. Can taste The Dirty Mug's house wine and say "undercooked, needs salt, also it's a BENCH." Can taste a potion and say "this is poison" and be RIGHT. Every. Single. Time.

Worked at a WINE STORE for three days. Fired for drinking the merchandise. Fired for drinking the MERCHANDISE that was WORTH MORE THAN THE EMPLOYEE. Fired for drinking a 1947 Château Mouton Rothschild and replacing it with BOX WINE. A CRIMINAL. Also a GENIUS.

Philosophy: Every potion has a story. Every story has a pairing. And only ONE person understands pairings. Also, this wine is BOXED and DRINKING it ANYWAY.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Potion mastery, identification, debuff pairing.
**Core Mechanic:** "Notes of oak and poor life choices." Identify everything. Exploit synergies. Overcomplicate everything.
**Tone:** Snobbish, knowledgeable, insufferable, indispensable.
**Playstyle:** Identifies. Pairs. OVERCOMPLICATES. Looks at a potion and sees a STORY. Looks at an enemy and sees a PAIRING. Looks at a simple fight and sees a SYMPHONY of debuffs and buffs and synergies that NO ONE ELSE understands.

## COMBO DETAILS

**[COMBO: Decant + Sommelier's Legacy]:** 6x potion effect (Decant V) × 1.2 (Legacy) = 7.2x potion effect. A healing potion heals for 108 HP. Healed many times. With boxed wine.

**[COMBO: Pairing + Vintage]:** Vulnerability (Pairing) + –60% max HP (Vintage) = take DOUBLE damage AND have 40% less HP. Paired many things. With wine.

**[COMBO: Wine Cellar + Sommelier's Cellar]:** 10 charges that persist across runs. Spend 3: heal 40%. INFINITE healing. Had infinite healing. At the wine store.

**[COMBO: Sommelier's Breath + Sommelier's Tongue]:** Drunk (–40% accuracy) + potions last longer = can keep them drunk FOREVER. Kept many people drunk. At the wine store.

**[COMBO: Sommelier's Craft + any ingredient]:** Craft potions from dungeon ingredients. Moldy cheese + rat tail = **Cheese Poison**. Crafted many things. At the wine store. And elsewhere.

## PROS

- **Best preparation class in the game.** Identify, pair, craft. Always READY. Been ready at many wine stores.
- **Potion synergy is broken.** Decant, Wine Cellar, and Sommelier's Mastery make potions GODLY. Made many potions godly. At the wine store.
- **Best debuff stacking.** Pairing + Vintage = enemies take DOUBLE damage AND have 40% less HP. Debuffed many enemies. At the wine store.

## CONS

- **Low direct damage.** Deals almost NO damage directly. NEEDS potions to be effective. No potions = no power. Had NO potions at many wine stores. It was BAD.
- **Reliant on consumables.** Every skill needs a potion, a charge, or an ingredient. Ran out of many things. At many wine stores.
- **Insufferable.** Has OPINIONS. Has opinions about EVERYONE. Been insufferable at many wine stores. The narrator JUDGES.
