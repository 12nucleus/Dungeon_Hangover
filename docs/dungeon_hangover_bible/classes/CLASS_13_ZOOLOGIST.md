# ZOOLOGIST — "Fascinating. It's Trying To Eat Me."

## CLASS LORE

Once visited a zoo. Not because of liking ANIMALS — visited because the zoo had a RESTAURANT and thought the restaurant was INSIDE the zoo. It wasn't. The restaurant was ACROSS THE STREET. Accidentally entered the LION ENCLOSURE. The lion looked back. Looked at the lion. Said "Fascinating." The lion said nothing. The lion was CONSIDERING.

Became a zoologist. Not a GOOD zoologist — the kind who shows up at a TIGER's cage with a NOTEBOOK and a COMPLETE DISREGARD for the concept of "personal space." Has studied MANY animals. Most of them tried to EAT. But learned their SECRETS. What they EAT. Where they SLEEP. How they HUNT. And how to MAKE THEM HUNT FOR.

The head zookeeper said "Don't go in the cage." Went in the cage. The zookeeper said "The lion will EAT you." Said "Fascinating." The lion ATE the notebook. Said "The lion ate my NOTES." The zookeeper said "That's what I SAID would happen." Said "But now I know what lions EAT." The zookeeper said "NOTEBOOKS. Lions eat NOTEBOOKS." Said "SCIENCE."

Philosophy: Every animal has a NATURE. Every nature has a WEAKNESS. And I am the WEAKNESS. Also, fascinating. It's trying to EAT me.

---

## CLASS IDENTITY

**Role:** Creature tamer, form-shifter, beast synergy.
**Core Mechanic:** "Fascinating. It's trying to eat me." Turn enemies into temporary allies, beast forms.
**Tone:** Curious, reckless, surprisingly effective.
**Playstyle:** TAMES. TRANSFORMS. Makes the ANIMALS fight. The reason zoos have WARNING SIGNS — so they can be IGNORED.

## PROS

- **Best creature control in the game.** Can TAME enemies, summon animals, and transform. Controlled many things. With science.
- **The Tame is BROKEN.** Turn ANY beast-type enemy into an ally. Tamed many things. They're YOURS.
- **Incredible variety.** 50 different animal abilities. MANY animals. They're ALL different.

## CONS

- **Useless against non-beasts.** Tame only works on beast-types. Against GOBLINS, NOTHING. Had NOTHING in many fights. Against goblins.
- **Unreliable.** Tame can FAIL. Mimicry copies RANDOM abilities. Failed many times. At taming.
- **Animal noises.** Requires making ANIMAL NOISES. Made many animal noises. The narrator is UNCOMFORTABLE.
