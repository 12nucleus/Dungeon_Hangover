## ENCOUNTERS

### LAVA FLOW (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Stepping in lava.
**Description:** *"Lava. LAVA. Your foot is in LAVA. Your foot is in lava and your foot is BURNING. Your foot is in lava and your foot is burning and your foot is ON FIRE."*
**Effect:** 5 dmg + **Burning** (2/turn, 3 turns).
**Resolution:** Jump out or endure.

### FIRE ELEMENTAL AMBUSH (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Taking the fire crystal.
**Description:** *"The crystal was a GUARD. The crystal was a guard and the crystal is ANGRY. The crystal was a guard and the crystal is angry and the crystal is ALIVE."*
**Enemies:** 2× Fire Elementals.
**Resolution:** Fight or flee.

### FORGE UPGRADE (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Using the eternal forge.
**Description:** *"The forge. The ETERNAL forge. The forge is HOT. The forge is hot and the forge is READY. The forge is hot and the forge is ready and the forge can UPGRADE. The forge is hot and the forge is ready and the forge can upgrade your WEAPON."*
**Effect:** Upgrade one weapon (+10% damage, permanent for the run).
**Resolution:** Upgrade or don't.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE TUNNELS
**Objective:** Navigate the molten tunnels. Reach Floor 28.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate lava → Defeat Forge Master → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE COOLANT
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Find a way to cool the lava. Creates a path.
**Reward:** Safe passage. **Cooling Salve** (+10% fire resistance).

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE FIRE ELEMENTAL
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Fight or negotiate with the fire elemental.
**Reward:** Fight: **Fire Crystal**. Negotiate: **Fire Friend** (+5% fire damage).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE FORGE
**Giver:** The Forge Master (Room 3).
**Objective:** Bring coal or wood as an offering.
**Reward:** Use the forge. Upgrade any weapon. FREE.

## BOSS: THE FORGE MASTER (Room 3)

**HP:** 40
**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 3 (from heat-treated armor)
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Hammer Smash:** 5 dmg, 25% **Stun**.
- **Lava Splash:** 3 dmg + **Burning** (2/turn, 3 turns).
- **Forge Fire:** All in 2D radius: 3 dmg + **Burning**.
- **Anvil Throw:** 4 dmg + **Prone**.
- **Heat Treatment:** Heals 3 HP/turn while standing near lava.

**Drops:**
- **Forge Master's Hammer** (weapon: 7 dmg, 25% **Stun**, +10% fire damage)
- **Fire Crystal** (passive: +10% fire resistance)
- 30 gold
- **Cooling Cloak** (Body: +10% fire resistance)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Forge Master falls. The Forge Master falls and the forge goes COLD. The Forge Master falls and the forge goes cold and the tunnels are QUIET. The Forge Master falls and the forge goes cold and the tunnels are quiet and the tunnels are DARK. The Forge Master falls and the forge goes cold and the tunnels are quiet and the tunnels are dark and you are... you are COLD. You are cold and you are ALIVE. You are cold and you are alive and you are WELCOME."*
