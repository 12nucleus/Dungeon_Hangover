# FLOOR 16 — THE WAR CAMP

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** An army that died waiting for a war that never came. They're still WAITING.
**Mood:** Melancholy. The army is dead. The army is still WAITING. The army will ALWAYS wait.
**Ambient:** Wind. Flags. The creak of old tents.
**Lighting:** Grey. Overcast. The first SKY you've seen in floors.
**Soundtrack:** Wind. Flags. The sound of WAITING.

## ENCOUNTERS

### GENERAL'S QUESTION (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Talking to the General.
**Description:** *"The General asks you. The General asks you if the WAR happened. The General asks you if the war happened and the General is HOPEFUL."*
**Effect:** Tell him yes or no. Both have consequences.
**Resolution:** Lie or truth.

### SOLDIER'S LETTER (Room 4)
**Trigger:** Reading the letter.
**Description:** *"A letter. A SOLDIER's letter. The soldier's letter is from the soldier's FAMILY. The soldier's letter is from the soldier's family and the soldier's letter is SAD. The soldier's letter is from the soldier's family and the soldier's letter is sad and the soldier's letter is LONELY."*
**Effect:** Lore. A soldier's family. The soldier died waiting.
**Resolution:** Deliver the letter (Floor 11) or keep it.

### WAR DRUM (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Beating the war drum.
**Description:** *"The drum BEATS. The drum beats and the army MARCHES. The drum beats and the army marches and the army is DEAD. The drum beats and the army marches and the army is dead and the army is ETERNAL."*
**Effect:** All soldiers attack. Or: all soldiers are **Buffed** (if you have the War Drum from Floor 31).
**Resolution:** Fight or use the drum.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE CAMP
**Objective:** Navigate the camp. Reach Floor 15.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Deal with General → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE GENERAL
**Giver:** The General (Room 3).
**Objective:** Tell the General the war is over. He can rest.
**Reward:** Peaceful resolution. **General's Sword**. All soldiers rest.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE SOLDIER'S LETTER
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Find a letter from a soldier's family. Deliver it to Floor 11's ghost.
**Reward:** Emotional resolution. **Soldier's Gratitude** (+5% XP).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE WAR DRUM
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Beat the war drum. The army marches.
**Reward:** **War Drum** (consumable: buffs all allies). Or: fight the army.

## BOSS: THE GENERAL (Room 3, if you tell him the war never came)

**HP:** 40
**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 3
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Sword Swing:** 5 dmg, 15% **Bleeding**.
- **Shield Bash:** 4 dmg, 20% **Stun**.
- **War Cry:** All soldiers: +15% damage, 2 turns.
- **Summon Soldiers:** Summons 2× Ghost Soldiers.
- **Last Stand:** Below 25% HP: +25% damage.

**Drops:**
- **General's Sword** (weapon: 7 dmg, 15% **Bleeding**)
- **General's Helm** (Head: +5% physical resistance, +5% damage)
- 30 gold
- **War Map** (shows the full war plan)

**Narrator on death:** *"The General falls. The General falls and the army is QUIET. The General falls and the army is quiet and the army is FREE. The General falls and the army is quiet and the army is free and the army can REST. The General falls and the army is quiet and the army is free and the army can rest and the army can FINALLY rest. The army can rest and the army can finally rest and the army is DONE."*
