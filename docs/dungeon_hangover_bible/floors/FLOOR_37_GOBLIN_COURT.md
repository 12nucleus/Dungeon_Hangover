# FLOOR 37 — GOBLIN COURT

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Goblin nobles. Mockery. Social combat. You must survive wit.
**Mood:** Humiliating. The nobles mock you. You must survive socially.
**Ambient:** Giggling. Whispering. The clink of tiny goblets.
**Lighting:** Candelabras. Opulent. Almost elegant.
**Soundtrack:** Court music (goblin-style). Mocking laughter.

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — COURT HALL
**Dimensions:** 8m × 6m. Opulent. Goblin nobles everywhere.
**Type:** Hub / Social
**Contents:**
- A bonfire (lit — but it's DECORATIVE, not for warmth)
- 8× Goblin nobles (mock you, challenge you)
- A throne (empty — the Baron is in Room 4)
- A coat of arms (goblin heraldry — absurd)
**Enemies:** None (unless you insult them).
**Narrator on entry:** *"A court. A GOBLIN court. The goblin nobles are LAUGHING. The goblin nobles are laughing at YOU. The goblin nobles are laughing at you and your UNDERWEAR. The goblin nobles are laughing at you and your underwear and your STUPID face."*

---

### ROOM 2 — ANTECHAMBER
**Type:** Social + Loot
**Contents:**
- A waiting room (goblins waiting to see the Baron)
- A gossiping goblin (tells you secrets — for a price)
- A coat check (you have no coat. The goblin is SUSPICIOUS.)
- A privy (locked — someone's hiding in there)
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"An antechamber. A WAITING room. The goblins are WAITING. The goblins are waiting to see the BARON. The goblins are waiting to see the Baron and they're NERVOUS. The goblins are waiting to see the Baron and they're nervous and they're GOSSIPING."*

---

### ROOM 3 — MOCKERY HALL
**Type:** Social + Combat
**Contents:**
- A stage (goblins perform mockery)
- A heckling goblin (heckles YOU)
- A comedy routine (about you. about your UNDERWEAR.)
- A trapdoor (escape route — but it's HUMILIATING)
**Enemies:** None (unless you fight back).
**Narrator on entry:** *"A mockery hall. A hall of MOCKERY. The goils are PERFORMING. The goblins are performing a ROUTINE. The goblins are performing a routine about YOU. The goblins are performing a routine about you and your UNDERWEAR and your STUPID face and the goblins are LAUGHING."*

### ROOM 4 — THRONE ROOM
**Type:** Boss arena
**Contents:**
- The Baron of Mockery (Boss — see Boss section)

- A ridicule rack (torture device — it ridicules you)
**Enemies:** The Baron + Guards.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The Baron. The BARON of Mockery. The baron who MOCKS. The baron who mocks EVERYTHING. The baron who mocks everything and the baron is GOOD at it."*

### ROOM 5 — PRIVY COUNCIL
**Type:** Social + Secret
**Contents:**
- A secret meeting room

**Enemies:** None (unless caught).
**Narrator on entry:** *"A privy council. A SECRET meeting. The goblins are PLOTTING. The goblins are plotting against the BARON. The goblins are plotting against the Baron and they're WHISPERING."*

## ENCOUNTERS

### MOCKERY (Room 1)
**Trigger:** Entering the court.
**Description:** *"The nobles mock you. The nobles mock your UNDERWEAR. The nobles mock your underwear and your STUPID face."*
**Effect:** Must respond. Options: mock back, flatter, insult, ignore.
**Resolution:** Social combat.

### HECKLING (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Entering the mockery hall.
**Description:** *"A goblin heckles you. The goblin heckles your UNDERWEAR. The goblin heckles your underwear and your STUPID face."*
**Effect:** Must respond. Options: fight back (wit), fight back (fist), ignore.
**Resolution:** Social or physical combat.

### CONSPIRACY (Room 5)
**Trigger:** Eavesdropping.
**Description:** *"The goblins are plotting. The goblins are plotting against the BARON. The goblins are plotting against the Baron and they CAUGHT you."*
**Effect:** Fight or join conspiracy.
**Resolution:** Multiple options.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE COURT
**Objective:** Navigate the court. Reach Floor 36.
**Steps:** Enter → Survive mockery → Defeat Baron → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: FLATTER THE BARON
**Giver:** The Baron (Room 4).
**Objective:** Flatter the Baron.
**Reward:** Title ("Baron's Favorite"). **Noble's Medal** (useless but pretentious).

### SIDE QUEST 2: INSULT THE BARON
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Insult the Baron.
**Reward:** Fight his guards. **Baron's Signet Ring** (+10% gold find).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE SECRET PASSAGE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 5).
**Objective:** Find the hidden tunnel behind the throne.
**Reward:** Treasure room (20 gold, **Noble's Ring**).

## BOSS: THE BARON OF MOCKERY (Room 4)

**HP:** 25
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Mockery:** 2 dmg + **Humiliated** (–10% damage, 2 turns).
- **Laughing Fit:** All in 2D radius: **Distracted**.
- **Ridicule:** Target's worst stat is exposed (–20% that stat, 2 turns).
- **Summon Guards:** Summons 2× Goblin Guards.

**Drops:**
- **Baron's Signet Ring** (Ring: +10% gold find)
- **Noble's Medal** ( Necklace: +5% charisma)
- 20 gold

**Narrator on death:** *"The Baron falls. The Baron falls and the court is QUIET. The Baron falls and the court is quiet and the goblins are SHOCKED. The Baron falls and the court is quiet and the goblins are shocked and they're... they're SAD. They're sad because the Baron was CRUEL. The Baron was cruel and the Baron was MEAN but the Baron was FUNNY. The Baron was cruel and the Baron was mean and the Baron was funny and now the Baron is DEAD and the court is BORING."*
