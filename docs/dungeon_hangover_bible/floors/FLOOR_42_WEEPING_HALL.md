# FLOOR 42 — THE WEEPING HALL

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Walls that cry. A hall of tears. Sadness made physical.
**Mood:** Melancholy. Heavy. You cry. Everyone cries. The walls cry.
**Ambient:** Crying. Sobbing. Water flowing. Echoes of grief.
**Lighting:** Pale blue. Water refracts light. Rainbows in tears.
**Soundtrack:** Crying. Water. A lullaby (distant, broken).

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY HALL
**Dimensions:** 6m × 4m. The walls cry. Water runs down.
**Type:** Safe zone / Hub
**Contents:**
- A bonfire (lit — the water doesn't extinguish it. Magic.)
- A tear vial (can be filled: **Tear Vial** — consumable, heals 10 HP)
- A sadness journal (read: "Day 1: The walls cry. Day 2: I cry. Day 3: We all cry.")
- A dry spot (the only dry spot — resting here: heal 10 HP, once)
**Lighting:** Pale blue. Rainbows.
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The walls cry. The walls are CRYING. The walls have been crying for THREE HUNDRED YEARS. The walls are TIRED. The walls are tired of CRYING. But the walls CAN'T STOP."*

### ROOM 2 — TEAR POOL
**Dimensions:** 5m × 5m. Knee-deep water. Tears.
**Type:** Hazard + Loot
**Contents:**

## PUZZLES

### THE CRYING CHILD PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The child is crying. How do you comfort them?
**Solutions:**
1. Give the stuffed bear: Child is comforted. Gives you **Child's Tear**.
2. Play the music box: Child is comforted. All on floor calm.
3. Tell a story: Child is comforted. You gain **Sadness Wisdom** (+5% XP from emotional encounters).
4. Leave: Child keeps crying. You feel BAD.
**Narrator on solving:** *"The child smiles. The child LAUGHS. The child is HAPPY. The child is happy for the first time in THREE HUNDRED YEARS. The child can REST."*

### THE MEMORY MIRROR PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 5
**Problem:** The mirrors show regrets. How do you pass?
**Solutions:**
1. Accept the memory: Pass through. Gain **Memory Wisdom**.
2. Break the mirror: Pass through. But the mirror SHATTERS. The mirror shatters and you feel WORSE.
3. Look away: You can't pass. You have to ACCEPT.
4. Have Mirror Greg with you: He takes your place. He accepts for you.
**Narrator on solving:** *"You accept. You accept the memory. You accept the regret. You accept the CHANDELIER. You accept the horse. You accept the CHICKEN. You accept EVERYTHING. And you feel... you feel LIGHTER."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE HALL
**Objective:** Navigate the Weeping Hall. Reach Floor 41.
**Steps:**
1. Enter the hall (Room 1).
2. Navigate the tear pool (Room 2).
3. Comfort the child (Room 3).
4. Defeat or comfort the Weeping One (Room 4).
5. Find the hidden passage (Room 5).
**Reward:** Access to Floor 41. Experience. Loot.

**Steps:**

2. Give bear, play music, or tell story.

**Reward:** **Child's Tear** (consumable, full heal). **Sadness Wisdom**.

### SIDE QUEST 2: SLIP AND SLIDE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Navigate the tear pool without falling.
**Steps:**
1. Enter Room 2.

**Reward:** **Tear Salve** (passive: +10% ice resistance). 15 gold.

**Reward:** The narrator is slightly nicer for the rest of the game. (No mechanical reward. But it's something.)

## BOSS: THE WEEPING ONE (Room 4)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 1
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Tear Spray:** 3 dmg + **Wet** (50% slip chance) for 2 turns.
- **Weeping Wail:** All in 2D radius: **Sadness** (–10% damage) for 2 fights.
- **Tear Shield:** Absorbs first 5 damage each turn.
- **Drowning Touch:** Target is **Drowning** in tears (2 dmg/turn until saved).
- **Memory Flood:** Target sees memories. **Hallucinating** for 2 turns.

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Tear Spray, Tear Shield. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-0% HP):** More aggressive. Weeping Wail every turn. Drowning Touch.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Use the dry platform: Safe from tears.
- Use fire: Evaporates tears. Reduces Weeping One's armor.
- Comfort the child first: Weeping One is drawn to the child. Easier fight.
- Accept the memory in Room 5: Weeping One is calmer.

**Drops:**
- **Weeping Crown** (Head: +10% ice damage, +5% all resistances)
- **Tear Vial** (consumable: heal 20 HP OR remove **Sadness**)
- 20 gold
- **Sadness Wisdom** (passive: +5% XP from emotional encounters)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Weeping One falls. The tears fall. The tears are STILL. The tears are FINALLY still. The Weeping One can REST. The Weeping One can FINALLY rest. And you... you are COVERED in tears. You are covered in tears and you are SAD. You are covered in tears and you are sad and you feel... you feel LIGHTER. You feel lighter because someone FINALLY stopped crying."*

## DESIGN NOTES

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 42 is easy-medium.** The Weeping One is not tough. The emotional weight is the challenge.
- **Expected deaths:** 0.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 1:** Teaches environmental awareness (crying walls).
- **Room 2:** Teaches hazard navigation (tear pool).
- **Room 3:** Teaches emotional engagement (crying child).
- **Room 4:** Teaches boss mechanics (Weeping One).
- **Room 5:** Teaches memory mechanics (mirrors).

### SECRETS
- **The Tear Waterfall:** In Room 2. Hidden area.
- **The Memory Book:** In Room 5. Lore.
- **The Dry Spot:** In Room 1. Safe rest.
- **The Hidden Passage:** Behind mirror. Progression.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 42 is SAD.** The narrator is... QUIET? The narrator is quiet and GENTLE. The narrator sees the sadness.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You're crying, Greg. You're CRYING. This is the SADDEST thing you've ever seen. And you once saw yourself in a MIRROR."
- **The narrator LOVES the Crying Child.** "The child is CRYING. The child has been crying for THREE HUNDRED YEARS. The child is still WAITING. The child is still waiting for someone to STAY. This is the SADDEST thing you've ever seen. And you once saw yourself in a mirror."
