## ENCOUNTERS

### DRINKING SONG (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Sitting at the bar.
**Description:** *"The ghosts SING. The ghosts sing and the ghosts are TERRIBLE. The ghosts sing and the ghosts are terrible and the ghosts are DEAD. The ghosts sing and the ghosts are terrible and the ghosts are dead and the ghosts are HAPPY."*
**Effect:** Join the song (Karaoke Bard: +10% XP. Others: +5% XP).
**Resolution:** Sing or listen.

### GHOST ALE (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Ordering a drink.
**Description:** *"The bartender serves you. The bartender serves you GHOST ALE. The bartender serves you ghost ale and the ghost ale is COLD. The bartender serves you ghost ale and the ghost ale is cold and the ghost ale is BEAUTIFUL."*
**Effect:** Drink: +20% damage 1 turn, –20% accuracy. Heal 10 HP.
**Resolution:** Drink or refuse.

### BARTENDER'S TALE (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Talking to the bartender.
**Description:** *"The bartender tells you. The bartender tells you about the PARAGON. The bartender tells you about the Paragon and the bartender KNOWS. The bartender tells you about the Paragon and the bartender knows and the bartender is DEAD."*
**Effect:** Lore. The Paragon was a child. The Paragon tried. The Paragon FAILED.
**Resolution:** Listen or ignore.

## BOSS: THE BARTENDER (Room 2, if you refuse to pay or insult the ale)

**HP:** 20
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 1
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Mug Smash:** 3 dmg, 25% **Dazed**.
- **Ale Splash:** 2 dmg + **Intoxicated** (–10% accuracy, 2 turns).
- **Bar Counter:** Summons 2× Ghost Drunks.
- **Last Call:** All in 2D radius: **Intoxicated**.

**Drops:**
