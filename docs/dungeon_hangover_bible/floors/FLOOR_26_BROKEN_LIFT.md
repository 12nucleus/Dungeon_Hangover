# FLOOR 26 — THE BROKEN LIFT

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** A vertical puzzle. A broken lift. Climbing inside climbing.
**Mood:** Vertical. Disorienting. You're climbing. You've been climbing. You'll keep climbing.
**Ambient:** Gears grinding. Chains. The creak of old machinery.
**Lighting:** Dim. Shafts of light from above. You can see UP.
**Soundtrack:** Gears. Chains. The creak of old machinery. The sound of HEIGHT.

## PUZZLES

### THE LIFT PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 2, 3
**Problem:** The lift is broken. How do you get up?
**Solutions:**
1. Climb the ledges (Athletics — intended).
2. Fix the lift (Plumber skill or wrench — alternative).
3. Use the Lift Key (from Room 2 — operates the lift).
4. Fall (you take damage but you're at the bottom. try again).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You fixed the lift. You fixed the lift with a WRENCH. You fixed the lift with a wrench and the lift WORKS. You fixed the lift with a wrench and the lift works and the lift goes UP. You fixed the lift with a wrench and the lift works and the lift goes up and you feel... you feel like an ENGINEER. You feel like an engineer and you are an ENGINEER. You are an engineer and you are in a LIFT SHAFT. This is the most ENGINEERING you've ever felt."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: CLIMB THE SHAFT
**Objective:** Climb the shaft. Reach Floor 25.
**Steps:** Enter → Climb → Deal with Operator → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE MECHANIC
**Giver:** The Lift Operator (Room 3).
**Objective:** Fix the lift. Show Plumber skills.
**Reward:** Use the lift. Skip the climb. **Wrench**.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE CLIMB
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Climb manually. Harder but rewarding.
**Reward:** **Climbing Wisdom** (+10% Athletics checks, permanent).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE DROP
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Fall down a shaft. You take damage but land in a secret area.
**Reward:** **Fall Wisdom** (+10% fall resistance, permanent). 15 gold.

## BOSS: THE LIFT OPERATOR (Room 3)

**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Slow
