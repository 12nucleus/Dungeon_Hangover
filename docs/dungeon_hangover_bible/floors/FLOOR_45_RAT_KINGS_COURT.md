# FLOOR 45 — THE RAT KING'S COURT

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Rat politics. A parody of feudal court. Absurd. Hilarious. Dangerous.
**Mood:** Slapstick. Absurd. The rats have built a civilization. It's TERRIBLE.
**Ambient:** Squeaking (conversational). Scratching. Occasional rat arguments.
**Lighting:** Dim. Candlelight (stolen candles). Throne room is brighter.
**Soundtrack:** Squeaky "music." Rat footsteps. Occasional rat drama.

## PUZZLES

### THE RAT KING'S TRIBUTE PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 5
**Problem:** The Rat King demands tribute. What do you offer?
**Solutions:**
1. Offer cheese from Room 3 (intended — he's pleased, peaceful passage).
2. Offer gold (he's okay with it, but not impressed).
3. Offer nothing (he attacks).
4. Offer the Rat Jester's bell (he's DELIGHTED — best outcome).
5. Attack him (default).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You offered cheese. The Rat King is PLEASED. The Rat King is pleased with your OFFERING. The Rat King is pleased with your CHEESE. You have made a rat HAPPY. You have made a rat king happy. This is your GREATEST achievement."*

### THE RAT TRIAL PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 4
**Problem:** A rat is on trial for being too small. What's your verdict?
**Solutions:**
1. Guilty: The rat is executed. The court is satisfied. You get **Rat Justice** (+5% damage to rats).
2. Not guilty: The rat is freed. The court is angry. But the rat follows you (minor companion).
3. No verdict: The court is confused. The trial continues. Forever.
**Narrator on solving:** *"You judged the rat. You judged a RAT. You are a JUDGE now. You are a half-naked judge in a rat court. This is your LIFE."*

## NPCs

### THE RAT KING (Room 5)
**Appearance:** A rat the size of a large dog. Wears a tiny crown. Sits on a cheese throne.
**Personality:** Vain. Petty. Absurd. But weirdly regal.

**Dialogue — First Meeting:**
> **Rat King:** "You dare enter my court? You dare stand before the KING? Kneel! Kneel, fleshy one! Kneel or be GNawed!"
> **Greg:** "You're a rat."
> **Rat King:** "I am THE rat. There is a difference. I have a CROWN. I have a COURT. I have SUBJECTS. What do you have?"
> **Greg:** "Underwear."
> **Rat King:** "...Fair point. But I have a crown."

**Dialogue — With Cheese Offering:**
> **Rat King:** "Is that... is that the cheese? The GOOD cheese? From the surface? *(sniffs)* It is! It is the cheese! You may pass! You may have safe passage! You may have the cheese! I mean — you may pass! Give me the cheese!"

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE COURT
**Objective:** Navigate the Rat King's court. Reach Floor 44.
**Steps:**
1. Enter the court (Room 1).
2. Navigate through the court (Rooms 2-4).
3. Deal with the Rat King (Room 5).
4. Find the exit (Room 8, through the treasury).
**Reward:** Access to Floor 44. Experience. Loot.

### SIDE QUEST 1: RATATOUILLE
**Giver:** The Rat King (Room 5).
**Objective:** Bring the Rat King a specific cheese from Room 3.
**Steps:**
1. Find the good cheese in Room 3.
2. Offer it to the Rat King.
**Reward:** Safe passage. **Rat Whisker** (+5% evasion). The Rat King's respect.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE USURPER
**Giver:** The Rat Noble (Room 4).
**Objective:** Help the Rat Noble overthrow the Rat King.
**Steps:**
1. Talk to the Rat Noble.
2. Agree to help (or refuse).
3. If help: Fight the Rat King together. If refuse: The Rat Noble is disappointed.
**Reward:** If helped: New Rat King. Different rewards. If refused: The Rat Noble gives you **Noble's Ring** (+5% gold find).

### SIDE QUEST 3: COURT JESTER
**Giver:** The Rat Jester (Room 7).
**Objective:** Laugh at the Rat Jester's joke.
**Steps:**
1. Talk to the Rat Jester.
2. Hear the joke.
3. Laugh (dialogue choice).
**Reward:** **Jester's Bell** (consumable, confuses all enemies 1 turn). The Rat Jester's friendship.

## BOSS: THE RAT KING (Room 5)

**HP:** 25
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 1 (from crown)
**Speed:** Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Gnaw:** 3 dmg, 25% **Bleeding** (2/turn, 3 turns).
- **Summon Guards:** Summons 2× Rat Guards (5 HP each, 2 dmg).
- **Cheese Throw:** 2 dmg + **Slippery** (50% fall chance) for 1 turn.
- **Royal Decree:** All rats on the floor are buffed (+10% damage) for 2 turns.
- **Frenzy:** Below 25% HP, attacks twice per turn.

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Gnaw, Cheese Throw. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-0% HP):** More aggressive. Summon Guards. Royal Decree.
**Phase 3 (25-0% HP):** Frenzy. Attacks twice per turn.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Eat the throne cheese: Heal 20 HP. But the Rat King is ENRAGED (+20% damage).
- Destroy the throne: Rat King loses crown. Loses armor.
- Offer cheese: Peaceful resolution.
- Offer bell: Best peaceful resolution.
- Have Sporefriend: The mushroom HATES rats. Sporefriend attacks the Rat King automatically.

**Drops:**
- **Rat Crown** (Head: +5% damage to beasts, +5% evasion)
- **Rat King's Sword** (weapon: 5 dmg, 10% **Bleeding**)
- 20 gold
- **Cheese Crown** (consumable: heal 20 HP + **Well Fed** 3 fights)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Rat King falls. The crown falls. The crown ROLLS. The crown rolls across the floor. The crown stops at your feet. You pick it up. You put it on. You are the Rat King now. You are the Rat King and you are in your UNDERWEAR. This is your kingdom. This is your CHEESE kingdom."*

## DESIGN NOTES

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 45 is easy-medium.** The Rat King is not tough. The social options make it easier.
- **The Rat Trial is the first real social puzzle.** Judge or don't judge?
- **Expected deaths:** 0.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 1:** Teaches social navigation (rats are neutral).
- **Room 2:** Teaches swarm combat (Rat Guards).
- **Room 3:** Teaches risk/reward (cheese).
- **Room 4:** Teaches social puzzle (Rat Trial).
- **Room 5:** Teaches boss mechanics (Rat King).
- **Room 6:** Teaches exploration (guards' quarters).
- **Room 7:** Teaches social engagement (Rat Jester).
- **Room 8:** Teaches trap awareness (treasury).

### SECRETS
- **The Rat Jester's Bell:** Best offering for the Rat King.
- **The Rat Map:** In Room 6. Navigation aid.
- **The Stolen Sword:** In Room 8. Good weapon.
- **The Joke Book:** In Room 7. Social tool.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 45 is the FUNNIEST floor.** The narrator is at its most sarcastic, most judgmental, most HILARIOUS.
- **The narrator LOVES the Rat King.** "You're fighting a RAT. You're fighting a KING-SIZED rat. This is the most Greg thing that has ever happened."
- **The narrator LOVES the Rat Jester.** "The rat jester tells JOKES. The rat jester tells jokes and NO ONE LAUGHS. This is the SADDEST thing you've ever seen. This is the FUNNIEST thing you've ever seen. This is BOTH."
