## PUZZLES

### THE UNION DISPUTE PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 2
**Problem:** The miners are disputing. Side with miners or foreman?
**Solutions:**
1. Side with miners: They give you **Miner's Pick** (weapon: 4 dmg, +10% vs stone).
2. Side with foreman: He gives you **Foreman's Badge** (+5% gold find).
3. Side with neither: Both are disappointed. No reward.
4. Have the Permit from the side quest: Both respect you. Best outcome.
**Narrator on solving:** *"You judged the dispute. You judged a DWARVEN dispute. You judged a dwarven dispute and you feel... you feel like a JUDGE. You feel like a judge and you are a JUDGE. You are a judge and you are in a DWARVEN office. This is the most BUREAUCRATIC you've ever felt."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE OFFICE
**Objective:** Navigate the mining office. Reach Floor 27.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Deal with Foreman → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE UNION DISPUTE
**Giver:** The Ghost Miners (Room 2).
**Objective:** Judge the dispute.
**Reward:** **Miner's Pick** OR **Foreman's Badge**.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE FOREMAN'S LEDGER
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Find the ledger. It shows what they were mining: the Lodestone shard.
**Reward:** Lore. Floor 27 clue.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE BUREAUCRAT
**Giver:** A ghost bureaucrat (Room 2).
**Objective:** Fill out forms. Do it: **Permit** (+5% shop discounts). Refuse: fight.
**Reward:** **Permit** OR 10 gold.

## BOSS: THE FOREMAN (Room 3)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Hammer Swing:** 4 dmg, 15% **Stun**.
- **Safety Violation:** 3 dmg + **Fined** (lose 5 gold).
- **Workplace Hazard:** All in 2D radius: **Prone**.
- **Overtime:** Summons 2× Ghost Miners (6 HP each, 3 dmg).
- **Paperwork:** Target is **Bogged Down** (–1 AP, 2 turns).

**Drops:**
- **Foreman's Hammer** (weapon: 5 dmg, 15% **Stun**)
- **Mining Permit** (passive: +5% shop discounts)
- 20 gold
- **Foreman's Badge** (passive: +5% gold find)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Foreman falls. The Foreman falls and the office is QUIET. The Foreman falls and the office is quiet and the paperwork is DONE. The Foreman falls and the office is quiet and the paperwork is done and the ghosts are... the ghosts are RELIEVED. The ghosts are relieved and the ghosts are FREE. The ghosts are relieved and the ghosts are free and the ghosts can REST."*
