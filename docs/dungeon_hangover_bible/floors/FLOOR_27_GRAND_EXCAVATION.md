## PUZZLES

### THE LODESTONE PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The Lodestone is guarded. How do you get it?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight the Guardian (hard).
2. Have the Architect's Compass from Floor 21 (Guardian steps aside — peaceful).
3. Take the Lodestone without fighting (Dexterity check — but the Guardian attacks).
4. Don't take it (Miner's Ghost gives you **Miner's Helmet** instead).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You took the Lodestone. You took the LODESTONE. You took the Lodestone and the Lodestone PULSES. You took the Lodestone and the Lodestone pulses and the Lodestone is WARM. You took the Lodestone and the Lodestone pulses and the Lodestone is warm and the Lodestone is ALIVE. You took the Lodestone and the Lodestone pulses and the Lodestone is warm and the Lodestone is alive and the Lodestone is SINGING."*

## BOSS: THE LODSTONE GUARDIAN (Room 3)

**HP:** 45
**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 4
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Crystal Slam:** 5 dmg, 20% **Prone**.
- **Regret Wave:** All in 2D radius: **Sadness** (–10% damage, 2 fights).
- **Crystal Shield:** Absorbs first 8 damage.
- **Summon Crystals:** Summons 2× Crystal Golems.
- **Regret Gaze:** Target sees their worst memory. **Frightened** 2 turns.

**Drops:**
- **Crystal Heart** (consumable: full heal + **Blessed** 3 fights)
- **Regret Crystal** (crafting: powerful items)
- 35 gold
- **Lodestone Shard** (critical item)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Guardian falls. The Guardian falls and the Lodestone DIMS. The Guardian falls and the Lodestone dims and the chamber is QUIET. The Guardian falls and the Lodestone dims and the chamber is quiet and you are... you are ALONE. You are alone and the Lodestone is YOURS. You are alone and the Lodestone is yours and the Lodestone is SINGING."*
