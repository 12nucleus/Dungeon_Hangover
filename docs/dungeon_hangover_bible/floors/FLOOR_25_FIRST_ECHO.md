# FLOOR 25 — THE FIRST ECHO

**Theme:** The Spire shows you yourself. You fight a mirror. The first real SELF confrontation.

**Ambient:** Your own heartbeat. Your own breathing. Your own voice (twisted).

**Soundtrack:** Your heartbeat. Your breathing. The sound of YOURSELF.

## ENCOUNTERS

## PUZZLES

### THE MIRROR PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The Mirror is a copy of you. How do you beat it?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight it directly (hard — it copies you).
2. Merge with it (dialogue — become one. +10% all stats, –10% max HP).
3. Talk to it (dialogue — it's lonely. It joins you briefly. Then shatters).
4. Break all the mirrors in Room 2 (it's weakened).
5. Accept yourself (the Mirror accepts you. Peaceful).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You merged with the Mirror. You merged with the Mirror and the Mirror is YOU. You merged with the Mirror and the mirror is you and you are the MIRROR. You merged with the Mirror and the mirror is you and you are the mirror and you are ONE. You merged with the Mirror and the mirror is you and you are the mirror and you are one and you are BETTER. You merged with the Mirror and the mirror is you and you are the mirror and you are one and you are better and you are SAD. You merged with the Mirror and the mirror is you and you are the mirror and you are one and you are better and you are sad because you LOST something. You lost something and you GAINED something. You lost something and you gained something and you are DIFFERENT."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE ECHO
**Objective:** Navigate the echo. Reach Floor 24.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Deal with Mirror → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: SURRENDER
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 3).
**Objective:** Let the Mirror win. It merges with you.
**Reward:** +10% all stats, –10% max HP. Different ending path.

### SIDE QUEST 2: DESTROY IT
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 3).
**Objective:** Kill the Mirror. Destroy it.
**Reward:** Normal rewards. **Mirror Shard**.

### SIDE QUEST 3: TALK TO IT
**Giver:** The Mirror (Room 3).
**Objective:** The Mirror speaks. It knows things.
**Reward:** Lore. Companion candidate (Mirror Greg).

## BOSS: THE MIRROR (Room 3)

**HP:** Same as your max HP
**Damage:** Same as your weapon
**Armor:** Same as yours
**Speed:** Same as yours

**Abilities:**
- Copies your last used skill.
- **Mirror Image:** Creates a copy (50% HP, 50% dmg).
- **Reflection:** Reflects 25% of damage back.
- **Self-Awareness:** Knows your build. Exploits weaknesses.
- **Perfect Strike:** Guaranteed crit every 3 turns.

**Drops:**
- **Mirror Shard** (weapon: 5 dmg, 25% copy skill)
- **Mirror Frame** (Shield: +5% evasion, reflects 10% damage)
- 25 gold
- **Self Portrait** (lore: the Spire knows you)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Mirror falls. The Mirror falls and the reflections SHATTER. The Mirror falls and the reflections shatter and the room is DARK. The Mirror falls and the reflections shatter and the room is dark and you are... you are ALONE. You are alone and the Mirror is GONE. You are alone and the Mirror is gone and you are REAL. You are alone and the Mirror is gone and you are real and you are YOURSELF."*
