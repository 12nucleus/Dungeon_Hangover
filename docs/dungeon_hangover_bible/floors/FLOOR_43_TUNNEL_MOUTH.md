# FLOOR 43 — THE TUNNEL MOUTH

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** A living tunnel. The dungeon's throat. You are swallowed.
**Mood:** Claustrophobic. Organic. Alive. You are INSIDE something.
**Ambient:** Breathing. Gulping. Peristalsis (the tunnel moves). Wet sounds.
**Lighting:** Bioluminescent flesh. Pink-red. Pulsing.
**Soundtrack:** Breathing. Heartbeat. Gulping. The sound of DIGESTION.

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — MOUTH ENTRY
**Dimensions:** 5m × 5m. The mouth. Lips. Tongue. You are INSIDE.
**Type:** Entry / Hub
**Contents:**
- A bonfire (lit — how? it's inside a MOUTH. it burns anyway.)
- The tongue (can be stood on — slippery, 50% fall chance)
- The uvula (can be grabbed — swing to Room 2)
- A swallowed adventurer (loot: 10 gold, a note: "It SWALLOWED me. It swallowed me WHOLE. I am inside. I am inside the DUNGEON.")
**Lighting:** Pink-red. Pulsing.
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"You are inside a MOUTH. You are inside the dungeon's MOUTH. The dungeon has TEETH. The dungeon has a TONGUE. The dungeon is ALIVE. The dungeon is alive and you are INSIDE it. This is the most DISGUSTING thing you've ever experienced. And you once ate a suspicious kebab."*

## PUZZLES

### THE TEETH GATE PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** Four giant teeth block the gate. How do you open it?
**Solutions:**
1. Pull all 4 teeth (Strength check each — intended).
2. Defeat the Tooth Guardian (gate opens).
3. Use the Stomach Key (from tooth cavity — bypass).
4. Use acid (from Room 4) to dissolve teeth (creative).
**Narrator on solving:** *"The teeth come out. The teeth come out with a SQUELCH. The gate opens. The gate opens with a GURGLE. You are through. You are through the TEETH. You are through the teeth and you are NOT chewed."*

### THE ACID PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 4
**Problem:** Treasure is in acid. How do you get it?
**Solutions:**
1. Grab it quickly (take 4 dmg, get treasure).
2. Use a tool (wrench, plunger) to fish it out.
3. Use a body (throw an enemy in — they die, treasure floats).
4. Leave it (it's not worth it).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You grab the treasure. You grab the treasure in ACID. Your hand BURNS. Your hand is BURNING. But you have the TREASURE. You have the treasure and you have a BURN. This is a FAIR trade."*

## BOSS: THE TUNNEL MOUTH (Room 4)

**HP:** 40
**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 2 (from stomach lining)
**Speed:** Slow (acts every other turn, but hits hard)

**Abilities:**
- **Acid Spray:** 5 dmg in cone. 25% **Acid Burn** (2 dmg/turn, 3 turns).
- **Swallow:** Target is **Swallowed** (3 dmg/turn, cannot act, must be cut out).
- **Peristalsis:** All in room are pushed 1D toward acid pools.
- **Digest:** Heals 5 HP per turn while someone is swallowed.
- **Gurgle:** All in 2D radius: **Nauseated** for 2 turns.

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Acid Spray, Peristalsis. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-0% HP):** More aggressive. Swallow. Digest.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Drop toxic item: Tunnel spits you out. Boss takes 20 dmg.
- Use fire: 2x damage. Tunnel hates fire.
- Use the stomach acid geyser: Lure boss into geyser path.
- Destroy stomach lining: Reduces armor.

**Drops:**
- **Stomach Acid Vial** (consumable: 8 dmg, ignores armor, corrosive)
- **Digestive Ring** (Ring: +5% damage, +5% poison resistance)
- 30 gold
- **Tongue Whip** (weapon: 5 dmg, 15% **Bound**)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Tunnel Mouth dies. The Tunnel Mouth goes LIMP. The tunnel is no longer a tunnel. The tunnel is just a TUNNEL. The tunnel is just a tunnel and you are just a PERSON. You are just a person who was inside a MOUTH. You are just a person who was inside a mouth and now you are OUTSIDE the mouth. You are outside the mouth and you are ALIVE."*

## DESIGN NOTES

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 43 is medium-hard.** The Tunnel Mouth is tough. The environment is hostile.
- **Fire makes it easy.** Use fire. Use fire and win.
- **Expected deaths:** 1-2.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 1:** Teaches environmental awareness (living tunnel).
- **Room 2:** Teaches hazard navigation (peristalsis, mucus).
- **Room 3:** Teaches puzzle-solving (teeth gate).
- **Room 4:** Teaches boss mechanics (Tunnel Mouth).
- **Room 5:** Teaches escape mechanics (peristalsis, bile).

### SECRETS
- **The Swallowed Sword:** In Room 2. Good weapon.
- **The Stomach Key:** In Room 3. Puzzle solution.
- **The Swallowed Chest:** In Room 5. Loot.
- **The Tonsil Room:** Hidden in Room 2. Loot.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 43 is disgusting.** The narrator is... DISGUSTED? The narrator sees the wrongness.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You're inside a MOUTH, Greg. You're inside a MOUTH. This is the most DISGUSTING thing you've ever experienced. And you once ate a suspicious KEBAB."
- **The narrator LOVES the peristalsis.** "The tunnel MOVES. The tunnel moves you. The tunnel moves you whether you LIKE it or not. This is the most HUMILIATING thing you've ever experienced. And you once proposed to a CHANDELIER."
