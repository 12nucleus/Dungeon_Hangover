# FLOOR 47 — THE BONE YARD

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** A field of bones. Ancient burial ground. The dead don't rest.
**Mood:** Eerie. Quiet. The weight of death. Something is watching.
**Ambient:** Wind through bones (whistling). Occasional bone clatter. Distant howling.
**Lighting:** Pale moonlight from cracks above. Bones gleam. Ghostly blue lights (will-o-wisps).
**Soundtrack:** Low wind. Bone chimes. A heartbeat-like thump from below.

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY FIELD
**Dimensions:** 8m × 6m. Open field. Bones scattered everywhere.
**Type:** Safe zone / Hub
**Contents:**
- A bonfire (lit — someone was here)
- A bone pile (search: 5 gold, a finger bone, a note: "The bones remember. The bones ALWAYS remember.")
- A will-o-wisp (floats — follows you if you get close, provides light)

**Lighting:** Pale moonlight. Blue wisps.
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"Bones. Everywhere. Bones under your feet. Bones in the walls. Bones in the CEILING. You are walking on the DEAD. The dead don't mind. The dead are USED to it."*

## PUZZLES

### THE BONE RAT KING PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 4
**Problem:** The Bone Rat King is tough. How do you beat him?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight him directly (hard).
2. Place the Hermit's Finger on the bone pedestal (pacifies — peaceful).
3. Destroy the throne (removes his crown, weakens him).
4. Use fire (prevents reassembly of all bone enemies).
5. Have the Bone Crown from Floor 50's side quest (he respects you).
**Narrator on solving:** *"The Bone Rat King sits. The Bone Rat King REMEMBERS. The Bone Rat King remembers when he was SMALL. The Bone Rat King remembers when he was ALIVE."*

### THE GHOST CHILD PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The Ghost Child is waiting. Waiting for what?
**Solutions:**
1. Play with the child (use the wooden sword — play fight).
2. Give the child a gift (any item).
3. Talk to the child (dialogue — tell a story).
4. Leave the child alone (sad — child cries).
**Narrator on solving:** *"The child smiles. The child LAUGHS. The child is HAPPY. The child is happy for the first time in THREE HUNDRED YEARS. The child can REST."*

## BOSS: THE BONE RAT KING (Room 4)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 2 (from bone crown)
**Speed:** Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Bone Gnaw:** 3 dmg, 25% **Bleeding** (2/turn, 3 turns).
- **Summon Bones:** Summons 2× Bone Rats (8 HP each, reassemble once).
- **Reassemble:** If killed, reassembles after 2 turns (unless bones are burned).
- **Bone Shield:** +3 armor for 2 turns (3-turn cooldown).
- **Skeleton March:** All bone enemies attack simultaneously.

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Bone Gnaw, Summon Bones. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-0% HP):** More aggressive. Bone Shield every turn. Skeleton March at 25%.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Destroy the throne (attack it: 20 HP): Removes his crown. Loses armor.
- Use fire: Prevents reassembly. Deals 2x damage.
- Place Hermit's Finger on pedestal: Pacifies. Peaceful resolution.
- Have Bone Crown: He respects you. Easier fight.

**Drops:**
- **Bone Crown** (Head: +10% damage to beasts, +5% physical resistance)
- **Bone Rat Fang** (weapon: 4 dmg, 25% **Bleeding**)
- 20 gold
- **Bone Dust** (consumable: +10% physical resistance for 3 fights)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Bone Rat King falls. The bones fall. The bones are QUIET. The bones are FINALLY quiet. The bones can REST. The bones can FINALLY rest."*

## DESIGN NOTES

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 47 is medium difficulty.** The Bone Rat King is tough but fair.
- **Many paths.** You can avoid most combat.
- **Expected deaths:** 0-1.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 1:** Teaches environmental awareness (bone fence, will-o-wisp).
- **Room 2:** Teaches NPC interaction (Ghost Adventurer).
- **Room 3:** Teaches emotional engagement (Ghost Child).
- **Room 4:** Teaches boss mechanics (Bone Rat King).
- **Room 5:** Teaches exploration (buried chest).

### SECRETS
- **The Ghost Adventurer's Map:** Shows Floor 40's market.
- **The Buried Chest:** In Room 5. 30 gold, Bone Crown.
- **The Locket:** In Room 3. Lore.
- **The Will-o-Wisp:** In Room 1. Risk/reward.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 47 is eerie.** The narrator is quieter. The narrator is... UNCOMFORTABLE? The narrator sees the death.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You're walking on BONES, Greg. You're walking on the DEAD. This is DISRESPECTFUL. This is... this is actually kind of IMPRESSIVE. You have no respect for ANYTHING."
- **The narrator LOVES the Ghost Child.** "The child is DEAD. The child has been dead for THREE HUNDRED YEARS. The child is still WAITING. The child is still waiting for someone to PLAY. This is the SADDEST thing you've ever seen. And you once saw yourself in a mirror."
