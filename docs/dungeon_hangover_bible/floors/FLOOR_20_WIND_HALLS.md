# FLOOR 20 — THE WIND HALLS

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** You hear the surface. First Act IV floor. The air changes. The WIND blows.
**Mood:** Hopeful. The surface is CLOSE. You can hear it. You can SMELL it.
**Ambient:** Wind. The sound of the surface. Birds (distant). Rain (distant).
**Lighting:** Grey. Natural light. The first NATURAL light in 30 floors.
**Soundtrack:** Wind. Birds. Rain. The sound of the SURFACE.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE HALLS
**Objective:** Navigate the wind halls. Reach Floor 19.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Listen → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE WIND'S MESSAGE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** The wind carries voices. You hear someone calling your name.
**Reward:** Lore. Hope. +5% all stats.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE KITE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 3).
**Objective:** A kite is stuck in the wind. Retrieve it.
**Reward:** **Kite String** (+5% ranged damage). Wind carries you past Floor 19.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE ECHO OF THE TAVERN
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** You hear The Murky Flagon. The narrator is quiet.
**Reward:** Lore. Emotional. The narrator is quiet for a long time.

## BOSS: THE WIND (Room 2, if you resist the wind)

**HP:** 25
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 1
**Speed:** Very Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Wind Gust:** 3 dmg + pushed 2D.
- **Wind Vortex:** All in 2D radius: pulled 1D toward center.
- **Wind Shield:** Absorbs first 3 damage.
- **Wind Chime:** Plays the Paragon's lullaby. All are **Calmed** 2 turns.

**Drops:**
- **Wind Chime** (passive: +10% wind resistance)
- **Wind Cloak** (Body: +10% evasion, immune to push)
- 20 gold
- **Surface Map** (shows the way to the surface)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Wind falls. The Wind falls and the hall is STILL. The Wind falls and the hall is still and the air is CALM. The Wind falls and the hall is still and the air is calm and you are... you are at PEACE. You are at peace and you are CALM. You are at peace and you are calm and you are READY. You are at peace and you are calm and you are ready for what comes NEXT."*
