# FLOOR 34 — THE TOLL BRIDGE

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** A troll. A bridge. A compliment required. The troll just wants kindness.
**Mood:** Sad. The troll is lonely. The troll just wants someone to say something nice.
**Ambient:** Wind. Water below. The troll humming.
**Lighting:** Grey. Overcast. A crack in the ceiling shows SKY (first time in 16 floors).
**Soundtrack:** Wind. Water. The troll's humming (off-key, like father like son).

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY
**Type:** Hub
**Contents:** Bonfire. A sign ("TOLL: ONE COMPLIMENT"). A view of the bridge.
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A bridge. A TROLL bridge. A troll bridge and the troll wants TOLL. A troll bridge and the troll wants toll and the toll is... the toll is a COMPLIMENT. The toll is a compliment and you have NO compliments. You have no compliments and you have no CHARM. You have no compliments and you have no charm and you have no PANTS."*

---

### ROOM 2 — BRIDGE
**Type:** Hazard + Social
**Contents:**
- A long bridge (10m). Over a chasm. Windy.
- A troll (blocking the way)
- A compliment booth (empty — the troll IS the booth)
- A view (look down: see the chasm. look up: see SKY.)
**Enemies:** The troll (if you insult him).
**Narrator on entry:** *"The bridge. The TROLL bridge. The bridge is LONG. The bridge is long and it's WINDY. The bridge is long and it's windy and it's SCARY. The bridge is long and it's windy and it's scary and there's a TROLL. The bridge is long and it's windy and it's scary and there's a troll and the troll is BIG."*

---

### ROOM 3 — TROLL LAIR
**Type:** Boss arena
**Contents:**
- The Troll (Boss — see Boss section)
- A cave (the troll's home)
- A collection of things left by travelers (loot: 30 gold, **Troll's Tooth**)
- A diary (the troll's diary — sad. so sad.)
**Enemies:** The Troll.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The troll's lair. The TROLL's lair. The troll's lair is SAD. The troll's lair is sad and it's LONELY. The troll's lair is sad and it's lonely and it's FULL of things. The troll's lair is sad and it's lonely and it's full of things that TRAVELERS left. The troll's lair is sad and it's lonely and it's full of things that travelers left and the troll KEPT them. The troll's lair is sad and it's lonely and it's full of things that travelers left and the troll kept them because the troll is LONELY."*

---

### ROOM 4 — UNDER BRIDGE
**Type:** Secret + Loot
**Contents:**
- Under the bridge (climb down: Athletics)
- A troll's treasure (hidden: 40 gold, **Troll's Tear-Stained Pebble**)
- A troll's grave (the troll's parent)
- A passage (leads to Floor 33)
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"Under the bridge. Under the TROLL bridge. Under the bridge is DARK. Under the bridge is dark and it's SECRET. Under the bridge is dark and it's secret and there's TREASURE. Under the bridge is dark and it's secret and there's treasure and the treasure is SAD. Under the bridge is dark and it's secret and there's treasure and the treasure is sad because the treasure is all the troll HAS."*

## ENCOUNTERS

### TOLL DEMAND (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Approaching the troll.
**Description:** *"The troll blocks your path. The troll wants TOLL. The troll wants toll and the toll is a COMPLIMENT."*
**Effect:** Give a compliment or fight.
**Resolution:** Multiple options.

### BRIDGE WIND (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Crossing the bridge.
**Description:** *"The wind BLOWS. The wind blows HARD. The wind blows hard and you're on a BRIDGE. The wind blows hard and you're on a bridge and you're going to FALL."*
**Effect:** Athletics check or fall (5 dmg).
**Resolution:** Hold on or fall.

### TROLL'S DIARY (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Reading the diary.
**Description:** *"The troll's diary. The troll's diary is SAD. The troll's diary is sad and it's LONELY. The troll's diary is sad and it's lonely and it's... it's HEARTBREAKING."*
**Effect:** Understand the troll. Easier social resolution.
**Resolution:** Read or don't read.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: CROSS THE BRIDGE
**Objective:** Cross the bridge. Reach Floor 33.
**Steps:** Enter → Cross bridge → Deal with troll → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE COMPLIMENT
**Giver:** The Troll (Room 2).
**Objective:** Give the troll a genuine compliment.
**Reward:** Safe passage. **Troll's Tear-Stained Pebble** (+5% ice damage).

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE INSULT
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Insult the troll.
**Reward:** Fight the troll. **Troll's Tooth** (+5% physical damage).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE RIDDLE
**Giver:** The Troll (Room 2).
**Objective:** Answer the troll's riddle.
**Reward:** Correct: compliment + pass. Wrong: fight.

## BOSS: THE TOLL TROLL (Room 3)

**HP:** 40
**Damage:** 6
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Club Smash:** 6 dmg, 25% **Stun**.
- **Throw Boulder:** 5 dmg, 15% **Prone**.
- **Tantrum:** All in 2D radius: **Frightened** 2 turns.
- **Bridge Slam:** Shakes bridge. All on bridge: Athletics check or fall.

**Drops:**
- **Troll's Club** (weapon: 7 dmg, 20% **Stun**)
- **Troll's Tooth** (passive: +5% physical damage)
- 30 gold
- **Troll's Tear-Stained Pebble** (passive: +5% ice damage)

**Narrator on death:** *"The troll falls. The troll falls and the bridge is QUIET. The troll falls and the bridge is quiet and the troll is... the troll is SAD. The troll is sad and the troll is LONELY. The troll is sad and the troll is lonely and the troll just wanted someone to say something NICE. The troll just wanted someone to say something nice and you... you HIT the troll. You hit the troll and the troll is DEAD and you feel... you feel TERRIBLE."*

## DESIGN NOTES

- **Floor 34 is EASY.** The troll is tough but the compliment makes it trivial.
- **Teaches:** Social mechanics. Kindness. Consequence.
- **Secrets:** The Under Bridge in Room 4. The Troll's Diary.
- **Narrator:** "The troll wants a COMPLIMENT, Greg. The troll wants a COMPLIMENT. The troll wants a compliment and you have NO idea what to say. You have no idea what to say and you're TERRIBLE at compliments. You're terrible at compliments and you once told a chandelier you LOVED it. You once told a chandelier you loved it and the chandelier SAID YES. You once told a chandelier you loved it and the chandelier said yes and that was the best compliment you ever GAVE. Give the troll a compliment, Greg. Give the troll a compliment and MEAN it."
