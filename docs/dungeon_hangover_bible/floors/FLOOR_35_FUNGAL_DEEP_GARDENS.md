**Theme:** Deep fungal forests. The mushroom companion evolves or dies here.
**Mood:** Beautiful but deadly. The fungi are aware. The fungi are WATCHING.
**Ambient:** Spore release. Mushroom pops. A deep, rhythmic pulse.

**Soundtrack:** Deep drone. Spore pops. A fungal heartbeat.

## COMPANION EVOLUTION (Sporefriend)

**This is the critical floor for Sporefriend.**

### If you have Sporefriend AND side with the Spore King:
- Sporefriend absorbs the Spore King.
- Evolves into **Sporelord** (HP 30, Damage 5, Spore Cloud AoE).
- Stays with you until Floor 25 (sacrifices itself to save you from the Mirror).
- Dies. The narrator is quiet for a long time.

### If you have Sporefriend AND fight the Spore King:
- Sporefriend stays small.
- Stays with you until Floor 33 (merges with the Ghost Chef's soup).
- The soup becomes permanent (you can craft it forever).
- Sporefriend is gone. The chef says: "The little one... it wanted to be part of something."

### If you don't have Sporefriend:
- The Spore King fight is harder (no distraction).
- No companion evolution.
- But you get the **Spore Crown** (better loot).

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE GARDENS
**Objective:** Navigate the gardens. Reach Floor 34.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Deal with Spore King → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: EVOLUTION
**Giver:** The Spore King (Room 4).
**Objective:** Side with the Spore King. Let Sporefriend evolve.
**Reward:** **Sporelord** companion (powerful).

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE SPORE CHILD
**Giver:** The Spore Child (Room 3).
**Objective:** Comfort the Spore Child.
**Reward:** **Spore Tear** (full heal). **Mushroom Wisdom**.

### SIDE QUEST 3: SPORE SACRIFICE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 5).
**Objective:** Let Sporefriend die to save yourself from a spore cloud.
**Reward:** You live. Sporefriend dies. The narrator is quiet.

## BOSS: THE SPORE KING (Room 4)

**HP:** 45
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 3
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Spore Burst:** 4 dmg, 25% **Poisoned**.
- **Summon Guardians:** Summons 2× Fungal Guardians.
- **Mycelial Network:** Heals 4 HP/turn on fungal ground.
- **Root Grab:** **Bound** 2 turns, 2 dmg/turn.
- **Fungal Storm:** All in 3D radius: 3 dmg + **Poisoned**.

**Drops:**
- **Spore Crown** (Head: +10% nature damage, +10% poison resist)
- **Mycelial Staff** (weapon: 6 dmg, heals 1 dmg/hit)
- 35 gold
- **Elder Mushroom** (crafting: powerful potions)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Spore King falls. The garden FALLS. The garden falls and the fungi DIM. The garden falls and the fungi dim and the garden is QUIET. The garden falls and the fungi dim and the garden is quiet and the garden is SAD."*
