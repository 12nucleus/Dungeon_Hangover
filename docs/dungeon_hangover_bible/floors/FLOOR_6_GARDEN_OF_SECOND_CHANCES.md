## PUZZLES

### THE GARDEN PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The Gardener offers to fix everything. What do you say?
**Solutions:**
1. Say "fix everything": The Gardener fixes everything. But you lose something. (The Gardener takes a memory.)
2. Say "nothing — I'm okay": The Gardener is pleased. You gain **Gardener's Blessing** (+10% all stats, permanent).
3. Say "fix one thing": The Gardener fixes one thing. You choose.
4. Say "leave it broken": The Gardener is VERY pleased. You gain **Gardener's Blessing** AND **Acceptance Wisdom**.
**Narrator on solving:** *"You say you're okay. You say you're okay and the Gardener is PLEASED. You say you're okay and the Gardener is pleased and the Gardener gives you a BLESSING. You say you're okay and the Gardener is pleased and the Gardener gives you a blessing and the blessing is... the blessing is ACCEPTANCE. You say you're okay and the Gardener is pleased and the Gardener gives you a blessing and the blessing is acceptance and the acceptance is BEAUTIFUL."*
