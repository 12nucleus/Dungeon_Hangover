# FLOOR 7 — THE FLOOR OF REGRETS

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Your specific regrets, manifested. The things you did. The things you didn't do.
**Mood:** Heavy. Personal. This floor is DIFFERENT for every player. This floor is YOURS.
**Ambient:** Your own voice (regretting). Your own heartbeat. The echo of your choices.
**Lighting:** Dark. Personal. The regrets GLOW.
**Soundtrack:** Your own voice. Your own heartbeat. The echo of your choices.

## PUZZLES

### THE REGRET PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** Your Regret is you. How do you beat it?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight it directly (hard — it's you).
2. Say "I forgive myself" (the Regret dissolves — peaceful).
3. Say "I'm sorry" (the Regret forgives you — peaceful).
4. Accept the regret (the Regret merges with you — +10% all stats, –10% max HP).
5. Have Mirror Greg with you (he fights the Regret for you — but he dies).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You forgive yourself. You forgive yourself and the Regret DISSOLVES. You forgive yourself and the Regret dissolves and the Regret is GONE. You forgive yourself and the Regret dissolves and the Regret is gone and you are... you are FREE. You are free and you are LIGHT. You are free and you are light and you are YOURSELF. You are free and you are light and you are yourself and you are ENOUGH."*

## BOSS: YOUR REGRET (Room 3)

**HP:** Same as your max HP
**Damage:** Same as your weapon
**Armor:** Same as yours
**Speed:** Same as you

**Abilities:**
- **Regret Strike:** Same as your weapon + **Sadness** (–10% damage, 2 fights).
- **Swallowed Words:** All the things you didn't say. 3 dmg + **Silenced** 2 turns.
- **What If:** Target sees what could have been. **Frightened** 3 turns.
- **Mirror Image:** Creates a regret copy (50% HP, 50% dmg).
- **Self-Loathing:** Reflects 25% of damage back.

**Drops:**
- **Regret Shard** (weapon: 6 dmg, 20% **Sadness**)
- **Forgiveness** (consumable: remove all guilt, full heal)
- 25 gold
- **Self-Forgiveness** (passive: +10% all resistances)

**Narrator on death:** *"Your Regret falls. Your Regret falls and the tavern is QUIET. Your Regret falls and the tavern is quiet and the chandelier is STILL. Your Regret falls and the tavern is quiet and the chandelier is still and you are... you are FREE. You are free and you are LIGHT. You are free and you are light and you are YOURSELF. You are free and you are light and you are yourself and you are ENOUGH. You are free and you are light and you are yourself and you are enough and you can FORGIVE yourself. You are free and you are light and you are yourself and you are enough and you can forgive yourself and you DO forgive yourself. You are free and you are light and you are yourself and you are enough and you can forgive yourself and you do forgive yourself and you are AT PEACE."*
