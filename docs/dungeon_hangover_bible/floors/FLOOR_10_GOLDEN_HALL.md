## ENCOUNTERS

### GOLD TEMPTATION (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Taking gold.
**Description:** *"You take the gold. You take the gold and the gold is BEAUTIFUL. You take the gold and the gold is beautiful and the gold is CURSED. You take the gold and the gold is beautiful and the gold is cursed and the gold wants you to STAY."*
**Effect:** **Cursed Gold** (–20% loot quality). Can donate to ghost on Floor 47.
**Resolution:** Take or leave.

### GOLDEN FEAST (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Eating the feast.
**Description:** *"You eat the feast. You eat the feast and the feast is BEAUTIFUL. You eat the feast and the feast is beautiful and the feast is INTOXICATING. You eat the feast and the feast is beautiful and the feast is intoxicating and the feast wants you to STAY."*
**Effect:** Heal 30 HP. **Intoxicated** 3 turns.
**Resolution:** Eat or refuse.

### GOLDEN BED (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Sleeping in the bed.
**Description:** *"You sleep. You sleep in the GOLDEN bed. You sleep in the golden bed and the golden bed is COMFORTABLE. You sleep in the golden bed and the golden bed is comfortable and the golden bed is ETERNAL. You sleep in the golden bed and the golden bed is comfortable and the golden bed is eternal and the golden bed wants you to STAY."*
**Effect:** Full heal. **Asleep** 2 turns. Vulnerable.
**Resolution:** Sleep or refuse.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE HALL
**Objective:** Navigate the golden hall. Reach Floor 9.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Defeat Knight → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE GOLD
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Take the gold. It's cursed.
**Reward:** Gold (with curse). Or: donate to ghost on Floor 47.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE FEAST
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Eat the feast. It's intoxicating.
**Reward:** Heal 30 HP. **Intoxicated** 3 turns.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE GOLDEN KNIGHT
**Giver:** The Golden Knight (Room 3).
**Objective:** The knight is hollow. Fill him with real gold.
**Reward:** Peaceful resolution. **Golden Heart**.

## BOSS: THE GOLDEN KNIGHT (Room 3)

**HP:** 45
**Damage:** 6
**Armor:** 5
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Golden Sword:** 6 dmg, 15% **Bleeding**.
- **Golden Shield:** Absorbs first 8 damage.
- **Summon Guards:** Summons 2× Golden Guards.
- **Gilded Cage:** Target is **Bound** 2 turns.
- **Hollow Strike:** 5 dmg + **Hollow** (–10% max HP, permanent this fight).

**Drops:**
- **Golden Sword** (weapon: 8 dmg, 15% **Bleeding**)
- **Golden Crown** (Head: +10% all stats, –10% max HP)
- 40 gold
- **Golden Heart** (consumable: full heal + **Blessed** 5 fights)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Golden Knight falls. The Golden Knight falls and the gold is TARNISHED. The Golden Knight falls and the gold is tarnished and the hall is DARK. The Golden Knight falls and the gold is tarnished and the hall is dark and you are... you are PAST. You are past and you are THROUGH. You are past and you are through and you are on Floor 9."*
