# FLOOR 46 — THE DROWNED CHAPEL

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Flooded chapel. Holy water. Drowned faithful. Sacred things profaned.
**Mood:** Sacred but wrong. Holy but drowned. Beauty corrupted.
**Ambient:** Water dripping. Hymns (distant, underwater). Creaking wood.
**Lighting:** Pale blue light through stained glass (underwater). Candles (some still burning — underwater candles, impossible).
**Soundtrack:** Drowned hymns. Water echoes. A bell (tolling, muffled).

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY ALTAR
**Dimensions:** 6m × 5m. The altar. Flooded (ankle-deep).
**Type:** Safe zone / Hub
**Contents:**
- A bonfire (lit — how? underwater? it shouldn't work. It works anyway.)

**Lighting:** Blue through stained glass.
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A chapel. A DROWNED chapel. The water is ankle-deep. The water is COLD. The water is CLEAR. You can see the floor. You can see the PEWS. You can see the SKELETONS sitting in the pews. They are PRAYING. They are still PRAYING."*

## PUZZLES

### THE DROWNING GOBLIN PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 4
**Problem:** A goblin is drowning in the baptismal font. Save him or loot him?
**Solutions:**
1. Save him: He's grateful. He becomes a recurring NPC (Floor 40 discounts). But he's also a GOBLIN. He might steal from you.
2. Loot him: He drowns. You get his gold (15 gold). But the goblins on Floor 40 are hostile.
3. Ignore him: He drowns. Nothing happens. But you feel BAD.
4. Push him under: You're a MONSTER. But you get his gold AND he can't steal from you.
**Narrator on saving:** *"You saved the goblin. The goblin is GRATEFUL. The goblin is WET. The goblin is SHIVERING. The goblin follows you. The goblin is your FRIEND. The goblin is your WET, SHIVERING friend."*
**Narrator on looting:** *"The goblin drowns. You take the gold. You take the WET gold. You are a MONSTER. You are a WET, gold-taking monster."*

### THE CONFESSION PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The ghost priest wants you to confess. What do you say?
**Solutions:**
1. Confess your real regrets (dialogue tree): He gives you **Absolution** (+10% all resistances, 3 fights).
2. Confess fake sins: He knows. He's DISAPPOINTED. No reward.
3. Confess nothing: He waits. He'll wait FOREVER.
4. Attack him: He fights. He's a GHOST PRIEST. He's tougher than he looks.
**Narrator on confessing:** *"You confess. You confess your SINS. You confess the chandelier. You confess the horse. You confess the CHICKEN. The priest listens. The priest UNDERSTANDS. The priest forgives you. The priest gives you ABSOLUTION."*

## BOSS: THE DROWNED PRIEST (Room 5)

**HP:** 35
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2 (from holy vestments)
**Speed:** Slow (acts every other turn, but hits hard)

**Abilities:**
- **Holy Water Spray:** 4 dmg + **Wet** (50% slip chance) for 2 turns.
- **Drowning Touch:** Target is **Drowning** (3 dmg/turn until saved or priest dies).
- **Hymn of Sorrow:** All in 2D radius: **Frightened** for 2 turns.
- **Prayer:** Heals 5 HP per turn while not attacked.
- **Blessed Ground:** Creates holy ground (undead can't approach, 2D radius, 2 turns).

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Holy Water Spray, Prayer. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-0% HP):** More aggressive. Drowning Touch every turn. Hymn of Sorrow every other turn.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Light holy candles: +5% damage to the priest each (max +20%).
- Use the altar: Holy ground. Priest can't approach.
- Use fire: Burns away holy water. Reduces his armor.
- Have Bleddy (saved goblin): He distracts the priest for 1 turn.

**Drops:**
- **Priest's Robes** (Body: +10% all resistances, +5% healing received)
- **Holy Water Vial** (consumable: heals 30 HP OR deals 10 dmg to undead)
- 25 gold
- **Drowned Bible** (consumable: read to calm all undead for 3 turns)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Drowned Priest falls. The water falls. the water is STILL. the water is FINALLY still. the priest can REST. the priest can FINALLY rest."*

## DESIGN NOTES

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 46 is medium difficulty.** The Drowned Priest is tough but fair.
- **The Drowning Goblin is the first real moral choice.** Save or loot? Both have consequences.
- **Expected deaths:** 0-1.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 1:** Teaches holy mechanics (holy water, blessed ground).
- **Room 2:** Teaches undead mechanics (Drowned Faithful).
- **Room 3:** Teaches NPC interaction (confession).
- **Room 4:** Teaches moral choice (drowning goblin).
- **Room 5:** Teaches boss mechanics (Drowned Priest).
- **Room 6:** Teaches environmental awareness (hymn, choir loft).

### SECRETS
- **The Submerged Door:** Behind altar. Progression.
- **The Secret Compartment:** Behind confessional screen. Loot.
- **The Drained Font:** Opens hidden chamber.
- **The Baptismal Record:** Lore. Floor 42 connection.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 46 is sacred.** The narrator is... RESPECTFUL? The narrator sees the holiness.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You're in a CHAPEL, Greg. You're in a chapel in your UNDERWEAR. This is DISRESPECTFUL. This is... this is actually kind of ON BRAND for you."
- **The narrator LOVES the Drowning Goblin choice.** "You saved the GOBLIN. You saved a WET, DROWNING goblin. You could have taken his GOLD. You could have let him DROWN. But you SAVED him. You beautiful, half-naked idiot. You saved the goblin."
