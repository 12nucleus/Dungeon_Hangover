# FLOOR 13 — THE FLOOR OF FACES

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Every NPC you've met or failed to save stands in a long hallway. They watch. They don't speak.
**Mood:** Haunting. The weight of every choice. The weight of every FAILURE.
**Ambient:** Silence. Footsteps. Your own heartbeat.
**Lighting:** Dim. Pale. The faces are LIT. The rest is DARK.
**Soundtrack:** Silence. Footsteps. Your heartbeat.

## ENCOUNTERS

### THE FACES SPEAK (Room 2)
**Trigger:** If you saved them.
**Description:** *"The faces SPEAK. The faces speak and the faces say... the faces say THANK YOU. The faces speak and the faces say thank you and the faces are SMILING. The faces speak and the faces say thank you and the faces are smiling and the faces are AT PEACE."*
**Effect:** Emotional. Gratitude. +5% all stats for 3 fights.
**Resolution:** Accept or deny.

### THE FACES LOOK AWAY (Room 3)
**Trigger:** If you failed them.
**Description:** *"The faces LOOK AWAY. The faces look away and the faces are SAD. The faces look away and the faces are sad and the faces are DISAPPOINTED. The faces look away and the faces are sad and the faces are disappointed and the faces are NOT ANGRY. The faces look away and the faces are sad and the faces are disappointed and the faces are not angry and that's WORSE."*
**Effect:** Emotional. Sadness. –5% all stats for 3 fights.
**Resolution:** Accept or deny.

### THE EMPTY SPACES (Room 4)
**Trigger:** Seeing the empty spaces.
**Description:** *"Empty spaces. EMPTY spaces. The empty spaces where faces SHOULD be. The empty spaces where faces should be and the empty spaces are YOU. The empty spaces where faces should be and the empty spaces are you and the empty spaces are the ones you DIDN'T MEET."*
**Effect:** Emotional. Regret. Lore.
**Resolution:** Accept or deny.

### THE NEW FACE (Room 4)
**Trigger:** If you destroyed your statue on Floor 44.
**Description:** *"A face. A NEW face. The new face is YOU. The new face is you and the new face is WAX. The new face is you and the new face is wax and the new face is the statue you DESTROYED. The new face is you and the new face is wax and the new face is the statue you destroyed and the new face is ANGRY."*
**Effect:** The Wax Greg statue appears. It's angry. It attacks.
**Enemies:** Wax Greg (weakened version).
**Resolution:** Fight or apologize.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE HALL
**Objective:** Walk through the hall of faces. Reach Floor 12.
**Steps:** Enter → Walk → Face yourself → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE FACES SPEAK
**Giver:** The faces (Room 2).
**Objective:** If you saved them, they nod. If you didn't, they look away.
**Reward:** Saved: +5% all stats. Failed: –5% all stats. But you can APOLOGIZE.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE NEW FACE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** A face you don't recognize. It's you. From a previous run. (Meta.)
**Reward:** Lore. Meta-narrative. **Previous Run's Item**.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE EMPTY SPACE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** A space where a face should be. Someone you didn't meet.
**Reward:** Lore. Regret. Understanding.

## BOSS: THE FACES (Room 3, if you failed everyone)

**HP:** 50
**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 3
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Disappointment:** 5 dmg + **Sadness** (–10% damage, 2 fights).
- **Silent Judgment:** All in 2D radius: **Frightened** 2 turns.
- **Empty Spaces:** Summons 2× Empty Golems.
- **Regret Wave:** Target sees their worst failure. **Frightened** 3 turns.

**Drops:**
- **Face of the Forgotten** (passive: +10% XP from emotional encounters)
- **Empty Shard** (weapon: 5 dmg, 20% **Sadness**)
- 30 gold
- **Forgiveness** (consumable: remove all guilt)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Faces fall. The Faces fall and the hall is QUIET. The Faces fall and the hall is quiet and the faces are GONE. The Faces fall and the hall is quiet and the faces are gone and you are... you are ALONE. You are alone and the faces are GONE. You are alone and the faces are gone and you can finally BREATHE."*

## DESIGN NOTES

- **Floor 13 is EMOTIONAL.** There is no real combat unless you failed everyone.
- **Teaches:** Consequence. Choice. Regret. Forgiveness.
- **Secrets:** The New Face (meta). The Empty Spaces.
- **Narrator:** "The faces are WATCHING, Greg. The faces are watching and the faces are SILENT. The faces are watching and the faces are silent and the faces are JUDGING. The faces are watching and the faces are silent and the faces are judging and the faces are RIGHT. The faces are right and you should FEEL bad. The faces are right and you should feel bad and you DO feel bad. You feel bad and you are SORRY. You feel bad and you are sorry and you can APOLOGIZE. You feel bad and you are sorry and you can apologize and the faces will FORGIVE you. The faces will forgive you because the faces are KIND. The faces will forgive you because the faces are kind and the faces are YOU. The faces will forgive you because the faces are kind and the faces are you and you are KIND. The faces will forgive you because the faces are kind and the faces are you and you are kind and you are ENOUGH."
