# FLOOR 36 — THE BATHHOUSE ANTECHAMBER

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** The bathhouse approach. Guards. Soap required. You're close.
**Mood:** Tense. You're close to the Goblin King. The guards demand soap.
**Ambient:** Steam. Water. Goblin guards arguing.
**Lighting:** Warm. Steam diffuses light. Pink and orange.
**Soundtrack:** Water. Steam. Goblin guard chatter.

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY
**Type:** Hub
**Contents:** Bonfire. A sign ("BATHHOUSE — SOAP REQUIRED"). A goblin guide (tells you the rules).
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The antechamber. The BATHHOUSE antechamber. The bathhouse is CLOSE. The bathhouse is close and you can HEAR it. You can hear the water. You can hear the STEAM. You can hear the King. You can hear the King SINGING."*

---

### ROOM 2 — GUARD POST
**Type:** Social + Combat
**Contents:**
- 2× Goblin Guards (demand soap)
- A soap checkpoint (show soap or don't pass)
- A guard schedule (read: when guards change)
- A bribe box (gold can substitute for soap)
**Enemies:** 2× Goblin Guards (if you don't have soap).
**Narrator on entry:** *"Guards. GOBLIN guards. The goblin guards want SOAP. The goblin guards want soap and you need SOAP. The goblin guards want soap and you need soap and you have... you have... do you have SOAP?"*

---

### ROOM 3 — STEAM ROOM
**Type:** Hazard + Puzzle
**Contents:**
- Steam (visibility: 1D. Beyond: invisible.)
- A steam puzzle (turn valves to clear steam)
- A hidden chest (visible only when steam clears: 20 gold, **Steam Cloak**)
- A slip hazard (wet floor: 50% fall chance)
**Enemies:** None (but hazard).
**Narrator on entry:** *"Steam. STEAM everywhere. You can't SEE. You can't see and you're WET. You can't see and you're wet and you're SLIPPERY. You can't see and you're wet and you're slippery and you FALL."*

---

### ROOM 4 — GUARD CAPTAIN
**Type:** Boss arena
**Contents:**
- The Guard Captain (Boss — see Boss section)
- 4× Goblin Guards
- A soap inspection station
- A rubber duck (the Captain's pet)
**Enemies:** The Guard Captain + Guards.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The Guard Captain. The CAPTAIN of the bath guards. The captain who INSPECTS. The captain who inspects SOAP. The captain who inspects soap and the captain is STRICT. The captain who inspects soap and the captain is strict and the captain has a RUBBER DUCK."*

---

### ROOM 5 — BATHHOUSE ENTRANCE
**Type:** Exit + Social
**Contents:**
- The bathhouse door (locked — requires soap)
- A soap fountain (fill your soap here)
- A waiting area (goblins waiting for the bath)
- The exit (staircase to Floor 35 — but the bathhouse is on Floor 25)
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The bathhouse. The BATHHOUSE. The bathhouse is CLOSE. The bathhouse is close and you can HEAR the King. You can hear the King SINGING. You can hear the King singing and the King is OFF-KEY. The King is singing and the King is off-key and the King is ALWAYS off-key."*

---

## ENCOUNTERS

### SOAP CHECK (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Approaching the guards.
**Description:** *"The guards stop you. The guards want SOAP. The guards want soap and you need SOAP."*
**Effect:** Show soap or fight.
**Resolution:** Multiple options.

### STEAM MAZE (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Entering the steam room.
**Description:** *"You can't SEE. You can't see and you're LOST. You can't see and you're lost and you're WET."*
**Effect:** Navigate blind. Turn valves. Find the chest.
**Resolution:** Puzzle.

### DUCK DISTRACTION (Room 4)
**Trigger:** Entering the Captain's room.
**Description:** *"The Captain has a DUCK. The Captain has a rubber DUCK. The Captain has a rubber duck and the Captain LOVES it."*
**Effect:** Use the duck to distract the Captain.
**Resolution:** Social or combat.

---

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE ANTECHAMBER
**Objective:** Navigate the antechamber. Reach Floor 35.
**Steps:** Enter → Pass guards → Defeat Captain → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE SOAP YOU FOUND
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** If you have soap from Floor 50 or 39, you can pass.
**Reward:** Safe passage. Guard respect.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE GUARD'S SECRET
**Giver:** A sympathetic guard (Room 2).
**Objective:** Bribe a guard with food. He tells you about the King's weakness.
**Reward:** Lore. The King's weakness (bath cap).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE OTHER ENTRANCE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 5).
**Objective:** Find a sewer grate that leads to the bathhouse.
**Reward:** Alternative path. Skip the Captain.

## BOSS: THE GUARD CAPTAIN (Room 4)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 3 (from plate armor)
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Halberd Swing:** 5 dmg, 25% **Bleeding**.
- **Shield Bash:** 3 dmg, 25% **Stun**.
- **Soap Inspection:** Target is **Exposed** (–15% defense).
- **Summon Guards:** Summons 2× Goblin Guards.
- **Duck Distraction:** Throws rubber duck. All are **Distracted** 1 turn.

**Drops:**
- **Captain's Halberd** (weapon: 6 dmg, 15% **Bleeding**)
- **Rubber Duck** (consumable: distracts all enemies 1 turn)
- 25 gold

**Narrator on death:** *"The Captain falls. The Captain falls and the duck QUACKS. The Captain falls and the duck quacks and the duck is SAD. The Captain falls and the duck quacks and the duck is sad and the duck is LONELY. The Captain falls and the duck quacks and the duck is sad and the duck is lonely and you take the DUCK. You take the duck and the duck is YOURS. You take the duck and the duck is yours and the duck is HAPPY."*
