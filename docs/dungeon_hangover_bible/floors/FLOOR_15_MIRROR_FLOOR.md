## PUZZLES

### THE PERFECT MIRROR PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The Perfect Mirror is perfect. How do you beat it?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight it directly (hard — it's perfect).
2. Accept it (dialogue — you become one. +10% all stats, –10% max HP).
3. Reject it (dialogue — you reject perfection. The Mirror shatters).
4. Have the most regrets (highest Curse stacks): The Mirror can't copy you. It shatters.
5. Talk to it (dialogue — it's lonely. It joins you briefly. Then shatters).
**Narrator on solving:** *"The Perfect Mirror shatters. The Perfect Mirror shatters and the reflections SHATTER. The Perfect Mirror shatters and the reflections shatter and the room is DARK. The Perfect Mirror shatters and the reflections shatter and the room is dark and you are... you are IMPERFECT. You are imperfect and you are REAL. You are imperfect and you are real and you are ENOUGH."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE FLOOR
**Objective:** Navigate the mirror floor. Reach Floor 14.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Deal with Perfect Mirror → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE PERFECT MIRROR
**Giver:** The Perfect Mirror (Room 3).
**Objective:** Fight, accept, or reject the Perfect Mirror.
**Reward:** Accept: +10% all stats, –10% max HP. Reject: **Mirror Shard**. Fight: 25 gold.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE MIRROR'S GIFT
**Giver:** A mirror (Room 2).
**Objective:** A mirror shows you a skill you don't have. You can learn it.
**Reward:** One free skill from any tree.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE WORST MIRROR
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Face your worst self. Accept it or fight it.
**Reward:** Accept: **Self-Acceptance** (+10% all resistances). Fight: **Worst Shard**.

## BOSS: THE PERFECT MIRROR (Room 3)

**HP:** Same as your max HP
**Damage:** Same as your weapon + 2
**Armor:** Same as yours + 2
**Speed:** Same as you

**Abilities:**
- Copies your last used skill (perfectly).
- **Perfect Strike:** Guaranteed crit every 2 turns.
- **No Flaws:** Immune to all debuffs.
- **No Regrets:** Immune to **Frightened**, **Sadness**, **Confused**.
- **Perfect Reflection:** Reflects 50% of damage back.

**Drops:**
- **Perfect Mirror Shard** (weapon: 6 dmg, 25% copy skill, +5% all stats)
- **Mirror Crown** (Head: +10% all stats, –10% max HP)
- 30 gold
- **Self-Acceptance** (passive: +10% all resistances)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Perfect Mirror shatters. The Perfect Mirror shatters and the reflections SHATTER. The Perfect Mirror shatters and the reflections shatter and the room is DARK. The Perfect Mirror shatters and the reflections shatter and the room is dark and you are... you are IMPERFECT. You are imperfect and you are REAL. You are imperfect and you are real and you are ENOUGH. You are imperfect and you are real and you are enough and you are YOURSELF."*
