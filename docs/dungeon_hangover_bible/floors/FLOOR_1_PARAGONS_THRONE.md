# FLOOR 1 — THE PARAGON'S THRONE

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** The ending. The Paragon is a child. The Paragon is crying. The Paragon asks: "Can you be better?"
**Mood:** Emotional. The Paragon is not a boss. The Paragon is a CHILD. The Paragon is a child who has been trying to be good for ten thousand years and failing every single day.
**Ambient:** A child's crying. The echo of failure. The sound of TRYING.
**Lighting:** White. Blinding. Beautiful. The light of a THOUSAND suns.
**Soundtrack:** A child's crying. The echo of failure. The sound of TRYING.
