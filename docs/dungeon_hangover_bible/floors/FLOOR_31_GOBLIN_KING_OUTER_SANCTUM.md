## BOSS: THE GOBLIN WARLORD (Room 5)

**HP:** 50
**Damage:** 6
**Armor:** 4
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Axe Swing:** 6 dmg, 20% **Bleeding**.
- **Shield Bash:** 4 dmg, 25% **Stun**.
- **Summon Guards:** Summons 2× Elite Guards.
- **War Cry:** All goblins on floor: +15% damage, 2 turns.
- **Execute:** Target below 20% HP: instant kill.

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Axe Swing, Shield Bash. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-25% HP):** More aggressive. Summon Guards every turn. War Cry.
**Phase 3 (25-0% HP):** Execute. Desperate.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Steal the banner: Goblins are **Demoralized** (–15% damage).
- Destroy the war drums: Goblins lose their buff.
- Have the Goblin Banner from the side quest: Warlord yields. Peaceful resolution.
- Have Scrag (goblin companion): Scrag talks to his great-uncle. Warlord lets you pass.

**Drops:**
- **Warlord's Axe** (weapon: 8 dmg, 15% **Bleeding**)
- **War Banner** (consumable: +15% damage to all allies, 2 turns)
- 50 gold
- **Warlord's Helm** (Head: +5% physical resistance, +5% damage)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Warlord falls. The Warlord falls and the army is BROKEN. The Warlord falls and the army is broken and the goblins FLEE. The Warlord falls and the army is broken and the goblins flee and the sanctum is QUIET. The Warlord falls and the army is broken and the goblins flee and the sanctum is quiet and you are... you are a WARRIOR. You are a warrior and you are a HERO. You are a warrior and you are a hero and you are still in your UNDERWEAR."*
