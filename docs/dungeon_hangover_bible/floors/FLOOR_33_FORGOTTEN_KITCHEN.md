# FLOOR 33 — THE FORGOTTEN KITCHEN

**Mood:** Warm. Sad. The chef just wants to feed someone. The chef is lonely.

**Lighting:** Warm. Firelight. The most COMFORTABLE you've felt.
**Soundtrack:** Cooking. Bubbling. The chef humming (off-key, like a lullaby).

### ROOM 2 — KITCHEN
**Type:** Social + Rest
**Contents:**
- The Ghost Chef (NPC — see below)
- A soup pot (infinite soup: heal 15 HP, once per visit)

## COMPANION RESOLUTION (Sporefriend)

**If you have Sporefriend AND choose the soup path:**
- Sporefriend senses something in the kitchen.
- It bounces to the soup pot.
- It merges with the soup.
- The soup becomes permanent (you can craft it forever).
- Sporefriend is gone.
- The chef says: "The little one... it wanted to be part of something. It's part of the soup now. It'll feed you forever. That's... that's beautiful."

**Narrator on Sporefriend merging:** *"The mushroom merges with the soup. The mushroom is GONE. The mushroom is gone and the soup is BEAUTIFUL. The mushroom is gone and the soup is beautiful and the soup is ALIVE. The mushroom is gone and the soup is beautiful and the soup is alive and the soup will feed you FOREVER."*

## ENCOUNTERS

### SOUP OFFERING (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Entering the kitchen.
**Description:** *"The chef offers you soup. The chef offers you soup and the soup is BEAUTIFUL. The chef offers you soup and the soup is beautiful and the soup smells LIKE HOME."*
**Effect:** Eat soup (heal 15 HP) or refuse (chef is sad).
**Resolution:** Eat or refuse.

### COOKING LESSON (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Talking to the chef.
**Description:** *"The chef wants to TEACH you. The chef wants to teach you to COOK. The chef wants to teach you to cook SOUP."*
**Effect:** Learn **Ghost Soup** recipe (permanent crafting).
**Resolution:** Learn or decline.

### RECIPE VAULT (Room 4)
**Trigger:** Finding the locked vault.
**Description:** *"A vault. A RECIPE vault. The vault is LOCKED. The vault is locked and the vault has a KEYHOLE. The vault is locked and the vault has a keyhole and you need a KEY."*
**Effect:** Find key or break open.
**Resolution:** Multiple options.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE KITCHEN
**Objective:** Navigate the kitchen. Reach Floor 32.
**Steps:** Enter → Eat soup → Learn recipe → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE SOUP
**Giver:** The Ghost Chef (Room 2).
**Objective:** Eat the soup. Compliment it.
**Reward:** Full heal + **Well Fed** (+5% all stats, 3 fights).

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE RECIPE
**Giver:** The Ghost Chef (Room 2).
**Objective:** Learn the recipe.
**Reward:** **Ghost Soup** recipe (permanent crafting).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE CHEF'S REGRET
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 3).
**Objective:** Read the plaque. Understand the chef's story.
**Reward:** Lore. **Sadness Wisdom**. Unlocks Ending C clue.

## BOSS: THE GHOST CHEF (Room 2, if you refuse soup or insult cooking)

**HP:** 20
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 1
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Ladle Smash:** 3 dmg, 25% **Burning** (hot soup).
- **Hot Oil Splash:** 2 dmg + **Burning** (1/turn, 2 turns).
- **Soup Splash:** Heals himself 5 HP.
- **Kitchen Nightmare:** All in 2D radius: **Nauseated** 2 turns.

**Drops:**
- **Chef's Ladle** (weapon: 4 dmg, 25% **Burning**)
- **Ghost Soup Recipe** (permanent crafting)
- 15 gold
- **Chef's Hat** (Head: +5% fire resistance, +5% cooking success)

**Narrator on death:** *"The chef falls. The soup falls. The soup is COLD. The soup is cold and the kitchen is QUIET. The soup is cold and the kitchen is quiet and the chef is... the chef is SAD. The chef is sad and the chef is LONELY. The chef is sad and the chef is lonely and the chef just wanted to FEED someone. The chef just wanted to feed someone and you... you ATTACKED the chef. You attacked the chef and the chef is DEAD and the soup is COLD and you feel... you feel TERRIBLE."*

The chef just wants to hear that the soup is GOOD."
