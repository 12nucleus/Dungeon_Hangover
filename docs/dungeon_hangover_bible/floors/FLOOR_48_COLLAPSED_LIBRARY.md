# FLOOR 48 — THE COLLAPSED LIBRARY

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Ruined library. Books everywhere. Knowledge lost. Ghosts of scholars.
**Mood:** Melancholy. Quiet. The weight of forgotten things.
**Ambient:** Pages turning (no wind). Whispered words. The creak of old wood.
**Lighting:** Dim. Some books glow (enchanted). Dust motes in light beams from cracks above.
**Soundtrack:** Soft whispers. Occasional book falls. A distant, rhythmic thumping (something below).

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY HALL
**Dimensions:** 6m × 5m. Fallen bookshelves. A grand entrance, now ruined.
**Type:** Safe zone / Hub
**Contents:**
- A bonfire (lit — someone was here before you)
- A reading desk (search: 5 gold, a quill, a note: "The Overdue does not forgive. The Overdue does not FORGET.")
- A fallen chandelier (can be used as weapon: 4 dmg, one use, heavy)
- A bookshelf (still standing — contains readable books: lore)
**Lighting:** Dim. Light beams from ceiling cracks.
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A library. A COLLAPSED library. Books everywhere. Knowledge everywhere. And you can't read. Well — you CAN read. You just don't WANT to. That's different."*

---

### ROOM 2 — THE STACKS
**Dimensions:** 8m × 4m. Tall bookshelves. Narrow aisles.
**Type:** Exploration + Trap
**Contents:**
- 12 bookshelves (searchable — random loot: gold, notes, skill pages)
- A falling shelf trap (trigger: pulling wrong book — 3 dmg, **Prone**)
- A hidden compartment (behind a loose book: 15 gold, **Library Card**)
- A ghost reader (harmless — reads the same book over and over)
**Lighting:** Very dim. Some books glow.
**Enemies:** None (but trap).
**Narrator on entry:** *"The stacks. Row after row. Shelf after shelf. Book after book. You have never read a book in your life. You are not about to start."*
**Narrator on finding Library Card:** *"A library card. It's yours. It has YOUR name on it. You don't remember getting a library card. You don't remember going to a LIBRARY. But here it is. In your name. In a dungeon. This is fine."*

---

### ROOM 3 — ARCHIVE VAULT
**Dimensions:** 4m × 4m. Locked. Underground.
**Type:** Loot + Puzzle
**Contents:**
- A locked door (requires Library Card or lockpick)
- Inside: 3 special books (see Side Quest 2)
- A skeleton at a desk (loot: 10 gold, a ring, a note: "I've been here for 300 years. I've read every book. I still don't understand.")
- A pressure plate (trap: darts, 2 dmg)
**Lighting:** Dark. One glowing book.
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A vault. A locked vault. A vault full of BOOKS. You don't like books. Books don't have PICTURES. Books don't have MONSTERS. Books are BORING."*

---

### ROOM 4 — RARE BOOKS
**Dimensions:** 5m × 5m. Glass cases. Some broken.
**Type:** Loot + Combat
**Contents:**
- 3× Glass cases (one intact: contains **Tome of Skills** — learn one random skill)
- 1× Book Golem (enemy — 18 HP, 4 dmg, **Paper Cut**: **Bleeding** 1/turn for 3 turns)
- A first edition book (valuable: 50 gold if sold)
- A book on the Paragon (lore — critical for understanding the story)
**Lighting:** Dim. Glass reflects light.
**Enemies:** 1× Book Golem.
**Narrator on entry:** *"Rare books. Books behind glass. Books you can't TOUCH. Books you can't READ. This is the worst kind of book."*
**Narrator on Book Golem:** *"The book is ALIVE. The book is ANGRY. The book has PAGES and the pages have TEETH. This is the most HORRIFYING thing you've ever seen. And you once saw yourself in a mirror."*

---

### ROOM 5 — BOOK WORM TUNNEL
**Dimensions:** 2m wide, 10m long. Chewed through books.
**Type:** Combat + Environmental
**Contents:**
- 1× Giant Bookworm (enemy — 20 HP, 3 dmg, **Swallow**: consumes one item from your inventory)
- 3× Bookworms (enemies — 3 HP each, 1 dmg)
- A tunnel of chewed books (can be collapsed: 4 dmg to all inside, but blocks path)
- A hidden cache (inside a hollow book: 20 gold, **Bookworm Ring**)
**Lighting:** Dark. Bookworms glow faintly.
**Enemies:** Giant Bookworm + Bookworms.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A tunnel. A tunnel made of CHEWED books. Something has been EATING the knowledge. Something has been EATING the WORDS. This is a CRIME against LITERATURE."*

---

### ROOM 6 — OVERDUE THRONE
**Dimensions:** 7m × 7m. A throne made of overdue books. The Overdue sits here.
**Type:** Boss arena
**Contents:**
- The Overdue (Boss — see Boss section)
- The Throne (made of 1000 overdue books — destroying it: removes his shield)
- 4× Book Piles (can be searched: random loot, but trigger **Dust Cloud** — 25% **Nauseated**)
- A stamp desk (can be used as cover)
**Lighting:** Dim. Ghostly green light.
**Enemies:** The Overdue.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The Overdue. The ghost of a librarian. The ghost of a librarian who has been WAITING. The ghost of a librarian who has been waiting for THREE HUNDRED YEARS. The ghost of a librarian who wants his BOOKS BACK."*

---

### ROOM 7 — READING ROOM
**Dimensions:** 5m × 5m. Comfortable chairs. A fireplace (cold).
**Type:** Rest + Lore
**Contents:**
- A reading chair (resting here: heal 15 HP, once)
- A bookshelf (contains: **The Paragon's History** — lore book, critical)
- A ghost reader (harmless — if you sit with it, it reads aloud: lore)
- A hidden passage (behind fireplace — leads to staircase to Floor 47)
**Lighting:** Warm. Fireplace glow (magical).
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A reading room. Chairs. A fireplace. Comfort. You haven't been comfortable in DAYS. You sit. You rest. You almost fall asleep. You almost DREAM."*

## ENCOUNTERS

### GHOST READER (Random)
**Trigger:** Entering any room with books.
**Description:** *"A ghost. Reading. It doesn't look up. It doesn't ACKNOWLEDGE you. It just reads."*
**Effect:** None (unless you disturb it — then it attacks: 2 dmg, **Cursed** for 2 turns).
**Resolution:** Leave it alone or fight.

### FALLING SHELF (Trap)
**Trigger:** Pulling the wrong book in Room 2.
**Description:** *"The shelf FALLS. The shelf is FALLING. The shelf is falling ON YOU."*
**Effect:** 3 dmg + **Prone**.
**Resolution:** Dodge (Dexterity check) or take damage.

### BOOKWORM AMBUSH (Random)
**Trigger:** Searching bookshelves too loudly.
**Description:** *"Something moves inside the book. Something with MANDIBLES. Something with HUNGER."*
**Enemies:** 2× Bookworms.
**Resolution:** Fight or flee.

## PUZZLES

### THE OVERDUE FINES PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 6
**Problem:** The Overdue demands payment for overdue books. You don't have his books.
**Solutions:**
1. Return 3 overdue books from Room 3's archive (intended — peaceful resolution).
2. Pay 50 gold (alternative — he accepts but is disappointed).
3. Fight him (default).
4. Have the Library Card (he's slightly nicer, but still wants books).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You returned the books. The Overdue cries. The Overdue LAUGHS. The Overdue is at peace. You are the first person in 300 years to return a library book. You are a HERO."*

### THE HIDDEN PASSAGE PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 7
**Problem:** How do you get to Floor 47?
**Solutions:**
1. Find the hidden passage behind the fireplace (Perception check).
2. Read the book on the Paragon (reveals the passage).
3. Have the Other Hermit with you (he knows).
4. Break through the wall (2+ Strength).
**Narrator on solving:** *"A passage. A hidden passage. A passage that's been waiting. A passage that's been WAITING FOR YOU."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE LIBRARY
**Objective:** Navigate the library. Reach Floor 47.
**Steps:**
1. Enter the library (Room 1).
2. Navigate the stacks (Room 2).
3. Find the archive (Room 3).
4. Defeat or bypass the Overdue (Room 6).
5. Find the hidden passage (Room 7).
**Reward:** Access to Floor 47. Experience. Loot.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE PARAGON'S FIRST RECORD
**Giver:** None (discover through reading).
**Objective:** Find the book about the Paragon.
**Steps:**
1. Find the book in Room 7.
2. Read it (or have someone read it to you).
**Reward:** Lore. Understanding. Ending C clue.

### SIDE QUEST 2: BOOKWORM
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 5).
**Objective:** Deal with the Giant Bookworm.
**Steps:**
1. Enter Room 5.
2. Fight the Bookworm OR feed it 3 books (from Room 2).
**Reward:** If fed: **Bookworm Ring** (+5% XP from books). If fought: 15 gold, **Bookworm Chitin** (crafting).

### SIDE QUEST 3: OVERDUE FINES
**Giver:** The Overdue (Room 6).
**Objective:** Return 3 overdue books.
**Steps:**
1. Find the books in Room 3's archive.
2. Return them to the Overdue.
**Reward:** Peaceful resolution. The Index (reveals enemy weaknesses). 20 gold.

## BOSS: THE OVERDUE (Room 6)

**HP:** 35
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 3 (from floating books)
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Shush:** 2 dmg + **Silenced** (cannot use skills) for 2 turns.
- **Book Barrage:** Throws 3 books. Each: 2 dmg. 50% chance **Dazed** (lose 1 AP).
- **Overdue Fine:** Target loses 10 gold (if no gold: 5 dmg instead).
- **Dust Cloud:** All in 2D radius: 25% **Nauseated**.
- **Page Turn:** Heals 5 HP (reads a book to recover).

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Shush, Book Barrage. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-0% HP):** More aggressive. Overdue Fine every turn. Page Turn every other turn.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Destroy the throne (attack it: 20 HP): Overdue loses armor. Becomes desperate.
- Use fire: Books burn. Overdue takes 2x damage from fire.
- Use water: Books become soggy. Overdue slows.

**Drops:**
- **Librarian's Robes** (Body: +10% XP gain, +5% skill success chance)
- **The Index** (consumable: reveals all enemy weaknesses for 3 fights)
- 25 gold
- **Overdue Stamp** (weapon: 4 dmg, 25% chance **Cursed**)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Overdue falls. The books fall. The books are QUIET. The library is quiet. The library is at peace. The library is FINALLY at peace."*

## DESIGN NOTNS

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 48 is medium difficulty.** The Overdue is tough but fair. The Bookworm is a puzzle.
- **Many paths.** You can avoid most combat.
- **Expected deaths:** 0-1.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 2:** Teaches trap awareness (falling shelf).
- **Room 3:** Teaches puzzle-solving (locked vault).
- **Room 4:** Teaches environmental combat (Book Golem).
- **Room 5:** Teaches alternative solutions (feed the worm).
- **Room 6:** Teaches boss phases (Overdue).
- **Room 7:** Teaches hidden passage discovery.

### SECRETS
- **The Library Card:** Hidden in Room 2. Opens Room 3.
- **The Tome of Skills:** In Room 4. Free skill.
- **The Paragon's History:** In Room 7. Critical lore.
- **The Hidden Passage:** Behind fireplace. Progression.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 48 is melancholy.** The narrator is quieter. The narrator is... RESPECTFUL? The narrator sees the beauty in the books.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You can't read, Greg. You can READ. You just don't WANT to. That's different. That's WORSE."
- **The narrator LOVES the Overdue fight.** "You're fighting a LIBRARIAN. You're fighting a GHOST librarian. This is the most TERRIFYING thing you've ever seen. And you once saw yourself in a mirror."
