# FLOOR 2 — THE DOOR

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** A door. It's locked. You have the key. It's made of every choice you made.
**Mood:** Tense. You're at the door. You have the key. You turn the key. The door OPENS.
**Ambient:** The echo of the key. The creak of the door. The sound of LIGHT.
**Lighting:** Dark. The door is the only thing you can see. The door GLOWS.
**Soundtrack:** The echo of the key. The creak of the door. The sound of LIGHT.
