# FLOOR 44 — THE WAX MUSEUM

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Wax figures. Adventurers who failed. One of them is you.
**Mood:** Uncanny. Unsettling. The figures look REAL. The figures look ALIVE.
**Ambient:** Dripping wax. Creaking floors. Breathing (is that the figures?).
**Lighting:** Dim. Spotlights on figures. Shadows move.
**Soundtrack:** Dripping. Occasional crack (wax settling). A heartbeat (yours?).

## PUZZLES

### THE WAX GREG PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 4
**Problem:** Wax Greg is a copy of you. How do you beat him?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight him directly (he has your build — hard).
2. Use fire (melts him — easy).
3. Destroy your statue in the side quest (he's weakened).
4. Look in the mirror (confuses him — he doesn't know which is real).
5. Talk to him (dialogue — he's lonely. He joins you briefly. Then melts.)
**Narrator on solving:** *"Wax Greg melts. Wax Greg DIES. Wax Greg dies and you feel... you feel NOTHING. You feel EVERYTHING. You killed yourself. You killed a WAX version of yourself. This is the WEIRDEST thing you've ever done."*

### THE SCULPTOR'S SECRET PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 2
**Problem:** The sculptor's journal mentions a "perfect figure." Where is it?
**Solutions:**
1. Read the journal (reveals Room 4).
2. Search the studio (find a hidden key).
3. Talk to the sculptor's ghost (he tells you).
4. Find the half-finished figure (it's the key to Room 4).
**Narrator on solving:** *"The perfect figure. The figure of YOU. The sculptor made a figure of you. The sculptor made a figure of you BEFORE you arrived. The sculptor KNEW. The sculptor KNEW you were coming."*

## BOSS: WAX GREG (Room 4)

**HP:** 30 (same as your max HP)
**Damage:** 4 (same as your weapon)
**Armor:** 2 (wax coating)
**Speed:** Same as you

**Abilities:**
- Copies your last used skill (if you use a skill, he uses it next turn).
- **Wax Shield:** Absorbs first hit each turn.
- **Mirror Image:** Creates a wax copy (50% HP, 50% dmg, lasts 2 turns).
- **Melt:** Below 25% HP, he melts. Loses armor. Gains +20% speed.
- **Perfect Strike:** Guaranteed crit every 3 turns.

**Phase 1 (100-50% HP):** Copies your skills. Defensive.
**Phase 2 (50-25% HP):** More aggressive. Mirror Image. Perfect Strike.
**Phase 3 (25-0% HP):** Melt. Fast. Desperate.

**Environmental Interaction:**
- Use fire: 2x damage. Melts him faster.
- Look in the mirror: Confuses him. He attacks himself.
- Destroy your statue in side quest: He's weakened (–20% HP).
- Have the Sculptor's Ghost with you: He pleads for Wax Greg. Peaceful resolution.

**Drops:**
- **Wax Crown** (Head: +5% all stats, –5% speed — you're made of wax)
- **Mirror Shard** (weapon: 4 dmg, 25% chance to copy enemy skill)
- 20 gold
- **Wax Heart** (consumable: +10% all resistances for 3 fights)

**Narrator on death:** *"Wax Greg melts. Wax Greg DIES. Wax Greg dies and you feel... you feel NOTHING. You feel EVERYTHING. You killed yourself. You killed a WAX version of yourself. This is the WEIRDEST thing you've ever done. And you once arm-wrestled a HORSE."*

## DESIGN NOTES

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 44 is medium difficulty.** Wax Greg is tough because he copies you.
- **Fire makes it easy.** Use fire. Use fire and win.
- **Expected deaths:** 0-1.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 1:** Teaches observation (wax figures).
- **Room 2:** Teaches lore gathering (sculptor's journal).
- **Room 3:** Teaches shop mechanics (gift shop).
- **Room 4:** Teaches mirror match (Wax Greg).
- **Room 5:** Teaches environmental combat (wax dragon).

### SECRETS
- **The Half-Finished Figure:** In Room 2. Lore.
- **The Wax Dragon:** In Room 5. Height advantage.
- **The Mirror:** In Room 4. Puzzle solution.
- **The Hidden Room:** Behind wax seal in Room 5. Loot.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 44 is uncanny.** The narrator is... UNCOMFORTABLE? The narrator sees the wrongness.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You're fighting YOURSELF. You're fighting a WAX version of yourself. This is the most NARCISSISTIC thing you've ever done. And you once proposed to a CHANDELIER."
- **The narrator LOVES the Sculptor.** "The sculptor makes WAX people. The sculptor makes wax people FOREVER. This is the most DISTURBING art you've ever seen. This is the most BEAUTIFUL art you've ever seen. This is BOTH."
