## PUZZLES

### THE GUARDIAN'S PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The Guardian will not let you pass. How do you get through?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight the Guardian (hard).
2. Say "I'm here to help" (the Guardian steps aside — peaceful).
3. Say "I'm here to hurt" (the Guardian attacks — fight).
4. Have the Teacher's Bell from Floor 9 (the Guardian is calmed).
5. Have the Other Hermit with you (he knows the Guardian).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You said you're here to HELP. You said you're here to help and the Guardian STEPS ASIDE. You said you're here to help and the Guardian steps aside and the Guardian is... the Guardian is RELIEVED. You said you're here to help and the Guardian steps aside and the Guardian is relieved and the Guardian is FREE. You said you're here to help and the Guardian steps aside and the Guardian is relieved and the Guardian is free and the Guardian can REST."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE ANTEROOM
**Objective:** Navigate the anteroom. Reach Floor 7.
**Steps:** Enter → Listen → Deal with Guardian → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE VOICE
**Giver:** The Paragon's voice (Room 2).
**Objective:** The Paragon asks: "Are you here to hurt me?" (Dialogue choice.)
**Reward:** Say no: the Paragon is calmer. Say yes: fight the Guardian.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE GUARDIAN
**Giver:** The Guardian (Room 3).
**Objective:** The Guardian protects the Paragon. Say "I'm here to help."
**Reward:** Peaceful passage. **Guardian's Oath** (+10% defense).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE DOOR
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** The door to Floor 7 is locked. The key is your choices.
**Reward:** The door opens when you're ready.
