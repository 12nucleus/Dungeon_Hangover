# FLOOR 30 — THE DWARVEN GATE

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** The dwarven floors begin. A ghost doorkeeper demands ID. You don't have any.
**Mood:** Bureaucratic. Absurd. The dwarves had RULES. The dwarves had rules and the rules REMAIN.
**Ambient:** Stone grinding. A quill scratching. The echo of a stamp.
**Lighting:** Torchlight. Stone. The first DWARVEN architecture.
**Soundtrack:** Stone. A quill. A stamp. The sound of BUREAUCRACY.

**Resolution:** Multiple options.

**Description:** *"Your name. Your name is in the REGISTRY. Your name is in the registry and your name was written THREE HUNDRED YEARS AGO."*

## PUZZLES

### THE ID PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 2
**Problem:** The doorkeeper demands ID. You don't have any.
**Solutions:**
1. Forge an ID in Room 4 (intended — requires materials).
2. Find the Dwarven ID in Room 4's chest (alternative).
3. Fight the doorkeeper (hard).
4. Charm the doorkeeper (social).
5. Have the Architect's Compass from Floor 21 (doorkeeper respects you).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You showed ID. You showed ID to a GHOST. You showed ID to a ghost and the ghost is SATISFIED. You showed ID to a ghost and the ghost is satisfied and the ghost lets you PASS. You showed ID to a ghost and the ghost is satisfied and the ghost lets you pass and you feel... you feel like a CITIZEN. You feel like a citizen and you are a CITIZEN. You are a citizen and you are in a DWARVEN city. This is the most BUREAUCRATIC you've ever felt."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE GATE
**Objective:** Pass the doorkeeper. Reach Floor 29.
**Steps:** Enter → Deal with doorkeeper → Find registry → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: FORGE AN ID
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Forge an ID at the ID forge.
**Reward:** **Dwarven ID** (passes all dwarven checks).

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE REGISTRY
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Find your name in the registry.
**Reward:** Lore. Mystery. Ending C clue.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE DOORKEEPER'S FEE
**Giver:** The Doorkeeper (Room 2).
**Objective:** Pay the doorkeeper in ale.
**Reward:** Safe passage. **Dwarven Ale** (+20% damage, 1 turn).

## BOSS: THE DOORKEEPER (Room 2, if you refuse ID)

**HP:** 25
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 3 (from bureaucratic armor)
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Stamp of Denial:** 3 dmg + **Denied** (cannot pass, 2 turns).
- **Paper Cut:** 2 dmg + **Bleeding** (1/turn, 3 turns).
- **Bureaucratic Nightmare:** All in 2D radius: **Confused** 2 turns.
- **File Complaint:** Summons 2× Paper Golems (5 HP each, 2 dmg).

**Drops:**
- **Doorkeeper's Stamp** (weapon: 4 dmg, 25% **Denied**)
- **Dwarven ID** (passive: passes all dwarven checks)
- 15 gold
- **Registry Book** (lore: your name is in it)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Doorkeeper falls. The Doorkeeper falls and the gate is OPEN. The Doorkeeper falls and the gate is open and the registry is QUIET. The Doorkeeper falls and the gate is open and the registry is quiet and you are... you are APPROVED. You are approved and you are a CITIZEN. You are approved and you are a citizen and you are in a DWARVEN city. This is the most BUREAUCRATIC you've ever felt."*

## DESIGN NOTES

- **Floor 30 is EASY.** The Doorkeeper is weak. The ID puzzle is the challenge.
- **Teaches:** Bureaucracy mechanics. ID. Dwarven culture.
- **Secrets:** The Registry (your name). The ID Forge.
- **Narrator:** "You need ID, Greg. You need ID and you have NO ID. You need ID and you have no ID and the doorkeeper is STERN. You need ID and you have no ID and the doorkeeper is stern and the doorkeeper is a DWARF. You need ID and you have no ID and the doorkeeper is stern and the doorkeeper is a dwarf and dwarves LOVE rules. Dwarves love rules and you HATE rules. You hate rules and you are in a DWARVEN city. This is the worst feeling in the world."
