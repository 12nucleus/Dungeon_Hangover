# FLOOR 18 — THE COURT OF WHISPERS

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Fae-like beings who trade in secrets, not gold. They know EVERYTHING.
**Mood:** Seductive. Dangerous. The whispers trade in SECRETS. The whispers want YOURS.
**Ambient:** Whispers. Laughter. The rustle of silk.
**Lighting:** Dim. Shifting. The fae lights dance.
**Soundtrack:** Whispers. Laughter. The sound of SECRETS.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE COURT
**Objective:** Navigate the court. Reach Floor 17.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Deal with Queen → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: TRADE A SECRET
**Giver:** A fae merchant (Room 2).
**Objective:** Give them a secret (your real name, a regret).
**Reward:** A boon. **Secret Keeper's Ring**.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE WHISPERING DEAL
**Giver:** The Whisper Queen (Room 3).
**Objective:** They offer to tell you the Paragon's name. Price: a memory.
**Reward:** The Paragon's name. Lore. Critical.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE SECRET VAULT
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Open the vault with a secret.
**Reward:** 40 gold. **Secret Keeper's Ring**. The Paragon's name.

## BOSS: THE WHISPER QUEEN (Room 3)

**HP:** 35
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Whisper:** 3 dmg + **Charmed** 1 turn.
- **Secret Exposure:** Target's worst secret is revealed. **Humiliated** (–10% damage, 2 fights).
- **Fae Glamour:** All in 2D radius: **Distracted** 2 turns.
- **Summon Guards:** Summons 2× Fae guards.
- **Truth or Dare:** Target must answer truth or take 5 dmg.

**Drops:**
- **Whisper Crown** (Head: +10% social success, +5% charm resist)
- **Fae Cloak** (Body: +10% evasion, +5% charm resist)
- 30 gold
- **Secret Keeper's Ring** (passive: +5% XP from secrets)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Whisper Queen falls. The Whisper Queen falls and the court is QUIET. The Whisper Queen falls and the court is quiet and the secrets are SAFE. The Whisper Queen falls and the court is quiet and the secrets are safe and you are... you are ALONE. You are alone and the secrets are YOURS. You are alone and the secrets are yours and the secrets are SAFE."*
