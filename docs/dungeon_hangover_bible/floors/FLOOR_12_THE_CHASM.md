# FLOOR 12 — THE CHASM

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** A floor that is just falling. Forever. You have to climb sideways.
**Mood:** Disorienting. You're falling. You've been falling. You'll keep falling.
**Ambient:** Wind. The sound of FALLING. Your own scream (distant).
**Lighting:** Grey. Endless. You can't see the bottom. You can't see the TOP.
**Soundtrack:** Wind. Falling. The sound of NOTHING.

## ENCOUNTERS

### FALLING DEBRIS (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Random.
**Description:** *"Something FALLS. Something falls from ABOVE. Something falls from above and something is HEAVY. Something falls from above and something is heavy and something is a ROCK."*
**Effect:** 4 dmg.
**Resolution:** Dodge or take damage.

### WIND CURRENT (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Random.
**Description:** *"The wind PUSHES. The wind pushes you. The wind pushes you and the wind is STRONG. The wind pushes you and the wind is strong and the wind is COLD."*
**Effect:** Pushed 1D sideways.
**Resolution:** Hold on or be pushed.

### CLIMB CHECK (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Each climb attempt.
**Description:** *"You climb. You climb and the climb is HARD. You climb and the climb is hard and the climb is DANGEROUS. You climb and the climb is hard and the climb is dangerous and you're FALLING. You climb and the climb is hard and the climb is dangerous and you're falling and you're SCARED."*
**Effect:** Success: climb 1D. Fail: fall 1D (2 dmg).
**Resolution:** Climb or fall.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE CHASM
**Objective:** Navigate the chasm. Reach Floor 11.
**Steps:** Enter → Fall → Climb → Deal with Fall → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE CLIMB
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Climb the walls. Hard. But rewarding.
**Reward:** **Climbing Wisdom** (+10% Athletics, permanent). 20 gold.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE FALL
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Let yourself fall. You land on Floor 11.
**Reward:** Skip Floor 12's boss. 10 gold. 5 dmg.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE WIND
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Use the wind to glide. (Requires specific item or skill.)
**Reward:** Glide past the boss. **Wind Wisdom** (+10% evasion).

## BOSS: THE FALL (Room 3)

**HP:** 40
**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Very Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Gravity Pull:** 5 dmg + pulled 2D toward center.
- **Debris Fall:** All in 2D radius: 4 dmg.
- **Wind Blast:** 3 dmg + pushed 2D.
- **Endless Fall:** Target is **Falling** (cannot act, 1 turn).
- **Crush:** Target below 25% HP: 8 dmg.

**Drops:**
- **Fall Boots** (Feet: +10% fall resistance, immune to **Prone**)
- **Gravity Orb** (consumable: pull all enemies 2D toward you)
- 30 gold
- **Wind Cloak** (Body: +10% evasion, immune to push)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Fall stops. The Fall stops and the chasm is STILL. The Fall stops and the chasm is still and the air is CALM. The Fall stops and the chasm is still and the air is calm and you are... you are STANDING. You are standing and you are SAFE. You are standing and you are safe and you are on SOLID GROUND. You are standing and you are safe and you are on solid ground and you are WELCOME."*
