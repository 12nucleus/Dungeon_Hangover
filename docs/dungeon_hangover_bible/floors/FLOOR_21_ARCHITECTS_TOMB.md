## PUZZLES

### THE ARCHITECT'S DESIGN PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The Architect wants to know if his design worked. What do you say?
**Solutions:**
1. Tell him yes: He's at peace. Gives you **Architect's Compass** (reveals hidden doors).
2. Tell him no: He's angry. Attacks. But drops **Architect's Blueprint** (shortcut).
3. Tell him the truth (it worked but the Paragon is still suffering): He cries. Gives you both.
4. Don't answer: He waits. He'll wait FOREVER.
**Narrator on solving:** *"You told the Architect. You told the Architect the TRUTH. You told the Architect the truth and the Architect CRIES. You told the Architect the truth and the Architect cries and the Architect is... the Architect is RELIEVED. The Architect is relieved and the Architect is FREE. The Architect is relieved and the Architect is free and the Architect can REST."*

## BOSS: THE ARCHITECT (Room 3, if you tell him his design failed)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Blueprint Strike:** 4 dmg + **Trapped** (cannot move, 1 turn).
- **Stone Wall:** Creates a wall (blocks movement, 2 turns).
- **Design Flaw:** Target is **Exposed** (–15% defense).
- **Summon Golems:** Summons 2× Stone Golems.
- **Structural Collapse:** All in 2D radius: 3 dmg + **Prone**.

**Drops:**
- **Architect's Compass** (passive: reveals hidden doors)
- **Architect's Blueprint** (shortcut: Floor 20 to Floor 15)
- 25 gold
- **Architect's Quill** (weapon: 4 dmg, 15% **Silenced**)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Architect falls. The Architect falls and the tomb is QUIET. The Architect falls and the tomb is quiet and the blueprints are STILL. The Architect falls and the tomb is quiet and the blueprints are still and the Architect is... the Architect is AT PEACE. The Architect is at peace and the Architect is FREE. The Architect is at peace and the Architect is free and the Architect can REST. The Architect can rest and the Architect can FINALLY rest. The Architect can rest and the Architect can finally rest and the Architect is DONE."*
