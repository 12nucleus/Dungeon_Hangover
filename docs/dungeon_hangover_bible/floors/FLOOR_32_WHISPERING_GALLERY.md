# FLOOR 32 — THE WHISPERING GALLERY

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Echoes of your choices. Twisted. Regrets. The things you said. The things you didn't say.
**Mood:** Unsettling. The echoes know what you did. The echoes JUDGE.
**Ambient:** Whispers. Echoes. Your own voice (twisted).
**Lighting:** Dim. Shifting. Shadows that move wrong.
**Soundtrack:** Whispers. Echoes. Your own voice (saying things you never said).

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY
**Type:** Hub
**Contents:** Bonfire. A sign ("BEWARE: ECHOES LIE"). A whisper mirror (hear your own voice).
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A gallery. A WHISPERING gallery. The gallery is QUIET. The gallery is quiet and the gallery is LOUD. The gallery is quiet and the gallery is loud and the gallery is FULL of whispers. The gallery is quiet and the gallery is loud and the gallery is full of whispers and the whispers are YOURS."*

---

### ROOM 2 — WHISPER HALL
**Type:** Hazard + Lore
**Contents:**
- Whispering walls (hear your choices. twisted.)
- A whisper trap (listen too long: **Confused** 2 turns)
- A whisper jar (can be used to capture a whisper)
- A whisper map (shows the floor — but it's WRONG)
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"Whispers. WHISPERS everywhere. The whispers are YOURS. The whispers are your choices. The whispers are your choices and the choices are TWISTED. The whispers are your choices and the choices are twisted and the choices are WRONG. The whispers are your choices and the choices are twisted and the choices are wrong and the choices are JUDGING you."*

---

### ROOM 3 — ECHO CHAMBER
**Type:** Boss arena
**Contents:**
- The Echo of You (Boss — see Boss section)
- 4× Echo Chambers (each plays a different echo)
- A silence zone (center — no whispers here)
- A memory mirror (see your past)
**Enemies:** The Echo of You.
**Narrator on entry:** *"The Echo. The ECHO of you. The echo of you and the echo is ANGRY. The echo of you and the echo is angry and the echo is SAD. The echo of you and the echo is angry and the echo is sad and the echo is LONELY. The echo of you and the echo is angry and the echo is sad and the echo is lonely and the echo is YOU."*

---

### ROOM 4 — MEMORY ROOM
**Type:** Lore + Exit
**Contents:**
- 4× Memory mirrors (see your past choices)
- A memory book (read: your story so far)
- A memory chest (contains items you lost)
- The exit (staircase to Floor 31)
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"Memories. MEMORY mirrors. Mirrors that show the PAST. Mirrors that show the things you DID. Mirrors that show the things you didn't DO. Mirrors that show the things you REGRET."*

## ENCOUNTERS

### WHISPER TRAP (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Listening too long.
**Description:** *"You listen. You listen to the WHISPERS. You listen to the whispers and the whispers are SEDUCTIVE. You listen to the whispers and the whispers are seductive and the whispers are LIES."*
**Effect:** **Confused** 2 turns.
**Resolution:** Stop listening or endure.

### ECHO AMBUSH (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Entering the echo chamber.
**Description:** *"The echo ATTACKS. The echo attacks and the echo is YOU. The echo attacks and the echo is you and the echo knows your MOVES."*
**Enemies:** The Echo of You.
**Resolution:** Fight or apologize.

### MEMORY HALLUCINATION (Room 4)
**Trigger:** Looking in a mirror.
**Description:** *"You see yourself. You see yourself in the TAVERN. You see yourself with the CHANDELIER. You see yourself PROPOSING."*
**Effect:** **Hallucinating** 1 turn.
**Resolution:** Accept or look away.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE GALLERY
**Objective:** Navigate the gallery. Reach Floor 31.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Defeat Echo → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: LISTEN TO THE ECHOES
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Hear what you said to NPCs. They're distorted.
**Reward:** Lore. Understanding. Can apologize (dialogue).

### SIDE QUEST 2: SCREAM BACK
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Scream at the echoes. They scream back.
**Reward:** **Deafened** (+10% resistance to charm/fear).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE WHISPERING VOICE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** A specific whisper tells you about Floor 27's Lodestone shard.
**Reward:** Lore. Floor 27 clue.

## BOSS: THE ECHO OF YOU (Room 3)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Same as you

**Abilities:**
- **Echo Strike:** Copies your last attack.
- **Whisper:** 2 dmg + **Confused** 1 turn.
- **Regret:** Target sees their worst memory. **Frightened** 2 turns.
- **Swallowed Words:** All the things you didn't say. 3 dmg + **Silenced** 2 turns.
- **Mirror Image:** Creates an echo copy (50% HP, 50% dmg).

**Drops:**
- **Echo Shard** (weapon: 5 dmg, 25% copy enemy skill)
- **Whisper Jar** (consumable: capture a whisper, use later)
- 20 gold
- **Memory Mirror** (consumable: see enemy's next 3 actions)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Echo falls. The Echo falls and the whispers are QUIET. The Echo falls and the whispers are quiet and the gallery is STILL. The Echo falls and the whispers are quiet and the gallery is still and you are... you are ALONE. You are alone and the whispers are GONE. You are alone and the whispers are gone and you can finally THINK."*

- **Teaches:** Self-reflection. Consequence. Regret.
- **Secrets:** The Whispering Voice (Floor 27 clue). The Memory Chest.
- **Narrator:** "The whispers are YOURS, Greg. The whispers are YOUR choices. The whispers are your choices and the choices are TWISTED. The whispers are your choices and the choices are twisted and the choices are JUDGING you. The whispers are your choices and the choices are twisted and the choices are judging you and the choices are RIGHT. The choices are right and you should FEEL bad. The choices are right and you should feel bad and you DO feel bad. You feel bad and you are SORRY. You feel bad and you are sorry and you can APOLOGIZE. You feel bad and you are sorry and you can apologize and the Echo will FORGIVE you."
