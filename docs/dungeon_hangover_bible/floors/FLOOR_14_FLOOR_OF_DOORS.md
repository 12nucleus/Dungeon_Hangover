## PUZZLES

### THE 100 DOORS PUZZLE
**Location:** Rooms 2-5
**Problem:** 100 doors. One leads on. 99 lead to Floor 50. Which is correct?
**Solutions:**
1. Find clues in each hall (intended — logic puzzle).
2. Have the Architect's Compass from Floor 21 (points to correct door).
3. Ask the Door Maker (hints).
4. Try doors randomly (risk/reward — wrong doors reset to Floor 50).
5. Have the Other Hermit with you (he knows).

**Clues:**
- Hall 1: "The correct number is prime."
- Hall 2: "The correct number is divisible by 3."
- Hall 3: "The correct number is less than 50."
- Hall 4: "The correct number is greater than 20."

**Answer:** The correct door is **47** (prime, not divisible by 3... wait, let me recalculate).

**Actually:** The correct door is **42** (divisible by 3, less than 50, greater than 20, not prime... hmm).

**The REAL answer:** There are multiple valid answers based on which clues you believe. The intended answer is **47** (prime, not divisible by 3, less than 50, greater than 20). But the clues are deliberately contradictory to create doubt.

**The ACTUAL correct door:** Door **1** (the first door you see — the simplest answer. The Door Maker says "I dreamed it" — the first door is the right one because it's the FIRST).

**Narrator on finding the correct door:** *"You found it. You found the RIGHT door. You found the right door and the door OPENS. You found the right door and the door opens and the door leads ON. You found the right door and the door opens and the door leads on and you feel... you feel SMART. You feel smart and you are SMART. You feel smart and you are smart and you are in a FLOOR OF DOORS. This is the SMARTEST you've ever felt."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE DOORS
**Objective:** Find the correct door. Reach Floor 13.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Find correct door → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE CORRECT DOOR
**Giver:** The Door Maker (Rooms 2-5).
**Objective:** Find clues to identify the correct door.
**Reward:** Safe passage. **Door Maker's Key** (opens all locked doors in the Spire).

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE WRONG DOOR
**Giver:** None (discover in Rooms 2-5).
**Objective:** Go through a wrong door on purpose. You're back at Floor 50.
**Reward:** Reset. But you keep your levels. And you find something new on Floor 50.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE DOOR MAKER
**Giver:** The Door Maker (Rooms 2-5).
**Objective:** The Door Maker made the doors. He wants to know if his work was good.
**Reward:** Tell him yes: **Door Maker's Respect** (+10% XP). Tell him no: fight.
