# FLOOR 41 — GATES OF THE HOLLOW MARKET

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** The gates. The first signs of goblin civilization. Commerce begins.
**Mood:** Suspicious. Bustling. A parody of capitalism. Everything has a price.
**Ambient:** Haggling. Coins clinking. Goblin chatter. A vendor calling out.
**Lighting:** Warm. Lanterns. Torches. The first COLOR you've seen in floors.
**Soundtrack:** Market sounds. Coins. Haggling. A goblin jingle (advertising).

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY GATE
**Dimensions:** 5m × 5m. A gate. Goblin guards. A sign.
**Type:** Hub / First shop
**Contents:**
- A bonfire (lit — goblins maintain it)
- A gate (locked — requires toll or fight)
- A sign: "WELCOME TO THE HOLLOW MARKET — ENTRY: 1 GOLD OR 1 BLOOD"

**Enemies:** None (unless you refuse toll).

## PUZZLES

### THE TOLL PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 1
**Problem:** The goblin demands 1 gold or 1 blood. How do you pass?
**Solutions:**
1. Pay 1 gold (intended — easy).
2. Give blood (1 HP — alternative).
3. Fight the guard (hard).
4. Charm the guard (social).
5. Sneak past (stealth).
6. Show the Gatekeeper's Key from Floor 47 (free passage).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You paid the toll. You paid 1 gold. You paid 1 gold to a GOBLIN. You paid 1 gold to a goblin and you feel... you feel CHEAP. You feel cheap and you are CHEAP. You are cheap and you are in a GOBLIN MARKET. This is the CHEAPEST you've ever felt."*

### THE MAP PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 3
**Problem:** The map maker sells maps. But which map do you need?
**Solutions:**
1. Buy the Market Map (10 gold — reveals Floor 40's secrets).
2. Buy the Spire Map (50 gold — incomplete, but shows the full structure).
3. Steal a map (risky).
4. Have the Goblin Map from Floor 50 (map maker is impressed, gives discount).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You bought a map. You bought a map of the MARKET. You bought a map of the market you're STANDING IN. This is either HELPFUL or INSULTING. This is BOTH."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE GATES
**Objective:** Pass through the gates. Reach Floor 40.
**Steps:**
1. Enter the gate (Room 1).
2. Pay toll or bypass.
3. Navigate the market (Rooms 2-5).
4. Find the exit (Room 5, through the market).
**Reward:** Access to Floor 40. Experience. Loot. Shop access.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE TOLL
**Giver:** The Toll Collector (Room 4).
**Objective:** Pay the toll. Or don't.
**Steps:**
1. Approach the gate.
2. Decide: pay, fight, charm, or sneak.
**Reward:** Passage. Or blood. Or fingers.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE MAP MAKER
**Giver:** The Map Maker (Room 3).
**Objective:** Buy a map. Or steal one.
**Steps:**
1. Talk to the Map Maker.
2. Buy a map (10 gold) or steal one.
**Reward:** Map (reveals secrets) or gold (if you steal) or guilt.

### SIDE QUEST 3: FIRST IMPRESSION
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 5).
**Objective:** A goblin child asks if you're a hero.
**Steps:**
1. Talk to the goblin child in Room 5.
2. Say yes or no.
**Reward:** Yes: you get a flower. No: you get a rock. Both are useless but the narrator comments.

## DESIGN NOTES

### DIFFICULTY TUNING
- **Floor 41 is EASY.** It's a transition floor. The Gatekeeper is weak. The market is safe.
- **Expected deaths:** 0.

### TEACHING MOMENTS
- **Room 1:** Teaches toll mechanics (pay or fight).
- **Room 2:** Teaches social engagement (dice game).
- **Room 3:** Teaches shop mechanics (map maker).
- **Room 4:** Teaches commerce (toll collector).
- **Room 5:** Teaches market navigation (vendors, stalls).

### SECRETS
- **The Toll Collector's Ledger:** In Room 4. Lore.
- **The Performance Area:** In Room 5. Earn gold.
- **The Fountain:** In Room 5. Small buff.
- **The Notice Board:** In Room 5. Side quests.

### NARRATOR TONE
- **Floor 41 is SUSPICIOUS.** The narrator is... SUSPICIOUS? The narrator doesn't trust the market. The narrator doesn't trust the GOBLINS.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You're in a MARKET, Greg. You're in a GOBLIN market. You're in a goblin market and you have NO money. You have no money and the goblins have EVERYTHING. This is the worst feeling in the world. And you once proposed to a CHANDELIER."
- **The narrator LOVES the Map Maker.** "The map maker is INSANE. The map maker is insane and the map maker is RIGHT. The map maker is insane and the map maker is right and the map maker is the most HONEST person you've met in FIFTY FLOORS."
