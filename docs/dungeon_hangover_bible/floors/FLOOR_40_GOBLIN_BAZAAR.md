# FLOOR 40 — THE GOBLIN BAZAAR

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** The goblin market proper. A parody of commerce. Everything is for sale.
**Mood:** Chaotic. Colorful. Absurd. The goblins have built a civilization.
**Ambient:** Haggling. Coins. Music (bad music). Goblin arguments.
**Lighting:** Bright. Colorful lanterns. The most LIGHT you've seen.
**Soundtrack:** Market chaos. A goblin band (terrible). Coins clinking.

**Trigger:** Standing in the crowd.
**Description:** *"Something touches your pocket. Something SMALL. Something GOBLIN."*
**Effect:** Lose 1d10 gold.
**Resolution:** Catch them (Perception) or lose gold.

## PUZZLES

### THE AUCTION PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 6
**Problem:** A rare item is being auctioned. You want it. You have no money.
**Solutions:**
1. Bid (you'll lose — you have no money).
2. Steal during the auction (Dexterity check).
3. Win a contest in Room 1 (earn gold, then bid).
4. Have Bleddy (saved goblin from Floor 46) — he gives you a loan.
**Narrator on solving:** *"You won the auction. You won the auction with MONEY. You won the auction with money you EARNED. You won the auction with money you earned and you feel... you feel PROUD. ## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE BAZAAR
**Objective:** Navigate the bazaar. Reach Floor 39.
**Steps:**
1. Enter the bazaar (Room 1).
2. Navigate the shops (Rooms 2-7).
3. Find the exit (Room 6, through the auction house).
**Reward:** Access to Floor 39. Experience. Loot. Shop access.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE DROWNING GOBLIN'S DEBT
**Giver:** Bleddy (Room 1, if saved on Floor 46).
**Objective:** Collect the debt.
**Steps:**
1. Find Bleddy in Room 1.
2. Decide: collect, forgive, or refuse.
**Reward:** 20 gold OR discounts OR lucky charm.

### SIDE QUEST 2: COUNTERFEIT GOODS
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 5).
**Objective:** A goblin sells "legendary" items that are junk.
**Steps:**
1. Buy a "legendary" item from Room 5.
2. It's junk.
3. Confront the goblin OR expose him.
**Reward:** Exposed: real legendary item. Confronted: refund + bonus.

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE AUCTION
**Giver:** The Auctioneer (Room 6).
**Objective:** Win the auction.
**Steps:**
1. Earn gold (contest, performance, fishing).
2. Bid at the auction.
3. Win or lose.
**Reward:** Rare item (if win) or experience (if lose).

**Damage:** 5
**Armor:** 3 (from gold armor)
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Gold Throw:** 5 dmg (throws gold coins).

- **Summon Guards:** Summons 2× Goblin Guards.

### DIFFICULTY TUNING

- **Expected deaths:** 0.

- **Room 1:** Teaches market navigation.

- **Room 3:** Teaches food mechanics.
- **Room 4:** Teaches potion mechanics.
- **Room 5:** Teaches weapon shopping.
- **Room 6:** Teaches auction mechanics.
- **Room 7:** Teaches information broker mechanics.

### SECRETS
- **The Auction Ledger:** In Room 6. Lore.
- **The Rare Potion:** In Room 4. Powerful buff.
- **The Performance Stage:** In Room 1. Earn gold.
- **The Fountain:** In Room 1. Gold farming.

- **Floor 40 is CHAOTIC.** The narrator is... OVERWHELMED? The narrator doesn't understand the market.
- **But the narrator still judges.** "You're in a BAZAAR, Greg. You're in a GOBLIN bazaar. You're in a goblin bazaar and you have NO money. You have no money and the goblins have EVERYTHING. This is the worst feeling in the world. And you once proposed to a CHANDELIER."
