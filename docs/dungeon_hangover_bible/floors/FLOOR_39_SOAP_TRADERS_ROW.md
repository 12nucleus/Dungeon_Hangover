# FLOOR 39 — SOAP TRADER'S ROW

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Soap economy. Soap currency. Soap everything. The goblin economy runs on soap.
**Mood:** Absurd. Slippery. Everything is soap. Everything COSTS soap. Everything IS soap.
**Ambient:** Sliding. Squelching. Goblin merchants haggling over soap.
**Lighting:** Colorful. Soap bubbles refract light. Rainbows everywhere.
**Soundtrack:** Sliding sounds. Popping bubbles. Goblin commerce.

## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY
**Type:** Hub
**Contents:** Bonfire. Welcome sign ("SOAP IS KING"). A soap fountain (slippery — 50% fall chance).
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"Soap. SOAP. The goblin economy runs on SOAP. The goblin economy runs on soap and you need SOAP. You need soap and you have NO soap. You need soap and you have no soap and this is the worst feeling in the world."*

### ROOM 2 — SOAP MARKET
**Type:** Shop
**Contents:**
- 4× Soap vendors (selling soap, buying soap, trading soap)
- Soap Potions (10 gold — **Slippery** area)

**Narrator on entry:** *"Soap. The goblins are selling SOAP. The goblins are selling soap and buying SOAP. The goblins are selling soap and buying soap and trading SOAP. The goblins are selling soap and buying soap and trading soap and this is ECONOMICS. This is economics and it's ABSURD."*

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH SOAP ROW
**Objective:** Navigate Soap Row. Reach Floor 38.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Defeat Oracle → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE SOAPMAKER'S SECRET
**Giver:** The Soap Maker (Room 3).
**Objective:** Learn the soap recipe.
**Reward:** Can craft soap. **Soap Chunk** ×3.

### SIDE QUEST 2: SOAP HEIST
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Steal a bar of Premium Soap.
**Reward:** **Premium Soap** (trade for best items).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE SMUGGLER
**Giver:** A goblin smuggler (Room 2).
**Objective:** Trade soap for information about the surface.
**Reward:** Lore. +5% XP.

## BOSS: THE SOAP ORACLE (Room 4)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 4 (soap crust)
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Soap Splash:** 3 dmg + **Slippery** (50% fall) 2 turns.
- **Bubble Shield:** Absorbs first 5 damage.
- **Cleanse:** Removes all your buffs.
- **Lather:** All in 2D radius: **Slippery**.

**Drops:**
- **Soap Orb** (weapon: 4 dmg, 25% **Slippery**)
- **Premium Soap** (trade item)
- 25 gold

**Narrator on death:** *"The Soap Oracle dissolves. The soap dissolves. The soap is GONE. The soap is gone and the floor is WET. The soap is gone and the floor is wet and you're SLIPPERY. The soap is gone and the floor is wet and you're slippery and you FALL."*
