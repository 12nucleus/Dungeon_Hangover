# FLOOR 17 — THE GARDEN OF STONE

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** Petrified forest. Beautiful. Dead. A ghost gardener tends stone plants.
**Mood:** Melancholy. Beautiful. The gardener tends the stone. The gardener has been tending for 300 years.
**Ambient:** Wind through stone. The creak of petrified wood. A ghostly hum.
**Lighting:** Pale. Moonlight. The stone glows faintly.
**Soundtrack:** Wind. Stone. A ghostly hum.

## ENCOUNTERS

### PETRIFIED TREE (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Touching a tree.
**Description:** *"The tree is STONE. The tree is stone and the tree is COLD. The tree is stone and the tree is cold and the tree is DEAD. The tree is stone and the tree is cold and the tree is dead and the tree is BEAUTIFUL."*
**Effect:** Lore. The tree was alive. The tree is stone now.
**Resolution:** Accept or deny.

### GARDENER'S TALE (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Talking to the gardener.
**Description:** *"The gardener tells you. The gardener tells you about the GARDEN. The gardener tells you about the garden and the garden was ALIVE. The gardener tells you about the garden and the garden was alive and the garden is STONE. The gardener tells you about the garden and the garden was alive and the garden is stone and the gardener is SAD."*
**Effect:** Lore. The garden was alive. The Spire came. The garden turned to stone.
**Resolution:** Listen or ignore.

### STONE FRUIT (Room 4)
**Trigger:** Eating stone fruit.
**Description:** *"You eat the stone fruit. You eat the stone fruit and the stone fruit is... the stone fruit is STONE. You eat the stone fruit and the stone fruit is stone and the stone fruit is HARD. You eat the stone fruit and the stone fruit is stone and the stone fruit is hard and the stone fruit is... the stone fruit is GOOD."*
**Effect:** 2 dmg + **Petrified Stomach** (+10% poison resistance, permanent).
**Resolution:** Eat or don't.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE GARDEN
**Objective:** Navigate the garden. Reach Floor 16.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Talk to Gardener → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE PETRIFIED TREE
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** A tree that was once alive. Touch it: you see its last moment.
**Reward:** Lore. **Stone Wisdom** (+10% nature resistance).

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE STONE FRUIT
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Eat a stone fruit. It's... stone.
**Reward:** **Petrified Stomach** (+10% poison resistance, permanent).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE GARDENER
**Giver:** The Gardener (Room 3).
**Objective:** The gardener tells you his story. He died because he wouldn't leave his garden.
**Reward:** Lore. **Stone Seed** (plant it at Floor 1 for Ending C).

## BOSS: THE GARDENER (Room 3, if you destroy the garden)

**HP:** 20
**Damage:** 3
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Stone Throw:** 3 dmg + **Prone**.
- **Root Grab:** **Bound** 1 turn, 1 dmg/turn.
- **Petal Storm:** All in 2D radius: 2 dmg.
- **Garden's Wrath:** Summons 2× Stone Plants.

**Drops:**
- **Gardener's Gloves** (passive: +10% nature damage)
- **Stone Seed** (plant at Floor 1 for Ending C)
- 15 gold
- **Petrified Wood** (crafting: stone items)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Gardener falls. The Gardener falls and the garden is QUIET. The Gardener falls and the garden is quiet and the stone is STILL. The Gardener falls and the garden is quiet and the stone is still and the Gardener is... the Gardener is AT PEACE. The Gardener is at peace and the Gardener is FREE. The Gardener is at peace and the Gardener is free and the Gardener can REST."*
