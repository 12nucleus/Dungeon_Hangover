## ROOM BREAKDOWNS

### ROOM 1 — ENTRY
**Type:** Hub
**Contents:** Bonfire. A sign ("ROLL WISELY"). A goblin bouncer (demands 5 gold entry fee).
**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A dice parlour. A GOBLIN dice parlour. The goblins are GAMBLING. The goblins are gambling with GOLD. The goblins are gambling with gold and LEVELS. The goblins are gambling with gold and levels and this is the most TERRIFYING thing you've ever seen."*

---

### ROOM 2 — GAMBLING HALL
**Type:** Social + Risk
**Contents:**
- 4× Gambling tables (dice, cards, wheel)
- A goblin dealer at each table
- A prize board (win big: gold, items, LEVELS)
- A warning sign ("HOUSE ALWAYS WINS — EXCEPT WHEN IT DOESN'T")

**Narrator on entry:** *"Tables. GAMBLING tables. The goblins are PLAYING. The goblins are playing with DICE. The goblins are playing with dice and the dice are LOADED. The goblins are playing with dice and the dice are loaded and the goblins are cheating and they're PROUD of it."*

### ROOM 3 — CHEAT ROOM
**Type:** Secret + Loot
**Contents:**

- Loaded dice (can be taken: +25% win chance)

**Enemies:** None.
**Narrator on entry:** *"A hidden room. A CHEAT room. The goblins have been CHEATING. The goblins have been cheating for THREE HUNDRED YEARS. The goblins have been cheating for three hundred years and they're STILL cheating. The goblins have been cheating for three hundred years and they're still cheating and they're PROUD of it."*

### ROOM 4 — DICE DEMON
**Type:** Boss arena
**Contents:**

## ENCOUNTERS

### DICE CHALLENGE (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Sitting at a table.
**Description:** *"The goblin challenges you. The goblin challenges you to DICE. The goblin challenges you to dice and the stakes are GOLD."*
**Effect:** Gamble gold. Win or lose.
**Resolution:** Play or refuse.

### LEVEL BET (Room 5)
**Trigger:** Sitting at high-stakes table.
**Description:** *"The goblin wants to bet LEVELS. The goblin wants to bet YOUR level. The goblin wants to bet your level and the prize is TWO levels."*
**Effect:** Bet a level. Win: +2 levels. Lose: –1 level.
**Resolution:** Play or refuse.

### CHEATER ACCUSATION (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Winning too much.
**Description:** *"The goblin accuses you. The goblin accuses you of CHEATING. The goblin accuses you of cheating and the goblin is ANGRY."*
**Effect:** Fight or prove innocence.
**Resolution:** Fight, flee, or prove (show loaded dice).

## PUZZLES

### THE DICE DEMON PUZZLE
**Location:** Room 4
**Problem:** The Dice Demon is made of dice. How do you beat him?
**Solutions:**
1. Fight him directly (he's tough).
2. Use the Loaded Dice from Room 3 (he's weakened).
3. Roll the giant die (random: might help, might hurt).
4. Have the Loaded Dice from the side quest (he respects you — peaceful).
**Narrator on solving:** *"You beat the Dice Demon. You beat the Dice Demon with DICE. You beat the Dice Demon with dice and you feel... you feel LUCKY. You feel lucky and you are LUCKY. You are lucky and you are in a GOBLIN DICE PARLOUR. This is the LUCKIEST you've ever felt."*

## BOSS: THE DICE DEMON (Room 4)

**HP:** 35
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Dice Roll:** Random effect (1-2: 6 dmg. 3-4: heals 5 HP. 5-6: summons 2× Goblin Gamblers).
- **Loaded Die:** 4 dmg + **Cursed** (bad luck).
- **Snake Eyes:** All in 2D radius: **Frightened**.
- **Double or Nothing:** Steals 10 gold OR deals 5 dmg.

**Drops:**
- **Dice Demon's Crown** (Head: +5% crit chance, +5% luck)
- **Loaded Dice** (consumable: guaranteed win next gamble)
- 30 gold

**Narrator on death:** *"The Dice Demon shatters. The dice shatter. The dice SCATTER. The dice scatter across the floor. The dice scatter across the floor and you PICK them up. You pick them up and they're LOADED. You pick them up and they're loaded and you're a CHEATER. You pick them up and they're loaded and you're a cheat and you're PROUD."*
