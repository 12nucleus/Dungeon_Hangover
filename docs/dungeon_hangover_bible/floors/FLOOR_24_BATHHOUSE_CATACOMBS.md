# FLOOR 24 — THE BATHHOUSE CATACOMBS

## ENCOUNTERS

### STEAM VENT (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Stepping on a vent.
**Description:** *"Steam. STEAM. The steam is HOT. The steam is hot and the steam is BURNING. The steam is hot and the steam is burning and the steam is on YOUR FACE."*
**Effect:** 2 dmg + **Burning** (1/turn, 2 turns).
**Resolution:** Dodge or endure.

### BATHHOUSE AMBUSH (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Entering the bath.
**Description:** *"The dead RISE. The dead rise from the BATH. The dead rise from the bath and the dead are WET. The dead rise from the bath and the dead are wet and the dead are ANGRY. The dead rise from the bath and the dead are wet and the dead are angry because you DISTURBED their bath."*
**Enemies:** 4× Bathhouse Dead.
**Resolution:** Fight or flee.

### SOAP OFFERING (Room 3)
**Trigger:** Having soap.
**Description:** *"You have SOAP. You have soap and the dead WANT soap. You have soap and the dead want soap and the dead are GRATEFUL."*
**Effect:** Share soap. Dead are friendly. Give you **Bathhouse Key**.
**Resolution:** Share or don't.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: THROUGH THE CATACOMBS
**Objective:** Navigate the catacombs. Reach Floor 23.
**Steps:** Enter → Navigate → Deal with Dead → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE BATHHOUSE GHOST
**Giver:** A bathhouse ghost (Room 2).
**Objective:** A ghost misses his bath. Find his soap.
**Reward:** **Bathhouse Key** (opens treasure room). 15 gold.

### SIDE QUEST 2: THE HOT SPRING
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 4).
**Objective:** Rest in the hot spring.
**Reward:** Full heal. **Relaxed** (–10% damage, 2 fights).

### SIDE QUEST 3: THE DRAIN
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 3).
**Objective:** Find the drain. It leads to a secret room.
**Reward:** **Drain Wisdom** (+10% water resistance). 20 gold.

## BOSS: THE BATHHOUSE DEAD (Room 3)

**HP:** 35
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Slow

**Abilities:**
- **Soak:** 4 dmg + **Wet** (50% slip chance) 2 turns.
- **Drowning Touch:** Target is **Drowning** (2 dmg/turn until saved).
- **Steam Blast:** All in 2D radius: 3 dmg + **Burning**.
- **Eternal Bath:** Heals 3 HP/turn while in water.
- **Soap Shield:** Absorbs first 5 damage.

**Drops:**
- **Bathhouse Key** (opens treasure room)
- **Soap Bar** (trade item)
- 25 gold
- **Bathrobe** (Body: +5% evasion, +5% water resistance)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Bathhouse Dead falls. The Bathhouse Dead falls and the bath is STILL. The Bathhouse Dead falls and the bath is still and the dead are QUIET. The Bathhouse Dead falls and the bath is still and the dead are quiet and the dead are... the dead are CONTENT. The dead are content and the dead are AT PEACE. The dead are content and the dead are at peace and the dead can REST."*
