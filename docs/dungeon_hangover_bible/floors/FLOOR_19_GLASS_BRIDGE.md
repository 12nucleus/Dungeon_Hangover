# FLOOR 19 — THE GLASS BRIDGE

## FLOOR OVERVIEW

**Theme:** A bridge over nothing. It shows your reflection. It shows your FEARS.
**Mood:** Tense. The bridge is fragile. The bridge shows you yourself.
**Ambient:** Wind. Glass creaking. Your own heartbeat.
**Lighting:** Grey. The bridge is TRANSPARENT. You can see NOTHING below.
**Soundtrack:** Wind. Glass creaking. Your heartbeat.

## ENCOUNTERS

### REFLECTION ATTACK (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Stepping on the bridge.
**Description:** *"Your reflection MOVES. Your reflection moves and your reflection is YOU. Your reflection moves and your reflection is you and your reflection is ANGRY. Your reflection moves and your reflection is you and your reflection is angry and your reflection ATTACKS."*
**Enemies:** The Glass.
**Resolution:** Fight, close your eyes, or break the glass.

### BRIDGE BREAK (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Fighting on the bridge.
**Description:** *"The bridge CRACKS. The bridge cracks and the bridge is BREAKING. The bridge cracks and the bridge is breaking and the bridge is GONE."*
**Effect:** Fall (10 dmg + **Prone**). Land on Floor 18 early.
**Resolution:** Dodge or fall.

### CLOSE YOUR EYES (Room 2)
**Trigger:** Choosing to close your eyes.
**Description:** *"You close your eyes. You close your eyes and you WALK. You close your eyes and you walk and you don't LOOK. You close your eyes and you walk and you don't look and the bridge DOESN'T BREAK. You close your eyes and you walk and you don't look and the bridge doesn't break and you're ACROSS."*
**Effect:** Peaceful resolution. The Glass doesn't break. You pass.
**Resolution:** Walk across blind.

## QUESTS

### MAIN QUEST: CROSS THE BRIDGE
**Objective:** Cross the glass bridge. Reach Floor 18.
**Steps:** Enter → Cross → Deal with Glass → Exit.

### SIDE QUEST 1: THE REFLECTION
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Your reflection moves independently. It knows your build.
**Reward:** Lore. Understanding. **Glass Shard**.

### SIDE QUEST 2: BREAK THE GLASS
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Shatter the bridge. You fall but land on a hidden floor.
**Reward:** Secret area. 30 gold. **Glass Crown**.

### SIDE QUEST 3: WALK CAREFULLY
**Giver:** None (discover in Room 2).
**Objective:** Cross without breaking anything.
**Reward:** **Glass Shard** (+5% critical hit damage). Peaceful resolution.

## BOSS: THE GLASS (Room 2)

**HP:** 30
**Damage:** 4
**Armor:** 2
**Speed:** Same as you

**Abilities:**
- **Reflection Strike:** Copies your last attack.
- **Glass Shard:** 4 dmg + **Bleeding** (1/turn, 3 turns).
- **Mirror Image:** Creates a glass copy (50% HP, 50% dmg).
- **Shatter:** Below 25% HP, the bridge cracks. All on bridge: fall (10 dmg).
- **Perfect Reflection:** Reflects 25% of damage back.

**Drops:**
- **Glass Shard** (passive: +5% critical hit damage)
- **Glass Crown** (Head: +5% all stats, –5% speed)
- 25 gold
- **Mirror Shard** (consumable: see enemy's next 3 actions)

**Narrator on death:** *"The Glass shatters. The Glass shatters and the bridge is GONE. The Glass shatters and the bridge is gone and you're FALLING. The Glass shatters and the bridge is gone and you're falling and you're SCARED. The Glass shatters and the bridge is gone and you're falling and you're scared and you're BRAVE. The Glass shatters and the bridge is gone and you're falling and you're scared and you're brave and you're FALLING. The Glass shatters and the bridge is gone and you're falling and you're scared and you're brave and you're falling and you're GOING TO MAKE IT."*
