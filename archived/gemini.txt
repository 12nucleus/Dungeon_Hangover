# 50 Floors, One Massive Headache, and a Goblin in a Bathtub

Welcome to the worst morning of your life, Greg.

---

## I. Floor 50: The Basement of Poor Choices

*   **Theme:** The Sewer / Foundations. Wet, dark, smells like regret and mildew.
*   **Objective:** Escape the sewer and kill the Goblin King (who is currently bathing).

### Room Breakdown (25 Rooms)

#### The Start (Room 1)
You wake up.
*   **Visual:** A small, damp stone room.
*   **Contents:** Bonfire (Checkpoint), Backpack (Rusty Dagger, Health Potion, Torch).
*   **Interaction:** You must physically light the torch on the bonfire to see the door handle.

#### The Hermit (Room 2)
"Old Man Stink" lives in a crate here.
*   **Quest:** "The Ring Finger." A Boss Rat gnawed off his ring finger (with his wedding ring on it). He wants it back.
*   **Reward:** "Dirty Rags" (Armor +1), Map of the Floor, and the "Backstory" (He explains you fell down a laundry chute 3 days ago).

#### The Gauntlet (Rooms 3-10)
Narrow corridors with "Sewer Slimes" and "Ratmen."
*   **Trap:** Room 6 has a "Slippery Floor" sign. Perception check on one of the floor tile when stepping on it, if fail, you slip, take 2 DMG, and drop your torch (it goes out).

#### The Gatekeeper (Room 15)
A locked heavy iron door. A Goblin Guard speaks through the slot.
*   **Dialogue:** "Boss is scrubbin'. No entry unless you got the bubble-stuff. He needs the lavender kind."
*   **Blocker:** You cannot pass without the Lavender Soap.

#### The Soap Hunt (Rooms 16-24)
*   **Room 20:** Contains a "Gelatinous Cube" that has absorbed a crate of spa supplies. You can kill it, or use a "Salt Shaker" (found in Room 18 kitchen) to dissolve it instantly.
*   **Item:** Bar of Lavender Soap (Quest Item).

#### The Boss Room (Room 25): The Royal Bathhouse
*   **Boss:** King Scrub-a-Dub. A fat Goblin King in a golden clawfoot tub.
*   **Mechanic:** He fights from the tub. He throws rubber ducks (Explosive, 1 turn fuse) and splashes "Hot Water" (Burn DOT).
*   **The Interrupt:** Entering the room catches him naked. He spends the first turn trying to cover up with a towel (Free attack round for you).

*   **Curse:** *Lavender Aura* – You permanently smell like grandma's soap. -5 Stealth, but +2 Charisma with elderly NPCs.

## II. The 15 Archetypes (Sobering Up Choices)

You don't pick a class; you remember skills as you sober up.

*   **Bar Bouncer**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Tank / Control. High HP, throws enemies into other enemies.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: The Heave-Ho (Throw enemy 3 tiles) / Lvl 5: ID Check (Stun target for 1 turn).
*   **Karaoke Bard**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Sonic / Buffs. Powers scale with how bad the "song" is.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Off-Key Shriek (AOE Shatter Dmg) / Lvl 5: Power Ballad (Heal allies, Enrage enemies).
*   **Gutter Rogue**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Dirty Fighting. Bonuses for hitting groin/eyes.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Pocket Sand (Blindness) / Lvl 5: Sucker Punch (Auto-crit if enemy isn't looking).
*   **Sommelier**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Alchemist / Poison. Identifies fluids, buffs potions.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Taste Test (Identify any liquid) / Lvl 5: Vintage Throw (Splash potion effect in AOE).
*   **Kebab Paladin**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Food Buffs / Fire. Heals by eating, burns by burping.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Spicy Sauce (Weapon deals Fire Dmg) / Lvl 5: The Meat Sweats (Aura of heat damage).
*   **Gym Bro**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Grapple / Str. Gains buffs from "flexing" (wasting a turn).
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Do You Even Lift? (Intimidate) / Lvl 5: Protein Shake (Temp +5 STR, -2 INT).
*   **Karen**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Psychic / Debuff. Summons "Managers" (Spectral minions).
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: I Want Your Name (Mark target for extra Dmg) / Lvl 5: Corporate Complaint (Silence enemy caster).
*   **Hipster Barista**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Speed / Heat. Moves fast, burns enemies with hot water.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Espresso Shot (+3 Movement) / Lvl 5: Scalding Pour (High water damage).
*   **DIY Dad**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Crafting / Turrets. Builds traps from junk.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Duct Tape Fix (Heal mechanism) / Lvl 5: Lawnmower Charge (AOE Slash).
*   **Conspiracy Theorist**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Illusion / Dodge. Hard to hit because "they" are watching.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Tinfoil Hat (Immune to Psychic) / Lvl 5: False Flag (Enemy attacks their own ally).
*   **Influencer**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Distraction. Uses camera flash and minions.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Selfie Flash (Cone Blind) / Lvl 5: Collab? (Charm enemy to fight for you).
*   **Designated Driver**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Support / Transport. Carries team, removes debuffs.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Sober Up (Remove Poison/Confuse) / Lvl 5: Uber Rush (Teleport party 5 tiles).
*   **Cat Person**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Agility / Summons. Summons stray cats. Agile.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Laser Pointer (Directs cat swarm) / Lvl 5: Nine Lives (Auto-resurrect once).
*   **Mall Ninja**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Crit / Fail. High damage but high chance to hurt self.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Naruto Run (Dodge up, Defense down) / Lvl 5: While You Studied The Blade (Big Slash).
*   **Tab Tabulator**
    *   *Core Mechanic:* Math Mage. Deals damage based on gold/numbers.
    *   *Sample Skills:* Lvl 1: Split the Bill (Divide Dmg among enemies) / Lvl 5: Compound Interest (DOT that doubles every turn).

### Stacking Rules
Skills stack "logically but stupidly."
*   *Example:* Combine Bar Bouncer (Throw) + Sommelier (Vintage Throw) = You can throw enemies and they explode into wine on impact.
*   *Example:* Mall Ninja (Naruto Run) + Gym Bro (Protein Shake) = You run fast enough to break walls but take impact damage.

## III. The 50-Floor Blueprint & Side Quests

### Act 1: The Foundations (Floors 50-41)
*   **Theme:** Sewers, Basements, Maintenance Tunnels.
*   **Enemies:** Rats, Slimes, Hobo-Goblins, Giant Roaches.
*   **Side Quest (Floor 45) - "The Clogged Pipe":** A depressed Ooze is blocking a pipe. You can kill it or find it a "friend" (another ooze) to leave with.

### Act 2: The Service Quarters (Floors 40-31)
*   **Theme:** Kitchens, Laundries, Staff Barracks.
*   **Enemies:** Kobold Janitors, Animated Brooms, Enraged Chefs.
*   **Side Quest (Floor 33) - "Hell's Kitchen":** A demon chef needs "Imp Eggs" for an omelet. If you deliver them, he gives you the *Frying Pan of Doom*.

### Act 3: The Dungeon Proper (Floors 30-21)
*   **Theme:** Classic Torture Chambers, Cells, Guard Posts.
*   **Enemies:** Skeletons (bored ones), Orc Guards, "Bureaucrat Beholders."
*   **Side Quest (Floor 28) - "The Skeleton War":** Two piles of bones are arguing over who started it. You must reassemble them (incorrectly is funnier) to stop the argument.

### Act 4: The VIP Lounge (Floors 20-11)
*   **Theme:** Succubus Clubs, Vampire Bars, Casinos. High stakes, neon lights.
*   **Enemies:** Bouncers, Succubi, Drunk Vampires, Mimic Slot Machines.
*   **Side Quest (Floor 15) - "The Tab":** A vampire insists you owe him $50,000. You can fight him or win it back in a game of Blackjack (Minigame).

### Act 5: The Corporate Offices (Floors 10-1)
*   **Theme:** Cubicles, Server Rooms, The Penthouse.
*   **Enemies:** Mind Flayer Middle Managers, Golem Security, The HR Dept.
*   **Side Quest (Floor 5) - "The Printer":** The printer is jammed with a soul. Fix it (Technician check) or Smash it (Strength check).

### The Ending (Floor 1)
You reach the Penthouse. The "Final Boss" is a mirror.
*   **Twist:** You aren't a prisoner. You are the Dungeon Lord. You got drunk, locked yourself in the basement, and set the security system to "Lethal" as a prank on yourself.
*   **Choices:**
    *   *Sober Up:* Sign the paperwork on the desk. Game ends, you go back to work managing the dungeon (New Game+ Mode: You manage the dungeon vs heroes).
    *   *Hair of the Dog:* Drink the "Reserve Whiskey" on the desk. You pass out and wake up on Floor 50 again (Prestige Mode: Keep stats, enemies harder).

## IV. Next Steps: Companion System

To build the "Temporary Companion" system mentioned in your pillars:

*   **Floor 48:** Recruit "Stabby Steve" (A goblin who hates his boss).
    *   *Mechanic:* He has a "Loyalty Meter." If you don't let him stab things, he leaves.
*   **Exit:** On Floor 40, he finds a better paying job with a Kobold Union and leaves the party, giving you a Union Card (Discount at vendors).
